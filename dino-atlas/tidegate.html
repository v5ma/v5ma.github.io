FIELDCRAFT / CONTROLS

Learn how this place works.

The orange crane marks your outpost. The amber mast marks the pump house. The upper lookout lets you watch the herd before committing. The front door, service aisle and roof stairs are real connected approaches. A narrow north maintenance doorway follows the sluice pipe straight into the machine aisle. It admits a ranger, not a jeep. A field cabinet beside it restocks tools with A, without opening a menu.

The herd moves between the bridge apron and the eastern forage yard. Stocking the shelter feeder creates an opportunity, but does not magically clear the bridge. Water and the zapper are non-injurious ranger tools. Watch the animals settle after a disturbance.

The sluice exposes a maintenance crossing. Move an occupied boat out of the central lock before draining. Move the ranger off that path before restoring the water. The lower harbor stays available. The bridge signal reports the actual gearbox, herd and boat state; it does not predict animals you have not watched.

Familiar Xbox preset: left stick moves or steers, right stick looks and aims, A interacts, X reloads, Y boards or exits. RT fires on foot; in a vehicle, hold LB and RT to fire without accelerating. RT/LT drive/reverse or rise/descend. RB cycles tools; D-pad left selects water, right selects zapper, up jumps and down sounds the horn. Menu pauses, View opens the map, B closes.

Legacy Quest preset: left stick moves or steers. Right stick snap-turns first-person VR or rotates the diorama, not your physical head. Right A interacts, right B opens or closes menus, left X reloads and left Y boards or exits. Right trigger fires on foot; left grip aims tools in a vehicle. Right grip jumps; right stick click cycles tools. Point and trigger or hand-ray pinch selects the same in-world controls. Hands have explicit hold-to-move and hold-to-fire tiles; releasing clears the action.

Active Quest and alternate Xbox layouts are shown in Settings / Travel controls. Either grip interacts in Active Quest; LT aims and RT fires. Click the left stick for vehicle express mode. Press T on the keyboard to toggle unlimited-duration express speed.

Keyboard: WASD moves, E interacts, F boards, R reloads, Q cycles tools, 1 selects water, 2 selects zapper, H sounds the horn, Space jumps on foot, Shift boosts, Z/X raise/lower the helicopter, M opens the map and Esc pauses. Drag the camera to look, use the right mouse button to aim, and the left to fire.

Diorama top and front are presentation surfaces. Top open, front open, or both open are the only states. Buildings still have real walls and tool collision; moving your head does not move or teleport the ranger. The roof cutaway follows the ranger's interior occupancy and does not remove collision.
