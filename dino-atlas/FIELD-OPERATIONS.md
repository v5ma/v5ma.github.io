# Mounted utilities and field assignments

Candidate component ranger-field-ops-20260917.1. This extends both existing public games, including the unmerged Express candidate c6f0991604a564b3d21eb29c68b2237c11a8c3fc. It does not remove that speed, input, navigation or character-centered portal work. The intended combined release is dino-field-operations-20260917.1, only after exact-source and separate public verification.

## Experience and scope

Twelve original assignments, six in Classic Reserve and six in Tidegate, reuse recognizable working places. The player chooses a vehicle and approach, inspects the situation, applies an appropriate mounted tool, recovers from interrupted work, and returns to file a unique field report. These are authored missions, not a claim of infinitely generated content or a new map.

Classic assignments are Gatehouse Cooling Run, Redwood Induction Service, Canopy Census, Wetland Researcher Recovery, Visitor Valley Supply Loop, and Forest Stand-off. Tidegate assignments are Outpost Cooling Circuit, Pump-house Roof Rescue, East Landing Evacuation, Quiet Apron Watch, Station Regulator Delivery, and Maintenance Android Recovery.

The same stable mission catalog drives the board, targets, guidance, completion and save allowlist. Observation, cooling, isolated pulse resets, wildlife deterrence/settling, actual herd-corridor clearance, cargo loading/installation, crew recovery/delivery and grounded reporting are distinct conditions. The original missions and their rewards remain separate. The full map and current compass follow the selected assignment rather than introducing another competing arrow.

## Mounted utilities

Every existing jeep, buggy, patrol boat and helicopter receives original modeled water and pulse barrels, tanks, a survey head, a recovery spool and a secured-cargo basket. Classic has all four vehicle types; Tidegate retains its original three rather than inventing a buggy there. Each profile has its own mount position, utility names and survey/recovery reach. The jeep is a road response platform, the buggy supports close induction work, the boat supports shoreline survey and basket recovery, and the helicopter supports elevated inspection and hoisting.

Water and electric pulse use the existing fictional non-injurious tools, magazines, reserves, reloads and wildlife reactions. They are not separate free ammunition pools. Scanner and recovery attachments have no ammunition but require a clear view, correct vehicle, permitted range, sufficiently low physical speed, and uninterrupted dwell. Vertical speed also matters while hovering. Pausing, aim loss or an unsuitable target cancels partial dwell, not completed evidence.

Shots originate at modeled vehicle equipment, including first-person XR. A tracked pointer chooses aim, not an alternative firing position through walls. The original world raycast limits every field target. A muzzle cannot protrude through a wall and bypass it. Diorama aiming retains the same physical world and mounted origin.

Human crew are friendly assessment/recovery subjects, not shock targets. Water and pulse fire is intercepted with crew-protection feedback and cannot complete their rescue. The fictional maintenance android accepts controlled induction pulses, followed by an on-foot confirmation. This is game behavior, not a biological, medical or electrical claim.

## Place, routes and recovery

Physical: the existing roads, aircraft bays, harbor landings, station roof terrace, narrow service aisle and observation points retain their geometry and collision. New equipment is nonblocking, so historical walking checkpoints are not occupied by new solids. The first placement test found a cooling unit inside a gate pillar; the new unit was moved beside the pillar rather than removing collision.

Conditional: the right vehicle and mounted tool matter. A roof recovery needs a helicopter and a stable approach; a boat extraction needs a usable water route; delivered equipment still requires hands-on installation with its cargo carrier nearby. Neither flight nor express travel remotely completes grounded work.

Behavioral: census and stand-off tasks use actual existing residents. Quiet Apron Watch measures the live herd, not an observation or feeder checkbox. A missed survey can be retried; a disturbed herd can settle and offer another opportunity. Three friendly crew roles and a maintenance android give tools a purpose beyond wildlife alone.

Information: the assignment board names the purpose, supported vehicles, next action and recovery. The existing compass/map tracks the selected target or the needed vehicle. Contextual tool status shows hold progress. A destination marker remains a bearing, not a route through walls or across unavailable water.

Embodiment: vehicles carry the visible tools, rays start at the equipment, and existing aim, fire, reload, braking, boarding and portal controls remain. Quest Active uses grips to interact and LT/RT to aim/use tools. Xbox Active cycles all four vehicle attachments with RB; Familiar Xbox/Legacy Quest retain their original two-tool cycle until a new attachment is explicitly selected. Direct Water/Pulse/Scan/Recovery tiles and keyboard 1/2/3/4 are available. N or the menu opens assignments. Hand-ray menus mirror the same buttons. Physical-device approval remains separate.

## Save and reward contracts

Only dino-atlas.field-operations.classic.v1 and dino-atlas.field-operations.tidegate.v1 are new. Historical inventory, remaps, field-guide, ranch, story, market, district and presentation records are not cleared or migrated. A report grants one new service commendation, derived from completed unique IDs, not credits in an older reward ledger. Completed reports cannot be restarted for duplicate awards.

Cargo is tied to the actual carrier ID. A second recovery cannot be started while another assignment has secured cargo. Suspension keeps evidence and cargo; partial holds reset. On reload, Tidegate restores only validated secured-cargo vehicle poses and their occupied state from this new key. This avoids teleporting a rescued crew member back to the default parked boat and skipping the return leg. Other parked vehicles retain Tidegate's existing save behavior. Classic continues to use its established fleet poses. Unknown future versions of the new keys are left untouched.

## Evidence and limits

The local candidate passed 227 model/real-physics tests, including all 198 existing tests. Pure progression fixtures cover all twelve mission sequences, interruption, state validation, delivery-carrier identity and idempotence; these are not twelve end-to-end browser playthroughs. Mounted equipment tests cover all four models, actual Rapier occlusion, first-person mounted origin, crew protection, stable hover and placement against original geometry.

The new native journeys are deliberately separate: Classic cooling; Tidegate cooling, android service and boat evacuation with an earned cargo reload; and aircraft travel, hoist, landing and report. All movement and progress must come from ordinary input. No actor, inventory, reward or mission-state assignment manufactures completion. The retained Express, portal, Tidegate/service and Classic journeys remain release requirements. No native/public pass is claimed until its actual run succeeds.

Failures are retained in verification/field-ops/. Two previously failing Express-candidate checks were reviewed: grip interaction legitimately reboarded a nearby jeep before a driver assumed walking, and the maintenance driver pressed A before stopping. Drivers now use ordinary Y exit and wait for the actual safe-work speed. Runtime acceptance rules were not weakened.

Local browser navigation is blocked by environment policy; rendered tests use hosted Actions rather than a workaround. Synthetic input, fixture worlds and software WebGL do not certify Xbox/Quest hardware, real hand tracking, stereo/passthrough, comfort, frame time or human enjoyment. Some mission branches are model-tested rather than native-tested; the final handoff must state the exact coverage. Hostile humanoid combat, independent per-vehicle ammunition economies, procedural contract generation and full character schedules are not part of this release.

Next opportunities are human testing of whether vehicle choice and approach knowledge create different solutions, and additional native coverage of distant Classic census, stand-off, delivery and induction routes. Do not substitute more mission titles for those tests or for improving the existing geography.
