The period navigation uses rounded ranges 252-201, 201-145, and 145-66 million years ago. Equal-sized chapter buttons are not a proportional timeline. Stops at 210, 150, and 67 million years ago are illustrative, not specimen dates or exact species ranges. Museum summaries are not a substitute for primary literature and expert paleontological review.

The walking world, evidence markers, colors, plants, poses, relative animal sizes, and fossil illustrations are stylized teaching material. The two Triassic animals represent different geographic regions. Movement animation is not a biomechanical claim. Trackway and rock-layer activities are invented teaching sites rather than real reported finds. The fossil-brushing activity is not a realistic excavation technique.

The new 3D expedition uses self-hosted Three.js, copied from the same public repository's existing distribution. Its original license notices are preserved; see vendor/LICENSE. The original field-guide renderer uses native WebGL. Technical references: https://threejs.org/docs/ and https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGL_API/Tutorial/Getting_started_with_WebGL
