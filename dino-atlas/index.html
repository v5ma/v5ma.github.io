RANGER 07 / EXPEDITION PAUSED

Take a breather.

WASD or arrows drive, steer or walk. V enters and exits nearby vehicles. Helicopter: Q rises, Z descends. Land before disembarking. Boats must approach shore before you get out. Space brakes; Shift boosts or sprints.

Aim with the mouse. Hold F to use the selected tool: 1 water, 2 herding zapper, 3 lure, 4 scanner. E operates gates, feeders, outposts and discoveries. R rights a vehicle in place. H sounds the horn; J hops a jeep. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, C switches cameras, M opens the map and B opens orders. Gamepad: triggers drive; left stick steers or walks; A interacts; X boards; RB uses a tool; LB cycles tools; D-pad up/down controls helicopter altitude.
