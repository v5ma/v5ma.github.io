XBOX / STANDARD GAMEPAD LAYOUT

Every control, within reach.

Ranch and Coast. Menu / Dispatch introduces every new activity. The horn reaches 42 m and shows orange waves. Water reaches 38 m. The zapper reaches 32 m and can arc to nearby animals. Tool halos show a successful hit. All tools are nonlethal.

New destinations. The wetland channel leads west to the coastal boat circuit. Follow 24 ordered buoys for a full lap. Three large buildings have landable roofs; land before exiting with Y. The maintenance lifts connect rooftops, mezzanine galleries and ground-floor archives. Narrow entrances require exploring on foot.

Move and look. The left stick steers or moves the ranger. In the helicopter it flies horizontally. The right stick looks and aims. Click the left stick to sprint or boost. Click the right stick to switch cameras.

Drive, fly, and sail. RT accelerates the jeep, buggy and boat. LT brakes or reverses. B is the handbrake or hover brake. In the helicopter, RT rises and LT descends. Land before leaving it.

Ranger equipment. On foot, RT fires and LT or LB aims. In a vehicle, hold LB to aim and press RT to fire without accelerating. X reloads the tank or battery. RB or D-pad right selects the next tool. D-pad left selects the previous tool.

Interact and explore. A interacts with animals, terminals and objectives. Y enters or exits a parked vehicle. D-pad up hops on land. D-pad down sounds the horn. The boat boards and disembarks at all four marked harbors. The wetland channel now opens to a complete ocean circuit.

Menus without a mouse. Menu pauses. View opens the map. D-pad or left stick moves focus, A selects and B closes. Left / right adjusts the focused setting. The right stick scrolls longer panels. The supply exchange uses the same controller navigation, so buying, selling, contracts and closing the market never require a mouse.

Supplies and recovery. Outposts replenish water and battery reserves and save checkpoints. Their supply exchanges have different prices, rival pressure, cargo trading and delivery contracts. Vehicles take no damage and automatically right themselves after a rollover. Sonic gates, gravity ripples and training blasts are spectacle effects, not damage systems.

Keyboard and touch. WASD or arrows move. E interacts. F boards or exits. R reloads. Q changes tools. G rights a vehicle in place. Space brakes, J hops, Shift boosts, and H honks. In the helicopter Z rises and X descends. Hold the right mouse button to aim, then the left to fire. C changes camera, M opens the map, and Escape pauses. Touch buttons offer the same core actions.

Right stick scrolls. B closes any panel. Physical controller support still depends on the browser and device.
