# Dino Atlas: Spectacle & Trade

This expansion layers a larger living reserve and an outpost economy over Ranger Operations while preserving the original resident IDs, walking expedition, save format, vehicles, tools, controller path and no-damage vehicle recovery model.

The reserve now exposes 30 journal species and 64 live residents. The original 40 resident IDs remain first and unchanged. Sixteen additional species and 24 open-range residents extend the survey without replacing the earlier journal.

World spectacle includes four illuminated sonic gates, three gravity-ripple research nodes, animated rival ranger convoys, stronger outpost beacons and expanding light rings. Sonic, gravity and training-blast effects are explicitly non-damaging. They do not reduce health or apply destructive force to animals or the player.

Audio remains procedural Web Audio. New layers include sonic-boom bass/noise sweeps, gravity-ripple oscillation, rival radio chirps, celebration bursts, contract stings and buy/sell cues. Existing engine, rotor, boat, footstep, wildlife, ambience and dynamic music layers remain intact.

The supply exchange is a legal park-logistics trading game inspired by regional-price cargo loops. It has 12 ordinary goods, finite cargo capacity, six outpost markets, shifting prices, rival pressure, buy/sell spreads and delivery contracts. Goods include ranger food, veterinary kits, fence hardware, batteries, biofuel, drone and LiDAR parts, camera traps, wetland reagents, culinary mushroom cultures, mycology substrate and fossil casting resin. Hard-drug trading is not part of the system.

The market is a native dialog in the controller focus system. D-pad or the left stick moves focus, A buys/sells/accepts, B closes, and the right stick can scroll longer panels. It therefore does not require a mouse to dismiss or operate.
