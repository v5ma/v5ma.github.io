## Focused historical update

Section 3.9 adds the 2022 temporal-interleaving, detection/rendering and selective-access sources. It distinguishes the historical uses of dominance from the separate measurements of encoding, persistence and downstream influence. The [historical-source ledger](HISTORICAL-SOURCE-LEDGER.json) gives exact quotations, hashes, Git dates and the limits of the September 2021 recording attribution.

No mathematical result, application, figure or prospective experimental design changes in this release. The technical supplement is reproduced byte-for-byte as v1.1, with its original version label and links retained. It is not presented as a newly revised supplement.

## Reproduction and preserved versions

All included analysis programs and result files are unchanged from the [v1.1 companion](https://github.com/v5ma/v5ma.github.io/tree/3fe373b2d2fe011e11467f1653eb92d52f461bfd/papers/companions/temporal-learning-v1.1-20261018/learning). Historical execution statements describe their original runs, not a new scientific computation for v1.2.
