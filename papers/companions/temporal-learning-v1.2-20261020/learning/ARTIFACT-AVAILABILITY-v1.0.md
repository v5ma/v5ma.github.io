# Supporting artifact availability — version 1.0

The paired PDFs contain the scientific argument, mathematical statements and proofs, prospective design, numerical summaries and adverse comparisons. The `analysis/` folder releases the original implementations and frozen outputs for Applications 01–05 and selected design/awareness fixtures. These are constructed computations, not biological results.

## Deliberate first-release exclusions

The complete private review archive is not distributed. It includes publisher-specific MATLAB code and derivatives whose redistribution permissions remain unresolved, third-party article caches, local audit machinery and development records. No MATLAB/SPM files, downloaded research papers, private correspondence or credentials are included here. Source citations and the comparator methods/results in the supplement remain available; this package is **not a complete independent replay of the external comparator**. Further reproducibility material may accompany a later version without delaying this first preprint.

Links from the PDFs to withheld development artifacts resolve to the explicit dispositions below, not to an implied downloadable file. Historical statements about preserving original source and complete records describe the retained research archive, not the contents of this smaller public release. Protocol text is available in main Section 6 and the cumulative supplement.
