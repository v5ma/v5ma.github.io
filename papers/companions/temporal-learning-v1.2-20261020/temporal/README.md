## Focused historical update

Section 1.1 adds three verified 2022 records: rapid interleaving beneath apparently simultaneous experience, recurrent detection/rendering, and selective dominance of conscious content. The exact wording, hashes, Git metadata and conceptual limits are in [the historical-source ledger](HISTORICAL-SOURCE-LEDGER.json). The September 2021 recording date remains catalogue evidence, not newly verified audio.

The comparison preserves a specific conceptual antecedent without claiming the exact Hesse/Tsao experimental result, a universal switching frequency, influence between authors, or validation of the complete SAN mechanism. Existing equations, applications, figures, negative results and prospective tests are unchanged.

## Reproduction and preserved versions

The included `analysis/check_state_selection.py` and its saved results are copied unchanged from v1.1. Run `python -B analysis/check_state_selection.py` for seven small constructed arithmetic checks. This does not download data, fit models or reproduce the Hesse recordings.
