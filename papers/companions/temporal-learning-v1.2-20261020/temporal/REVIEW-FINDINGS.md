# Review of the focused historical update

## Historical claims

Three public GitHub files match the frozen sources byte-for-byte. The two commit records agree with the dates used in the manuscripts: 8 June and 28 November 2022 in America/Phoenix. Git metadata, independently established public availability and the catalogue's September 2021 recording attribution remain separate. No new audio verification is claimed.

Temporal Ensembles adds the three sources in Section 1.1. Learning to Feel adds their selective-access connection in Section 3.9. Both preserve the bounded conceptual antecedent without attributing the exact conscious/suppressed-face experiment, numerical switching times, a universal clock or a dendritic triplet to the earlier passages.

## Interpretive corrections

The older dominance proposal contains persistence, recurrent pathways and amplitude/contrast language. The revision keeps these candidate explanations separate rather than silently converting all of them into effective connectivity. The Hesse comparison distinguishes feature-tuning gain from mean firing and remains a preprint comparison, not causal validation of the complete mechanism.

The historical source's stronger closing perception/action language is not adopted as a universal prohibition on effects of unperceived information. The existing prospective tests are explicitly prospective. No new experiment or data analysis is represented as completed.

## Preservation and presentation

Existing equations, applications, adverse comparisons, experimental designs and figure artwork are retained. The Learning to Feel technical supplement stays v1.1, byte-for-byte, with its original links and version label. Main-paper cross-references now identify v1.2 and retain links to both earlier versions.

PDF review removed a duplicated figure-number prefix inherited by Temporal Ensembles and compacted the reference typography. These are presentation changes, not altered figure content or results.

The public companion excludes private conversations, raw external literature, publisher-derived MATLAB code and complete private replay environments. The seven small arithmetic fixtures are the unchanged v1.1 programs; any replay is an integrity check, not a new scientific result. This editorial/source and release review is not independent scientific peer review.
