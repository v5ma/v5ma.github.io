## Ordered concept signature

The proposed operator joins seven separately checkable atoms:

1. Learned repertoire is distinct from its current effective expression.
2. Events have receiver-relative consequences.
3. Participation and relational binding determine what becomes jointly effective.
4. Granularity determines which differences survive for a declared endpoint.
5. Modal and temporal extent determine what kind of episode is assembled.
6. Action returns evidence and can change subsequent learning and recruitment.
7. Task and state reconfigure these quantities jointly without a universal high/low setting.

The signature is a retrieval and audit aid, not a requirement that an ancestor use the later vocabulary. Full context and negative controls matter more than word matches.
