## Strawman-compression check

The original argument is **not** that familiar inputs should always be ignored, that every novel input should update, or that high activity means learning. Familiar inputs may contain verified corrections or newly consequential relations; persistent discrepancies can be noise. ``Dissipation'' is the resolution of a specified transient mismatch while preserving competent readout, not erasure of memory or indiscriminate activity decay.
