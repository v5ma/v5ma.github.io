# Data and source access

This public companion supplies author-generated code, synthetic fixtures, model outputs, formal statements, analysis summaries and figures. It deliberately excludes private correspondence, source intakes, publisher PDFs, Dryad workbooks, cell-7 raw recordings and uncertain-license source arrays. Derived numerical analyses are not represented as newly collected biological observations.

The manuscript bibliography and source-specific contracts identify the external studies. References to omitted local files resolve to individual access records with identity/hash information where available. Obtain raw inputs from their original repository, verify the recorded checksum and follow its license before running data-dependent scripts. A failed external-data rerun must not be interpreted as a failed or successful biological replication.

Older frozen receipts may contain their original absolute workstation paths. These are historical provenance strings, not a requirement to use that drive and not a claim that those files are bundled. The portable checks described in REPRODUCE.md do not silently fabricate missing raw inputs.
