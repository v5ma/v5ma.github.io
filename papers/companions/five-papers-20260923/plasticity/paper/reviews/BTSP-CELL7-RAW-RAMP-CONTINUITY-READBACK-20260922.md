The high middle-pair shape similarity identifies a **candidate later within-cell state readback**. It is not a `p` value, a cross-animal effect, or proof of learning persistence on its own. The 100 spatial bins are repeated measurements from **one cell**. In particular, a correlation of two ramps does not provide an independent learned-correspondence map, a measured phase reference/PWD, dendritic eligibility, a response-decay course or an input-matched probe of changed receiving competence. Conversely, the correct source observation should not be discarded merely because it does not fill those SAN-specific fields.
