The exact causal countermodel agrees observationally in all 108 rational states. This strengthens the design requirement: separately measuring C, D and later Y, and predicting Y well, does not by itself identify D as a mediator. A latent receiver-state path can produce identical observations. The physical perturbation must have independently verified effects on the proposed course and on alternative pathways, including E and P. A command intended to change only D does not establish that it did so. Predictive held-out comparison, total intervention effect and path-specific mediation must be reported separately.

The direct journal pages failed and PMC opens reached a browser-security page; this bounded indexed-text check is not a fresh full-methods review.
