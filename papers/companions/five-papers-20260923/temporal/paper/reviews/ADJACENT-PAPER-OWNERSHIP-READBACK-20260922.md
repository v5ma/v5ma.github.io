The other three comparisons used their current manuscript openings, result statements and targeted mechanism passages; this is not yet a line-by-line full-text audit.

**Decision:** keep Temporal Ensembles as a distinct *candidate*, with a narrower causal target: independently measured recovery and retained content must predict a specified operating window, and a route-specific perturbation must change downstream use beyond what an equally informed flexible recurrent rival predicts. Present finite applications establish only schedule feasibility, a constructed readiness/competition cycle and passive I/O nonidentification. The present evidence does not establish branch triplets in tissue, biological necessity, or elementary experience.

**Still open before private final-draft designation:** full adjacent-text comparison, source-genealogy saturation proportionate to the historical claims retained, a genuinely matched route intervention or an explicit theory-and-methods genre that presents it as a future test, and source/claim validation. These are manuscript-quality gates; external higher-tier review and publication are later gates.
