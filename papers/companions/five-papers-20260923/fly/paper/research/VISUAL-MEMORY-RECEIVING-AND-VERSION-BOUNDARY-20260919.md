# Visual-memory receiving: source identity, model operation and limits

19 September 2026. This joins a new anatomical constraint to an executable
receiving question. It does not silently connect the existing hue and native
olfactory-memory models.

## Primary source and its actual contribution

Ganguly, Heckman, Litwin-Kumar, Clowney and Behnia's final 2024 study maps
visual projection neurons and local visual interneurons entering the
mushroom-body Kenyon-cell repertoire. This provides a specific route between
visual processing and memory circuitry, rather than a guessed connection
between two generic modules. The study distinguishes direct and indirect
visual channels and finds sparse, distributed sampling of an uneven input
repertoire. Its discussion leaves the assembly of coherent visual-object
representations open. That is relevant to the proposed SAN construction,
but does not experimentally establish NAPOT or multimodal experience.

The paper's methods principally specify FlyWire v630 and a >=5 contact rule.
It reports 74 inputs to 147 left KCγ-d cells and a visual connectivity
participation ratio of 9.1. Those are published claims, separate from the
archived numerical export inspected below. KCγ-d names a cell class; its
gamma character is not an oscillation frequency.

Primary route: [final article, 7 July 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-49616-z).
The [pinned repository](https://github.com/ishanigan/visual-inputs-to-mushroom-body/tree/f4e95eebfc08d03ebad913ee0eb19bcd9788fa04)
was identified from that article, not an unrelated search result. The initial
2023 preprint is not substituted for the final publication.

## Three distinct contracts, no silent repair

The source matrix CSV is exactly equal to its NPY counterpart: **79 source
rows, 147 target columns, 11,613 entries**. Its 528 nonzero entries total
11,489 contacts. Thirty entries equal five; none lies between zero and five.
Three target columns receive no retained input. Literal matrix IDs govern
the test, not current database IDs or positional indexing into another file.

The separately saved 147-cell ID list has a different ID set. Only 38/79
source IDs uniquely match the table's v630 field; 69/79 uniquely match its
dated October 2023 field. The notebook calls ID updating and live adjacency
fetching before saving the matrix, without a pinned version on those calls.
This explains why version reconciliation is necessary; it does not identify
the exact effective live query version. No current connectome is fetched to
replace it, and no missing identity is inferred by row position.

Notebook cell 34 removes the first numerical receiving column and uses a
strict >5 rule. Its input is the NPY matrix, which has no ID column to remove.
The saved literal operation consequently yields 146 targets and 495 edges,
versus 147 targets and 528 edges under the article's stated >=5 rule. These
are explicit operations, not an assertion about which historical execution
generated every published panel.

The main-text participation-ratio equation squares the covariance trace in
the numerator; notebook cell 46's displayed expression does not. Both were
calculated and retained, without selecting a form by closeness to 9.1:

| Export representation | Conventional PR | Literal unsquared ratio |
|---|---:|---:|
| All count entries | 15.437143 | 0.006808 |
| All columns, binary >=5 | 26.571971 | 8.355128 |
| Notebook slice, binary >5 | 25.837503 | 8.566774 |

The unsquared ratio changes when covariance is rescaled, unlike PR. These
are checks on this archived matrix, not reproductions of the final paper's
9.1, its olfactory result, randomization analysis or a measured activity
dimension. The source-data XLSX and exact final analysis state remain
unacquired. The 79-input export is not relabeled as the paper's 74-input
population.

## Why the executable extension belongs in the full construction

SAN requires a receiving organization to determine what an incoming
difference can do, not merely a final scalar novelty score. The new test
asks a restricted version of this question: can a selective change to
incoming contributions expose a contrast that was lost when those
contributions were mixed? A paired response repertoire is then used to
select the next receiving state, and only actual new evidence resolves the
relation used for action. Delivered movement revises a retained actuator
calibration. Predicted samples are not inserted as observations.

The [frozen plan](../../access/df463653cbe0ec3d.md)
declares all representations, 80 receiving states, the constructed contrast,
four disturbance levels and four cases before results. A smallest singular
vector defines each stress pair; this is deliberately in-sample design, not
held-out discovery. The initial paired prototypes are installed through
generous noiseless supervised familiarization. They are not the native
Huang/Luo plasticity circuit or proof of learned cellular morphology.

The [96 episodes](../../access/8d65e74a1f6603a3.md)
show 16/24 resolved choices with delivered queries, tied by the equally
informed ordinary observer. All 24 fixed-state cases and all 24 ignored-query
cases abstain. The remaining eight delivered-query cases also abstain. No
error-rate inference follows from these constructed midpoint disturbances.
Every representation retains its own contrast; differences between their
success counts are not a matched-stimulus effect of anatomy.

[M15](../MATHEMATICAL-SUPPLEMENT-12.md) gives an exact contrast witness, a
kernel-intersection result and the noise-ball boundary. The two identical
source rows each contribute five contacts to one target in the export.
Their dated-ID metadata predicts GABA for both LT43 cells, but reports zero
left KC contacts in that different table version. The nonnegative receiving
test therefore cannot be called a native excitatory circuit, a signed
conductance fit, or a validated pathway intervention.

## What remains to join

The mathematical W is a controlled mixing surrogate, not an experimentally
identified state-dependent Jacobian. A physiological extension must specify
how receiving receptors/conductances and current state implement selective
pre-mixing changes; arbitrary downstream amplification cannot undo an
already lost contrast. It must link identified upstream populations to
these particular ports, preserve tonic/PWD operations during reconstruction,
and join modalities and endogenous relations with plasticity and actual
action-return. No invented 4-channel-to-79-port map was added.

The exact Grabowska et al. dataset DOI was inspected for native temporal
observations. The public DOI request returned HTTP 403 and the DataCite
metadata route returned 404; this does not prove the data no longer exist.
No workaround or author contact was attempted. The published central-brain
attention result remains a source reference, not photoreceptor timing
calibration or a native oscillator supplied by this work:
[article](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2010749117),
[dataset route](https://doi.org/10.14264/43fb51f).

The full object remains multimodal and experiential, not a vision-only
decoder. These results create a concrete receiving-state interface and its
limits; they do not complete that object, demonstrate SAN-specific advantage,
or establish phenomenal experience.
