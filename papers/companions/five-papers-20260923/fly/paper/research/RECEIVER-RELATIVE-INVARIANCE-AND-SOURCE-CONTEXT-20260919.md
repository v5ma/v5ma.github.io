## What the coordinate control does and does not test

Draft 9's algebraic common carrier cancels from the relative readout. This is a correct mathematical and numerical result. The coordinate twin uses the **same relative dynamics, trained receiving weights and observation history**. It is therefore a coordinate re-expression, not an independently motivated competing physiological mechanism. Identical behavior cannot distinguish those descriptions, but it also cannot show that all temporal or phase relationships are irrelevant.

A receiver-relative proposal need not depend on an arbitrary shared phase origin. Nor should the next model be required to have no coordinate-equivalent realization: changing mathematical coordinates does not abolish a physical process. The valid next question is which measured input–receiver timing, receiving-state dependence or propagated difference has a specific causal consequence and how well an equally informed alternative explains it.

This is a precision correction to the interpretation of the earlier term “irreducibly oscillatory.” It is not a reversal of the saved result, a new favorable outcome, or an untested assertion that the present adapter implements the full SAN mechanism. The adapter still uses hypothetical dimensionless dynamics, resets each queried patch, and lacks a measured visual phase reference and the full PWD/NAPOT construction. Those are concrete missing quantities independent of coordinate invariance.

The no-coupling case also has a narrower meaning than an acute intervention: it is familiarized with its own altered dynamics. Its success establishes that this task is solvable under that relearned condition. A frozen-readout perturbation would ask a different question. Both must remain distinct from Christenson et al.'s physiological intervention and limited-refit comparisons.
