# Prisco Figure 4B/4D and supplement: exact source dependency

22 September 2026. Read-only analysis of the two SHA-verified [Prisco Dryad workbooks](../../access/51a5760223194112.md), reconciled with the [published eLife article](https://elifesciences.org/articles/74172). The original source files were not edited. The reproducible [source reader](../application/prisco-summary-v0/analyze.py) now checks the relationship below and records it in [RESULT.json](../application/prisco-summary-v0/results/RESULT.json).

The Figure 4B/4D workbook's `Sheet1!A3:L13` has four three-column blocks (`Mean, SD, N`) for APL ON Mch, APL ON Oct, APL OFF Mch and APL OFF Oct. There are ten populated rows, with no animal ID or time axis. The unweighted means of those ten exported `Mean` values are 64.052, 71.244, 116.198 and 177.486, respectively. Their corresponding displayed `N` ranges are 10–30, 10–30, 8–35 and 12–38. These are descriptive readbacks of summary rows, not independent ROI-level replicates or reconstructed calcium traces.

The supplement's `Sheet1!C2:D11` is **not independent new APL ON/OFF data**. Every one of its ten APL ON delta cells equals same-row Figure 4B/4D `Oct Mean − Mch Mean`; the same holds for every APL OFF delta cell (20/20 identities; maximum floating-point error below `2e-14`). Their mean deltas, 7.192 and 61.288, are also the differences between the corresponding unweighted mean columns above. The other two supplement columns concern PN and KC comparisons and have ten and nine populated rows, respectively. The sheet does not certify row-wise pairing *between those other populations or across ON/OFF groups*.

There is a source-label inconsistency: the supplement headers for its APL ON/OFF columns say **“Mch-Oct”**, but all twenty corresponding numeric rows equal **Oct minus Mch**, the opposite signed subtraction. The primary article's prose says Oct responses were higher after APL silencing, consistent with the positive numeric direction. This audit preserves the original labels and values without silently reversing either. The sign convention should be clarified before any downstream effect-size or graph caption promotes those columns.

The primary article identifies the intervention and interpretation: APL output was blocked with tetanus toxin in APL OFF animals, and the authors report larger Oct than Mch activity in those Kenyon-cell claw responses when APL is silenced; in APL ON controls the average response peaks were similar. The article reports `n=10` for Figure 4B/4D and its own ANOVA comparisons. This audit does **not** reproduce those ANOVAs, confirm each workbook row's animal identity, or turn the responsive-unit counts into an independent sample size. No p-value is estimated here.

The bounded Fly Brain use is evidence that a specific inhibitory route can change odour-dependent response normalization at the calyx under this preparation. It does **not** by itself establish native gamma phase, a SAN PWD, arbitrary receiver-port control, multimodal NAPOT reconstruction, action return or experience. The separate Figure 5C `n=8` versus article-caption `n=7` discrepancy remains unresolved; this Figure 4 readback neither explains nor repairs it.
