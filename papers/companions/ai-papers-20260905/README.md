# Two AI preprints: shared research companion

Micah Blumberg · Self Aware Networks Research Institute · September 5, 2026

This public companion accompanies two distinct papers that **share their experimental evidence**:

1. [AI Core Alignment Across Scales: Human-Guided Co-Regulation in Self-Aware Artificial Networks](project/output/pdf/ai-core-alignment-across-scales.pdf). How can legitimate guidance remain consequential through changes in scope, context, delegation, execution and learning?
2. [AI Mechanistic Interpretability and Self-Aware Networks: From Neural Decoding to Causal Self-Regulation](project/output/pdf/ai-mechanistic-interpretability-and-self-aware-networks.pdf). What separates decoding a representation, causally changing a model, and producing a coherent useful correction?

The PDFs are the unchanged, approved-format integrated Draft 10 texts: 15 and 17 pages. These are preprints, not peer-reviewed articles. Permission to publish is not a claim that independent scientific review or a complete author readback occurred. Historical documents retain the status that was true when written; this front door and the publication records describe the current release.

## Results and limits

The modern executed model is the pinned quantized text export of Qwen3.5-0.8B; GPT-2 and an earlier cached MiniLM encoder were also tested in their separately documented experiments. Original DeepSeek models were **mapped, not executed**. Naming a research organization or architecture implies no endorsement.

In the learned-Qwen test, ordinary competence was 48/48 answers. Linear and nonlinear learned corrections each obtained only **5/32 requested role answers and zero complete groups**. Privileged corrected-state donors passed all eight groups. Reproduction preserved this negative result. A changed tensor, lower reconstruction error, permitted intervention, or isolated correct answer does not establish coherent semantic repair. The donor controls use information unavailable to the learned interventions.

The original application suite recorded 84 passing tests, and four Lean files supply 31 accepted statements under explicit assumptions. Additional packaging, controller and reproduction checks are not added to those research counts. Failed development attempts remain available and are not accepted proof certificates. The experiments are small synthetic development fixtures, not independent population samples or evidence of scalable human oversight, ethical alignment, consciousness, or frontier-model generalization.

## What the package does and does not contain

Included: both manuscripts and their earlier drafts, scientific application code, fixed protocols and cases, saved outputs and fitted editors, architecture/results figures, proof sources and compiler history, numerical and native-reconstruction audits, and a browser evidence viewer. The two records attach the same companion; it is one shared evidence collection, not two replications.

Excluded: base-model weights, tokenizers, runtime binaries, raw private transcripts or chat attachments, clinical anecdotes, private review archives, credentials and unrelated projects. Dependency identity receipts and the upstream Qwen license are included without the corresponding binaries. The public genealogy preserves claim context and date qualifications without publishing the private original source archive.

Some unchanged historical receipts contain inert author-machine installation paths. They are provenance, not executable instructions or access to those locations. Public instructions use relative paths; no public discovery metadata requires the author's filesystem. Two public TeX copies use relative figure paths; the original PDF bytes and scientific text are unchanged. Distribution transformations are enumerated in the manifest.
