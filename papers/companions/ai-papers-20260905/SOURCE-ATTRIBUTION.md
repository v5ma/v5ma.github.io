## Scientific and authorship boundaries

Neuroscientific analogies, current AI implementation, measured experiments, formal claims and historical genealogy are distinct evidence classes. Modern causal abstraction, representation editing, activation steering, information-flow control and recurrent-state methods have their own credited prior literature. The papers do not claim that SAN invented all of mechanistic interpretability or that a conceptual ancestor predicted every later molecular or transformer result.

Neighboring papers already own network self-knowledge, External Cortex, shared-workspace engineering, receptor/population scaling and reciprocal AI-neuroscience translation. The [full-text ownership review](project/sources/SIBLING-FULL-TEXT-REVIEW-03.md) distinguishes those subjects from the two new questions. It is an authoring-agent review, not independent novelty certification.

Micah Blumberg is the sole named author of these releases. Mentioned people, laboratories and organizations are not represented as coauthors, reviewers, sponsors or endorsers without separate evidence. AI-assisted development and self-checks must not be relabeled human or independent review.
