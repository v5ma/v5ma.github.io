# Model mapping: primary-source presentation check

September 5, 2026. This is a bounded authoring-agent readback, not independent review, a new literature survey or new model execution. The [original transformer contract](../../sources/TRANSFORMER-BRIDGE-AND-ACCEPTANCE-04.md) and all earlier status files remain unchanged. The [current viewer](../../review-app/current-10/index.html) distinguishes tested and mapped models.

- **GPT-2:** the [official model implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/gpt-2/master/src/model.py) was reopened. Its causal attention, residual/MLP blocks and `past` interface supply actual intervention locations. Experimental checkpoint/quantization identity comes from the local pinned export receipts, not the mutable source branch.
- **Qwen3.5-0.8B:** the [official model overview](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B) specifies 24 layers, with six repeats of three Gated DeltaNet blocks and one gated-attention block. The generic family highlights mention MoE; the particular small model's feed-forward configuration and pinned local adapter are the authority for its dense blocks. The viewer does not describe this experiment as a large MoE or vision-system test.
- **DeepSeek V3:** the [official repository introduction](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V3) confirms Multi-head Latent Attention and DeepSeekMoE. Mapping therefore distinguishes latent state, output reconstruction, router selection and expert computation. No original DeepSeek model has been executed in this project.
- **DeepSeek V4:** the [current official release page](https://deepseek.com/en/news/v4-preview/) confirms token-wise compression and sparse attention. The older local technical-report intake retains its explicitly limited source depth and unresolved bibliographic-date distinction. This readback does not silently convert it into a full implementation audit or independent validation of release benchmarks.

All live pages were accessed through read-only web tools. No checkpoint download, external inference, private upload, model fitting or compiler job occurred. The viewer's model cards are a route into the actual methods and outcome records, not evidence that an organization has endorsed either paper.

The important publication contribution must remain discriminating evidence: a useful receiver/route intervention, a concrete authority property, a known failure mode or a testable prediction. Merely naming a current architecture does not supply that contribution. Dated SAN concepts, operational AI translations and measured consequences remain separate claim classes.
