## Existing owners and the residual

| Existing work | What its complete text already owns | Consequence for the new pair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The External Cortex Loop, v1.0 | Human EEG feedback incorporation; sensory-matched contingency interruption; exact versus mismatched reinstatement; independent task outcomes; latency, expectancy and artifact controls; acquisition versus later device independence | The MI paper cannot claim to invent matched feedback controls or useful-behavior endpoints. Its artificial-network contribution needs learned causal-use analysis and an explicit control-identification result. The passive-feedback null sharpens one identification risk but is not the promised active-regulation result. |
| The Network That Learns It Is a Network, v1.0 | Four distinct capacities: self-registration, learned self-model, embodied perspective and explicit network self-knowledge; educational mechanism transfer, calibration, updating and retention | Decoding activations or producing a self-description cannot be presented as new proof of explicit self-knowledge. The MI paper tests artificial mechanisms and control information, not a second human instructional protocol. |
| One Intelligence, Multiple Hands, single-author v3.1 | Stable identities, versioned commitments, role-specific retrieval, current-state transactions, correction propagation, provenance, consequence reconciliation and an 18-run deterministic two-task application | The CA paper inherits, rather than reinvents, these engineering mechanisms. Its remaining claim is retained human-authorized meaning and useful correction across learning and representational change. The origin-sensitive fixtures expose where supplying correct identities had hidden the problem. They do not yet demonstrate scalable oversight. |
| Reciprocal AI–Neuroscience Translation, v1.0 | Typed partial maps, learned receiver relations, held-out failure, correct/wrong rescue, off-target preservation, biological return and revision; synthetic proof/test examples with explicit measurement asymmetries | Neither new paper can own the general biological-to-engineered-to-biological method. MI instead investigates actual learned model states and feedback-information controls. CA focuses on authority and operative meaning. Basic observational non-identification is already present here and is not newly invented. |

## Decision

Keep the two research tracks separate provisionally because their questions and required evidence differ. Do not treat this as final novelty certification. The current CA application still depends heavily on supplied authority/ancestry, while the MI derivative result uses a conventional method and its pretrained assay is a passive null. A final abstract must be earned by the active, learned and independently evaluated contribution, or be narrowed honestly to a useful methods/counterexample paper with clear inherited ownership.

No earlier manuscript is invalidated by this review. The new drafts credit the existing operators and preserve their full arguments instead of revising a compressed version of them.
