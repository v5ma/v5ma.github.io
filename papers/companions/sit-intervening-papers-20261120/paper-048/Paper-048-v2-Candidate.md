# 1. Question and Claim Ceiling

The comparison question is:

> Does a defined map carry specified CFS objects, dynamics, invariants, or
> observables into a frozen SIT model while preserving their mathematical and
> empirical roles?

This question is narrower than whether the frameworks use similar words. It is
also narrower than whether later CFS work resembles earlier ideas in Micah
Blumberg's broader corpus. A mathematical relation, a narrative architecture,
and a historical priority finding require different evidence.

## 1.1 The problem in plain language

Two theories can produce the same familiar equation for very different
reasons. Conversely, two theories can organize information through the same
sequence of operations while using different objects, equations, and
vocabulary. Treating the first case as a unique match would overstate the
mathematics. Treating the second case as no match at all would miss a real
functional and historical relation.

This paper therefore separates four tasks:

1. reconstruct what each framework receives, transforms, and produces;
2. test whether a typed mathematical map preserves those roles;
3. compare the ordered explanatory architecture atom by atom; and
4. place each matched atom on a dated before-and-after timeline.

Only after those operations are visible are the paper's comparison terms
introduced. A **typed map** is a rule that says what mathematical object is
sent to what other object and what structure is preserved. A **narrative
architecture** is the ordered causal sequence that a theory says turns inputs
into outputs. A **priority delta** is a bounded matched atom for which one
dated public formulation is earlier; it is not a whole-theory ownership claim.

## 1.2 Relation levels and claim ceiling

The audit permits five relation levels:

1. shared vocabulary;
2. architectural analogy;
3. typed object or dynamics map;
4. proved limiting or invariant-preserving relation;
5. shared or competing empirical prediction.

Level 2 is reached for one generic variational sequence. At the effective
field-equation level, the frozen CFS regime, SIT submodel, and direct
Einstein-Dirac null also occupy the same equation class. That result does not
supply a map between primitive objects or derivations, and its zero
discrimination against the null prevents promotion to a distinctive
cross-framework mathematical relation. The newer-source audit additionally
finds a partial Level 2 pass for the ordered distributed-representation and
internal-feedback architecture. That positive narrative result is not erased
by the failure of a whole-framework typed map.
