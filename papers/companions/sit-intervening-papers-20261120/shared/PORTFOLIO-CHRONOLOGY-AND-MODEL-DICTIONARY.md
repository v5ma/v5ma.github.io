## 3. Shared Clock and Observable Dictionary

- **Field perturbation:** $r := \delta\rho_t / \rho_0$ (dimensionless).
- **Local intrinsic clock-response coefficient:** $\alpha_\rho$, defined by the fractional intrinsic (fixed-local-metric) clock frequency shift $y_{\mathrm{clock,int}} = \alpha_\rho r$.
- **Gravitational transfer coefficient:** $\mathcal{T}$, defined by $r = \mathcal{T}\,p$ where $p = \Delta\Phi / c^2$.
- **Total vs. anomalous gravitational redshift slope:** In a weak static gravitational potential $\Phi$, the total observed fractional redshift slope is
  \[
  \frac{d\ln\nu}{d(\Phi/c^2)} = 1 + \delta\alpha_\Phi, \qquad \delta\alpha_\Phi := \alpha_\rho \mathcal{T},
  \]
  where $1$ is the standard General Relativity metric redshift contribution and $\delta\alpha_\Phi := \alpha_\rho\mathcal{T}$ (also written $\alpha_\Phi^{\mathrm{anom}}$) is the **anomalous (non-metric, SIT-additional) fractional redshift slope** bounded by clock-redshift experiments. Thus the GR baseline is accounted for explicitly and $\mathcal{T}$ enters $\delta\alpha_\Phi$ **strictly once**.
- **Observable separation:** Event-count rate $N(\tau)/\tau$ (Paper 007) and fractional atomic transition frequency shift $\Delta\nu/\nu$ are distinct operational observables until linked by a calibrated microscopic detector/transduction model.
