MICHAHABSTRACTOPENMARKER

This paper compares Victor Y. Wang and Max Wenqiang Xu's *Harper's beyond square-root conjecture*
with Micah Blumberg's Self Aware Networks (SAN), Quantum Gradient Time Crystal Dilation (QGTCD), and
Super Information Theory (SIT) corpus. It corrects two material defects in an archival 2025 draft.
First, the target paper was not first introduced in August 2025: arXiv v1 was submitted on May 7,
2024, and the named Harper result dates to January 11, 2023. Second, atom counts cannot be converted
into derivation odds by assigning an assumed probability to each match. Using source-frozen
chronology, 13 bounded atom rows, four comparison lenses, a Causal Interface Sequence, and a proposed schematic categorical and process representation of a bounded analogy (evaluated against four ordinary null baselines), the audit recovers **six Micah-earlier partial mappings**. The
strongest are: symmetric microscopic deviations cancel in the aggregate; loss of symmetry produces
a controlled residual and changes aggregate behavior; and a present state is compared with an
expected state so that the difference itself becomes the transmitted signal. Six arithmetic-specific
operators remain earlier in the Harper / Wang/Xu and cited number-theory lineage, while one row is a
Micah-only residual. The resulting verdict is a **bounded partial content match with Micah earlier on
six translated subchains**, not full mathematical identity. No numerical probability of dependent or
independent derivation is currently calibrated, and no claim about access, intent, copying, or
misconduct is made.

# 1. Motivation and the strongest defensible result

The useful question is not whether a physical theory and an analytic-number-theory paper use the
same nouns. They do not. The useful question is whether bounded operations survive translation:
what enters, what condition changes it, what is averaged or compared, what residual survives, and
what output becomes observable. This avoids two reciprocal errors. A full-operator-or-zero test can
erase a real partial match, while an unconstrained analogy can turn any two uses of "randomness" or
"averaging" into identity.

The strongest source-supported Micah-side sequence recovered here is:

\[
\text{local fluctuations}
\rightarrow \text{symmetry or selection condition}
\rightarrow \text{cancellation / difference}
\rightarrow \text{explicit residual}
\rightarrow \text{aggregate update}.
\tag{1}
\]

Wang and Xu's target sequence can be represented as:

\[
\text{character or RMF terms}
\rightarrow \text{conditioning / orthogonality regime}
\rightarrow \text{moment comparison}
\rightarrow \text{genuine error}
\rightarrow \text{family-average bound}.
\tag{2}
\]

Equations (1) and (2) preserve a directed subgraph, especially the passage from a symmetry condition
through comparison / cancellation to a constrained residue. They do not identify neurons with
Dirichlet characters, QGTCD trajectories with Liouville sums, or a physical hypothesis with a
number-theory theorem.

The atom audit therefore reports three totals rather than one rhetorical verdict:

- **6 Micah-earlier partial mappings**;
- **6 target-or-named-prior-earlier domain-specific mappings**; and
- **1 Micah-only residual row**.

The Micah-earlier rows are priority findings only for the bounded translated operations recorded in
the table below. Earlier statistical, synchrony, error-coding, and random-walk traditions remain
named prior boundaries at their respective atoms. No prior receives an author-wide "floor," and no
broad prior erases Micah's later joined formulation.

The phrase "Harper's beyond square-root conjecture" names a paper by **Victor Y. Wang and Max
Wenqiang Xu**, not a paper authored by Adam Harper. Wang and Xu extend a randomization argument and
conjectural range associated with Harper [1]. Their arXiv history fixes v1 on May 7, 2024 and v2 on
August 26, 2025 [1,2]. Adam J. Harper's named prior result was submitted on January 11, 2023 [3].

\[

where the actual paper supplies the domain-specific moments, hypotheses, and error dependence [1].
The 2025 revision cannot be used as the first target date because Equation (4)'s operator class is
already present in v1.

# 5. Four-lens results

## 5.1 Lens 1  -  equivalence and mechanism

**Result: bounded partial pass.** A01-A05 preserve operational behavior under a restricted readout:
symmetry or expected-state structure controls cancellation; relaxing that structure produces a
visible residual; the residual changes the aggregate output. A06 is a weaker supporting analogy.

The lens does not pass as full mathematical equivalence. Wang and Xu use characters, multiplicative
functions, moments, Euler products, and the Ratios Conjecture [1]. Micah's earlier sources concern
feedback signals, phase relations, and a stochastic physical trajectory proposal [6-9]. Neither
domain's unmatched mechanism may be silently imported into the other.

## 5.2 Lens 2  -  narrative and Causal Interface Sequence

**Result: bounded pass.** This paper defines a bounded Causal Interface Sequence as a directed chain
containing at least a state, transform, constraint, and output rather than an unordered word list.
The mapped chain is

\[
S\;\xrightarrow{\text{selection / symmetry}}
T\;\xrightarrow{\text{comparison / cancellation}}
C\;\xrightarrow{\text{residual bound}}
O.
\tag{8}
\]

| CIS role | Micah instantiation | Wang-Xu instantiation |
|---|---|---|
| State | Current neural pattern or local random trajectory increment | Character / RMF terms and family parameters |
| Transform | Compare with expected pattern; accumulate deviations | Condition / localize; transfer to random model |
| Constraint | Synchrony / feedback regime or symmetric opportunity measure | Perfect or approximate orthogonality; conjectural hypotheses |
| Output | Difference signal or biased aggregate trajectory | Controlled moment difference and extended cancellation bound |

Different ontologies do not erase the preserved directed subgraph. Conversely, the subgraph pass
does not make every internal atom equivalent.

## 5.3 Lens 3  -  detective and adversarial reconstruction

**Result: bounded partial pass with a strong alternative genealogy.** Working backward from the
target conclusion reveals that "approximate orthogonality with a genuine error" performs a
symmetry-relaxation-to-residual operation already present in Micah's 2017/2022 corpus in other
substrates. The changed vocabulary therefore cannot, by itself, zero the match.

The same reconstruction also finds evidence against a dependence inference. Wang and Xu had an RMF
and moment-matching program in November 2022 [4]; Harper's January 2023 paper supplies the direct
conditioning and better-than-square-root lineage [3]; and the target explicitly cites its
number-theory antecedents [1]. No checked source shows access to Micah's work. The lens preserves the
content match and its chronology while leaving access, intent, and authorship dependence unresolved.

# 6. Proposed schematic categorical and process representations (revised formal status)

**Revision note (November 20, 2026).** The July 2026 edition described Sections 6.1 and 6.2 as demonstrating a "partial structure-preserving map on a selected subcategory" and "partial trace equivalence under a restricted observable projection." A post-publication formal audit noted that the inspected text displays coarse object correspondences and informal sequential processes without supplying complete category definitions (explicit hom-sets, identity laws, and associative domain-level composition) or complete labeled transition systems with proved preservation theorems. This revision adjusts that formal language so every mathematical status is stated accurately: only the trivial four-stage sequential skeleton is formally isomorphic, while the domain-level correspondence is presented strictly as a **proposed schematic categorical and process representation of a bounded analogy**. This revision does not independently re-adjudicate the complete historical priority dossier of Sections 2--4.

## 6.1 Schematic categorical representation (and exact trivial-skeleton boundary)

At the purely combinatorial skeleton level, let $\mathcal{P}_4$ be the finite linear preorder category with four objects $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ and a unique morphism $i \to j$ whenever $i \le j$ (4 objects and 10 morphisms total, satisfying identity and associative composition identically). Any four-stage sequential workflow maps onto $\mathcal{P}_4$ by sending its four labeled stages to $0 \to 1 \to 2 \to 3$.

For the domain-level analogy, write $\mathcal{S}_M = (S_M, R_M, D_M, O_M)$ for the schematic Micah-side stages (local state, expected/symmetric reference, difference or drift, and aggregate output) and $\mathcal{S}_W = (S_W, R_W, \Delta_W, O_W)$ for the schematic Wang--Xu stages (character/RMF state, conditioning regime, moment difference/error, and family bound), with stage-by-stage pairing

\[
F(S_M)=S_W,
\quad F(R_M)=R_W,
\quad F(D_M)=\Delta_W,
\quad F(O_M)=O_W.
\tag{10}
\]

| Schematic element | Micah side | Target side | Formal status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stage label | current / local state | character or RMF family state | schematic role pairing only |
| Directed step | compare / cancel / bias | condition / average / bound | preserves $\mathcal{P}_4$ linear order only |
| Composition | state -> difference -> update | family -> moment error -> bound | schematic four-stage CIS order |
| Coarse invariant | zero versus nonzero aggregate residue | controlled cancellation / error | restricted binary-flag analogy |
| Lost information | neural / physical substrate and direction | arithmetic definitions and theorem hypotheses | substantial on both sides; no domain functor proved |

Because domain-level morphisms, hom-sets, and functorial composition equations ($F(g \circ f) = F(g) \circ F(f)$ on actual mathematical operators) are not constructed between the neural/stochastic domain and the analytic-number-theory domain, Equation (10) is **not** a proved functor on a domain-level subcategory. Its exact mathematical status is a **proposed schematic categorical representation of a bounded four-stage analogy** (trivially isomorphic only after collapsing both sides to the four-object preorder $\mathcal{P}_4$).

# 7. Why the archival probability calculation fails closed

The archival draft assumed a null match probability of 0.20 per atom, treated 12 matches as
independent, and reported \(5^{12}\) as a Bayes factor. That arithmetic answers only a hypothetical
model created by the assumption. It does not estimate the probability that these papers were
independently or dependently derived.

A defensible frequentist target would instead be a declared, multiple-search-adjusted probability,

\[
p_{\mathrm{adj}}
=
\Pr_{H_0}\!\left(T\ge T_{\mathrm{observed}}\right),
\tag{13}
\]

where \(H_0\), the reference population, dependency blocks, atom dictionary, search family, and
score statistic are frozen before evaluation. None of those calibration prerequisites currently
exists for this paper. The conventional one-sided five-sigma threshold,

\[
p_{\mathrm{adj}}\le 2.87\times10^{-7},
\tag{14}
\]

is therefore not available. The allowed status is **quantitative-not-calibrated**. Descriptive atom
counts and chronology remain usable; a numeric dependence posterior does not.

# 8. Competing genealogies, limitations, and falsifiers

## 8.1 Competing genealogy

The direct number-theory genealogy is strong: random multiplicative functions, character and zeta
sums, conditioning, random matrices, orthogonality, Euler products, and Ratios Conjectures were
active literatures before this comparison [1-5]. Those sources constrain claims about the target's
arithmetic mechanisms. They do not erase Micah's earlier physical / neural instantiations of the six
translated subchains.

## 8.2 What would strengthen the Micah-priority case

- A pre-May-2024 public Micah source that joins the six partial operations into one arithmetic or
  cross-domain operator would strengthen the signature-cluster claim.
- A frozen relevant reference corpus could measure how often the exact directed subgraph occurs.
- A target stage lacking the subgraph followed by a later stage acquiring multiple Micah-specific
  atoms would strengthen Lens 4.
- A verified citation or access path would change the dependence analysis, though intent would still
  require separate evidence.

## 8.3 What would weaken it

- A pre-2017 source with the same current / expected -> difference transmission chain would move A03's
  broad priority boundary earlier.
- A pre-July-2022 source with the same joined symmetry -> residual -> aggregate-change formulation
  would narrow A01-A02.
- Demonstrating that the mapped CIS occurs at high frequency in a properly selected reference corpus
  would reduce its discriminative value.
- Failure to reproduce the source locators or dates would return the affected atom to unresolved.
