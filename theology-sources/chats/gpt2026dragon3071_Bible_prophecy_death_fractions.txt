Micah Blumberg:
During The end times bible prophecy is it 1/3 or 1/4th of the planet that dies?

Self Aware Networks GPT:
Both numbers show up, but they refer to different moments in Revelation.

In Revelation 6:8 (the fourth seal, the “pale horse”), it says that authority is given to kill “a fourth of the earth,” which is where the 1/4 figure comes from.

Later, Revelation repeatedly uses 1/3 language during the trumpet judgments. For example, Revelation 8 describes one-third of the earth/trees/sea creatures being struck in various ways, and Revelation 9:18 says that “a third of mankind” is killed by the plagues tied to the sixth trumpet, which is where the 1/3 figure comes from.

So if your question is “what fraction of people die,” Revelation explicitly says 1/4 at the fourth seal and later 1/3 of mankind at the sixth trumpet.

Micah Blumberg:
oh well one way of dicing Revelations is by arguing that it describes the same event four times in four different ways, so the question is does 1/4 of humanity die first, and then 1/3 of the remaining humanity die later.

Self Aware Networks GPT:
That “stacking” reading is possible, but it is not something Revelation states outright. It depends on whether you treat the seals and trumpets as a single chronological sequence, or as repeating cycles that portray the same period from different camera angles.

If you read them as sequential and non-overlapping, then yes, the arithmetic works like this: first `1/4` die, leaving `3/4` alive. Later, `1/3` of the remaining `3/4` die, which is `1/4` of the original population. In that strict sequential case the total death would be `1/4 + 1/4 = 1/2`, meaning half of humanity dead by the time both judgments have occurred.

If you read Revelation in a recapitulation framework, you usually do not add those fractions. Instead, the `1/4` and `1/3` are taken as stylized “limited but massive” judgments (not total annihilation), or as judgments aimed at different scopes in the imagery, or as overlapping descriptions of the same crisis. Under that approach, the text does not let you compute a single cumulative percentage of human deaths.

Micah Blumberg:
"If you read Revelation in a recapitulation framework, you usually do not add those fractions." what's that?

Self Aware Networks GPT:
It is a way of reading Revelation where the book is organized as repeating cycles, not as one long, strictly chronological timeline.

In a recapitulation framework, the seals, trumpets, and bowls are seen as different “passes” over largely the same end-times period. Each pass retells the story with new symbols, a different emphasis, and often increased intensity, rather than picking up where the previous pass left off like chapters in a normal history.

That is why people who read it this way usually do not add `1/4` plus `1/3`. They assume those fractions belong to different symbolic retellings of the same era (or overlapping judgments), not separate, non-overlapping events that you can stack like accounting.

A quick way to spot why people think this is plausible is that several of these series end with similar “end of the world” cues, like cosmic shaking, final wrath language, or scenes of ultimate judgment, which feels like “the end” arriving more than once if you insist everything is strictly sequential.

Sources
Revelation 6:12-17
Revelation 11:15-19
Revelation 16:17-21
