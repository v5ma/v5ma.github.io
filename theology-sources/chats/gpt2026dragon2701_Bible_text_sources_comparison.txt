### 2. The Sophia connection — why some see her there

Gnostic and wisdom-tradition readers notice that Revelation borrows language long associated with **Wisdom/Sophia**:

- “Clothed with the sun” parallels the *Wisdom of Solomon 7:26* description: *“She is a reflection of eternal light, a spotless mirror of God’s activity.”*  
- The crown of twelve stars evokes cosmic order—Sophia as the harmonizer of the twelve aeons in texts like *Pistis Sophia*.

In other words, **Sophia’s drama of fall and restoration** maps neatly onto Revelation 12’s celestial woman battling the dragon: both are cosmic mothers whose travail births redemption.

### 3. Why the “Sophia = Woman Clothed in the Sun” idea persists

1. **Esoteric continuity** – Modern esoteric Christianity (Russian Sophiology, Theosophy, Jungian mysticism) identifies the “return of Sophia” with the re-emergence of the divine feminine aspect of God.

3. **Symbolic depth** – In both traditions, the woman represents the **cosmic mediator**: she brings divine light into matter and then is elevated again—central to both Christian and Gnostic soteriology.
