### How “But” Divides Your Energy  
- **Cancelling Out Positivity:** When you say something positive and then follow it with “but,” you introduce a contradictory thought. The positive energy you’ve just generated is immediately countered by a negative or limiting belief.  
- **Mixed Signals:** The word “but” sends mixed messages. Instead of reinforcing a single, powerful desire or belief, it signals to your subconscious that there are conflicting priorities. This division can reduce the clarity and effectiveness of your focus.  
- **Fragmenting Focus:** The concept is that your energy is most potent when it’s fully concentrated on one intention. Adding “but” creates a split focus, meaning your energy is not wholly invested in your desired outcome.

### Examples

**Example 1: Personal Success**  
- **With “But”:** “I am really passionate about advancing my career, but I’m not sure I have what it takes.”  
  - *Issue:* The enthusiasm for your career is directly undermined by self-doubt.
- **Without “But”:** “I am passionate about advancing my career, and I am developing my skills every day.”  
  - *Result:* This reframed statement keeps your focus on growth and capability, reinforcing a unified, positive vibration.

**Example 2: Health and Well-being**  
- **With “But”:** “I am committed to living a healthy lifestyle, but I sometimes find it hard to exercise regularly.”  
  - *Issue:* The commitment to health is negated by the admission of difficulty, splitting your focus.
- **Without “But”:** “I am committed to living a healthy lifestyle, and I am finding creative ways to incorporate exercise into my routine.”  
  - *Result:* This approach maintains a positive, continuous focus on your goal, aligning your energy toward solutions rather than obstacles.

**Example 3: Relationships**  
- **With “But”:** “I am excited about building meaningful relationships, but I’m worried about past experiences repeating themselves.”  
  - *Issue:* The excitement about forming connections is diminished by fear and past negativity.
- **Without “But”:** “I am excited about building meaningful relationships, and I trust that every new connection brings positive growth.”  
  - *Result:* By removing “but,” you maintain a clear, positive outlook that aligns with your desire for enriching relationships.

### Final Thoughts  
By avoiding the word “but” and instead using “and” or simply stating your desire, you prevent the split in your energy. The aim is to keep your vibration aligned with what you truly want, without the interruption of contradictory thoughts.
