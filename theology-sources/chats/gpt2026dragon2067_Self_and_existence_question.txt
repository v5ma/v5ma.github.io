This **mass-as-a-time-crystal** picture means gravity isn’t a pull from mass directly; it’s a bias in motion caused by extra microscopic time slices near mass. Gravity emerges as particles drift toward regions where **time runs thicker**.

Mathematically, QGTCD modified Einstein’s famous equation $E=mc^2$ to include time-density. The early formulation added a correction term inversely proportional to the local time density $\rho_t$ (the symbol $\rho_t$ represents the **density of time frames**)【1†L122-L129】. In one draft, Blumberg expressed it as: 

- **Quantum Gradient Time Crystal Dilation law:** $E \;=\; mc^2 \;+\; \frac{k}{\rho_t}$【1†L122-L129】, 

where $k$ is a constant quantifying how much “extra energy” arises from dilating the stack of time frames. In regions of high time density (large $\rho_t$), the correction $k/\rho_t$ is small, effectively reducing the energy. In a region where time frames are extremely dense (such as near a black hole), a clock ticks more slowly, and QGTCD predicts a decrease in the effective mass-energy. In other words, **mass and energy become *cheaper* in environments where time is plentiful**. This idea connected to gravitational time dilation: a clock in a strong gravitational field runs slow – Blumberg’s twist was to attribute that to a *real field* $\rho_t(x)$ that can vary across space【5†L53-L61】【12†L452-L460】. Gravity became a bookkeeping of time frames: matter “crystallizes” time around it, and other objects fall into these dense time zones【4†L58-L66】.

Although radical, QGTCD was carefully crafted to dovetail with known physics. In a high $\rho_t$ region (near mass), a given energy $E$ corresponds to a *lower* apparent frequency – akin to gravitational redshift, but now derived from first principles of an underlying time-density field. By 2023, Blumberg had shared drafts of QGTCD on GitHub and Substack, refining the math and building intuition. ## Super Dark Time: Gravity as Time Density (2024)  
By early 2024, Blumberg pushed the QGTCD concept further, integrating it with cosmological mysteries. He coined the term **“Super Dark Time” (SDT)** for a new approach to gravity and cosmology. ## Coherence Enters: R<sub>coh</sub> and SuperTimePosition (2024)

By January 2025, the coherence ratio $R_{\text{coh}}$ had become a cornerstone of Blumberg’s framework【5†L53-L61】. ## A New Thermodynamic Law: Coherence and Entropy (2025)
