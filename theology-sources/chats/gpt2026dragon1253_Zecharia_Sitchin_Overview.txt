1. **Misinterpretation of "Nibiru":**
   - In actual Mesopotamian astronomy, *Nibiru* was a **term used for a crossing or a location in the sky**, often associated with **Jupiter, Mercury**, or even a **point on the ecliptic**, not a distant planet.
