Good question. The short answer is: **yes, many European gods can be traced back (by scholars of comparative mythology and linguistics) to very ancient Mesopotamian and Near Eastern roots**, but the picture is layered and complex.

---

### 1. Indo-European vs. Near Eastern Origins
- Most of the gods worshipped in Europe (Zeus, Odin, Thor, Apollo, etc.) are usually understood as belonging to the **Indo-European pantheon**, reconstructed by linguists as “Proto-Indo-European religion.”
- For example:
  - *Dyeus Ph₂tēr* → Greek **Zeus**, Latin **Jupiter**, Sanskrit **Dyaus Pitar**.
  - The thunder god (*Perkwunos*) → Slavic **Perun**, Norse **Thor**, Baltic **Perkūnas**.

So, on one layer, they are “native” to the Indo-European language family, not directly borrowed.

---

### 2. Cultural Borrowings from Mesopotamia
However, when you trace myths and motifs, you find many **borrowings from Sumerian and Akkadian traditions** that flowed into the Mediterranean and then into Europe:
- **Flood myth**: Sumerian *Ziusudra* → Akkadian *Utnapishtim* → Biblical Noah → Greek *Deucalion*.
- **Inanna/Ishtar** (goddess of love/war) parallels **Aphrodite/Venus**.
- **Dumuzi/Tammuz** (dying-rising god) parallels **Adonis**, later linked to seasonal fertility cults in Greece.
- **Underworld journeys**: Inanna’s descent echoes in Persephone’s abduction to Hades.
- **Sky-god succession myths** (Anu → Enlil → Marduk) resonate with Uranus → Cronus → Zeus.

---

### 3. Transmission Channels
- **Phoenicians and Canaanites** acted as cultural transmitters, blending Mesopotamian motifs into the Mediterranean.
- **Hittites** in Anatolia absorbed Mesopotamian myth (they translated the *Epic of Gilgamesh*), and the Hittite storm-god influenced both Greek Zeus and later Indo-European storm-gods.
- Through **trade, conquest, and shared cosmologies**, motifs diffused westward long before the Roman Empire.

---

### 4. Conclusion
So:  
- European gods are not *exclusively* Sumerian, but **they are part of a layered system** where Indo-European roots were enriched with Sumerian/Akkadian/Babylonian mythic material.  
- In other words: Zeus, Odin, Thor have Indo-European “DNA,” but their myths show Near Eastern “borrowed clothing.”  

That means if someone says “European gods are European,” they’re partly right in linguistic terms. But if you dig deeper, the genealogy of myths does lead back to **Sumerian prototypes** and Near Eastern religious systems.
