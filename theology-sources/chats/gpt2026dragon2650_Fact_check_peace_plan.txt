## 4) If you *do* want a **predictive** theory, here’s the least‑forced route

- **Drop date arithmetic** (Daniel’s “weeks” are already used once in the 2nd‑century BCE). Instead, adopt **“recapitulation”**—Revelation’s cycles portray **the same end‑conflict** from different angles (seals, trumpets, bowls) and can **replay** at different scales until a final consummation. That’s a recognized reading that avoids fragile timelines. citeturn5search2
- **Allow multivalence**: like the way many scholars read **Babylon = Rome** *and more than Rome* (an archetype of God‑opposed empire). You can consistently say “X looks ‘Babylon‑ish’ / ‘Beast‑ish’ ” without claiming the text *only* meant X. citeturn0search10

## 5) What you’d still need to see (text‑based “tripwires”)

Before anyone says “this leader **is** the Beast,” look for **hard signals** the texts make central:

- **Enforced worship/loyalty rites** with **sanctions** for non‑participants (not just soft power or public ceremony).  
- **Transactional exclusion** tied to a recognizable **allegiance marker** (Rev 13:16–17).  
- **A sacrilege event** in relation to a **holy site** that plausibly fits Daniel’s “abomination” logic.  
- **Sustained global coalition** (horns/heads) where **coercion + cult + commerce** interlock.  
If those don’t materialize, the resemblance remains **rhetorical** or **typological**, not a live fulfillment claim.
