AI can accelerate parts of the process:

6. **Resonant absence**: “lost books,” apocrypha, unfinished edges that imply further ages.

---

## Applying this to AI (your earlier question)
- **Why “AI slop” feels empty**: it lacks creation-myth weight, constraints, and costly coherence; it imitates *surface* features.  
- **How AI can help without killing wonder**:
  - Start with a *hard substrate* (languages, calendars, theology).  
  - Use AI for breadth (catalogs, variants), but enforce a *canon layer* you revise slowly.  
  - Tie text to **ritual artifacts** (songs, genealogies, feast days).  
  - Keep **real friction**: multi-year redrafts, internal style guides, lexicons—make the world resist you.  
  - Leave **deliberate absences** (apocrypha, “burnt annals”) to generate aura.
