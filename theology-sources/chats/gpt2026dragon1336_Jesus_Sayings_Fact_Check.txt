### 🧠 What does it mean?
This is a metaphor, and scholars, mystics, and theologians have interpreted it in multiple ways. Let’s explore three major interpretations—then bring it back to your point about “serving two masters.”

---

### 🔍 1. **Focus and Clarity (the “single eye”)**
- “Single” (Greek: *haplous*) can mean **undivided, whole, or pure.**
- In this view, Jesus is saying:
  > If your spiritual focus is undivided—like a clear, single beam of intention—then your whole being will be **illuminated with insight, truth, and spiritual light.**

It’s about **inner clarity**, like pointing a spotlight at one thing instead of scattering your attention everywhere.

---

### 💸 2. **Generosity vs. Greed**
In Greek, *haplous* (single) was also used to mean **generous** or **open-handed** (as opposed to stingy or greedy).

- In Hebrew idiom, a “good eye” meant generosity, and a “bad eye” meant selfishness.
- So Jesus may also be saying:
  > “If your eye is generous, you’ll be filled with light (goodness); if your eye is selfish or greedy, you’ll be filled with darkness.”

This fits perfectly with the **next verse**:

> “No one can serve two masters. You cannot serve both God and money.”  
> —Matthew 6:24

So yes—your connection is spot-on.

---

### 🧘‍♀️ 3. **Mystical/Esoteric Reading**
In mystical traditions (especially Gnostic and contemplative Christian readings), the “single eye” is often taken **literally** as referring to the **“third eye”** or **inner perception**—your inner faculty of spiritual sight.

- When that “eye” is opened, you see with divine light.
- This interpretation aligns with **Luke 11:34–36** and **the Gospel of Thomas Logion 24**.

---

### ✅ In summary:
- **Yes**, the “single eye” is tied directly to the idea of not serving two masters.
- Jesus is saying: **if your attention, values, and desires are focused on God—not split with greed or ego—then your whole being will radiate clarity and light.**
- If you try to split your loyalties (between God and money, or spirit and materialism), your perception becomes darkened.
