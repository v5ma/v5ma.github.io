### 1. Is it a military psyop?

What we *actually* know:

- The Law of Attraction comes out of **New Thought / metaphysical** movements from the late 1800s and early 1900s, then gets repackaged over and over (Think and Grow Rich, The Secret, etc.).
- There’s **no credible trail of evidence** (documents, whistleblowers, declassified material) showing “we invented LOA as a military psychological operation.”

So in the strict sense of *“this was designed in a Pentagon basement to control the masses”* → **no evidence.**

But the deeper thing you’re sensing is:

> “Even if they didn’t invent it, can this belief system be *used* like a psyop?”

That’s a different question – and there, the answer can be **yes, functionally.**

---

### 2. “Subconscious conscription” – how LOA can act like self-policing

If you strip the mysticism away, LOA says:

- “Whatever you focus on, you attract.”
- “If something bad keeps happening, your vibration / thoughts are the cause.”
- “Fix your mind and the universe will line up.”

That can become a kind of **inner cop** installed in your psyche:

1. **Self-blame as control mechanism**  
   - If you’re poor, sick, traumatized, or oppressed, LOA can tell you:  
     “This is your fault energetically.”  
   - That shifts attention away from **systems** (politics, institutions, exploitation) and turns everything into,  
     “You just didn’t visualize hard enough.”

2. **Depoliticization**  
   - If everyone is taught, “Don’t focus on problems, you’ll attract more problems,” then:
     - Don’t talk about war.
     - Don’t talk about corruption.
     - Don’t talk about climate, racism, housing, genocide.
   - That’s extremely convenient for anyone who *benefits* from those problems not being examined.

3. **Thought-crime vibes**  
   - LOA often pathologizes normal, adaptive reactions:
     - Anger at injustice → “Low vibration.”
     - Grief / fear → “You’re manifesting more of it.”
   - Result: people **suppress** authentic signals instead of using them to navigate reality.

4. **Endless self-optimization instead of collective power**  
   - Instead of “organize with others to change conditions,” you get:
     “Upgrade your mindset, your brand, your vibration, your hustle.”
   - That’s very compatible with **neoliberal, individualist control** – not because of a specific agency, but because the ideology slots perfectly into the existing power structure.

This is what you’re pointing at with **“subconsciousness conscription”**:  
you conscript your own subconscious to serve an external agenda you never consented to.

### 3. “Psyop” vs “psyop-like”

So I’d phrase it like this:

- **Not proven:** A top-down military psyop with a project name and budget line.
- **Very real:** A belief system that:
  - Encourages self-blame instead of systemic critique.
  - Discourages political awareness by branding it “negative.”
  - Rewards passivity dressed as “spiritual alignment.”
  - Is massively monetized by coaches, influencers, and some corporate cultures.

That’s **psyop-like behavior**, even if it evolved via capitalism + spiritual consumerism rather than a single covert operation.

You’re basically asking:

> “Is this an intentionally loaded memeplex designed to disarm people’s critical faculties and redirect blame inward?”

And my answer is:

- It **can absolutely function that way.**
- It does **not require** a secret military origin to be dangerous or controlling.

---

### 4. How to use the “useful bits” without getting conscripted

If you want to salvage anything from LOA without letting it colonize your psyche:

- Keep:
  - “Attention shapes behavior” → focusing on a goal can change what you notice and how you act. That’s just `reinforcement + salience`, not magic.
  - Visualization and optimism as **tools**, not metaphysics.
- Reject:
  - “You attracted your abuse / oppression.”
  - “Don’t talk about injustice or you’ll manifest more.”
  - “If you’re struggling, your soul is low-level.”

And add your own counter-spell:

- “My mind matters, but **structures also exist**.”
- “I can work on my inner state **and** fight external harm.”
- “Real spirituality doesn’t require gaslighting myself about reality.”
