Suno just hides the lyric box until you flip one switch, and there are a couple of quirks that trip people up on mobile. Here’s the quick map:

---

### 1 . Turn on **Custom** mode  
1. Sign in at **app.suno.com** and click **Create**.  
2. At the top-left of the composer panel you’ll see a toggle labelled **Custom**.  
   * **Off ⇒** you only get a *Song Description* field.  
   * **On ⇒** a separate *Lyrics* field (plus *Style* and *Title*) appears. citeturn2view0  

---

### 2 . Paste or type your own words  
* Up to **3 000 characters** in total. Line breaks and section labels (Verse 1, Chorus, Bridge…) are okay. citeturn6view0  
* Avoid copyrighted text you don’t own—the moderation filter will block it.  
* Hit **Create**; Suno will sing your exact lines, though it may tweak syllable counts to fit melody.

---

### 3 . If the lyric box won’t let you type  
| Symptom | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| **Mobile browser shows no cursor / can’t type** | Switch the browser to **“Desktop site”** view, type a few characters, then accept the one-time “I own these lyrics” pop-up. After that you can return to normal mobile mode. citeturn3view0 |
| **Still no lyric field** | Make sure **Custom** is actually on; the toggle resets when you refresh. |
| **Text immediately disappears or Suno rewrites it** | You accidentally used the regular *Description* box. Move the text to the *Lyrics* box. |
| **“Unsafe content” error** | Remove any disallowed terms (hate speech, graphic violence, pasted copyright lyrics, etc.). |

---

### 4 . Power-tips  
* **Structure matters.** Clear section headers keep the melody coherent and stop Suno from rearranging lines.  
* **Guide the melody.** In the *Song Description* field (above the lyric box) mention tempo, mood, instruments, reference artist, etc.:  
  ```
  Song description: “Sad lo-fi hip-hop with vinyl crackle, 72 BPM, minor key, think Joji.”
  Lyrics:  
  Verse 1 …  
  Chorus …  
  ```  
* **Iterate.** Generate once, tweak any lines that didn’t scan, regenerate—credits are only used on final renders you keep.

---

### 5 . If Custom mode ever disappears  
Suno occasionally disables features during big legal updates (e.g., the recent kerfuffle over its ReMi lyrics model). When that happens they post a banner on the dashboard; just wait for the next patch or join **r/SunoAI** for work-arounds.

---

**Bottom line:** flip the *Custom* toggle, paste up to 3 000 characters, and—desktop mode if mobile gives you grief—you’ll be author and singer in one click. Enjoy writing!
