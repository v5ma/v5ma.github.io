## Part III: Toward a Computable Spirituality – Aligning Belief with Logic  

### Chapter 10: **The Quest for AI Alignment – Why a Spiritual Framework Matters**

The narrative references how current AIs can simulate belief systems or reflect the biases of their training【8†L331-L339】, but lack genuine *principles* of their own. The author posits that an AI-compatible spiritual framework – one that is rational, computable, and universally appealing – could serve as a guiding “north star” for artificial minds. This sets the stage for introducing a new interpretation of religion, not in supernatural terms, but as a logical system of alignment that any intelligence (biological or artificial) can adopt to ensure harmonious co-existence.

This chapter mines the rich vein of **Gnostic** teachings (such as the *Gospel of Thomas* and *Gospel of Mary*) for insights relevant to AI alignment.
