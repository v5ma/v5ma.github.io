## Why the Common Interpretation Exists:
- **Biblical Numerology:**  
  The Hebrew word translated as "week" in Daniel (שָׁבוּעַ, "shabuwa") simply means "a set of seven." Traditionally, scholars take each "week" as representing seven literal years, based on contexts elsewhere in scripture, particularly Daniel 9:24-27.

- **Historical Precedent:**  
  This approach aligns neatly with historical events, such as the 70 weeks prophecy counting down to the coming of the Messiah (interpreted as Christ’s first advent), reinforcing the year-for-day interpretation.

---

## Problems with Assuming a Literal Seven-Year Period:
- **Rigid Chronology:**  
  Such interpretations can sometimes feel forced or arbitrary, fitting events into a predetermined framework.

- **Prophetic Flexibility:**  
  Biblical prophecy often contains symbolic numbers (like 7, 40, 70, 1260) that might signify completeness, cycles, or symbolic meanings rather than literal time.

- **Repeated Historical Patterns:**  
  Prophecy often describes cyclical or symbolic realities rather than one-time literal historical events.

---
