**1. Synoptic Jesus (Ethical Apocalyptic Prophet)**  
**Diagram Title:** *“The Kingdom Is Near: Ethics Before Eschaton”*  
**Visual Structure:**  
- Timeline with an **imminent apocalypse** approaching  
- Jesus with open arms and halo, surrounded by poor and sick  
- Emphasis on **deeds** (food, clothing, mercy icons)  
- Arrow pointing to “Entrance into Kingdom” = “Love your neighbor”  
- Contrast strip at bottom: “No vicarious atonement” / “No emphasis on belief”

**Key Difference:** Salvation through *behavioral obedience* in the face of a near-future divine intervention.

---

**2. Pauline Christ (Crucified–Risen Savior)**  
**Diagram Title:** *“Grace Over Law: The Cosmic Transaction”*  
**Visual Structure:**  
- Cross at center, with chains breaking  
- “Sin” and “Death” personified in shackles  
- “Faith” arrow to cross, “Justified” badge appears on believer  
- De-emphasis on law/deeds (Torah scroll crossed out)  
- Radiating light from resurrection to symbolize “New Creation”

**Key Difference:** Salvation is a **cosmic gift**, not earned but *received by faith in Christ’s death and resurrection*.

---

**3. Gnostic Jesus (Revealer of Hidden Knowledge)**  
**Diagram Title:** *“Know Thyself, Escape the World”*  
**Visual Structure:**  
- Human figure with **spark inside chest**, ascending past Archons  
- Jesus pointing not to the cross but to the stars and inner self  
- Labels: “Ignorance = Bondage,” “Knowledge = Freedom”  
- No cross, no judgment; instead, **hidden sayings scroll**

**Key Difference:** Liberation comes through **awakening to inner divinity**, not through forgiveness or judgment.

---

**4. Johannine Christ (Divine Logos)**  
**Diagram Title:** *“Word Made Flesh”*  
**Visual Structure:**  
- Jesus framed by Greek word *Logos*  
- Beam from Heaven to Earth = Incarnation  
- I AM statements floating around him (“I am the Light…”)  
- Cross = glorification, not sacrifice  
- Dual emphasis: *believe in me* AND *abide in me*

**Key Difference:** Jesus is not just a teacher or sacrifice but the **pre-existent divine Word**, revealing God directly.

---

**5. Ebionite Jesus (Adopted Mortal Prophet)**  
**Diagram Title:** *“The Righteous One, Chosen at Baptism”*  
**Visual Structure:**  
- Jordan River scene with dove and voice from heaven  
- Scrolls of Law behind him, labeled “Torah Kept”  
- Human-only Jesus, no halo or glow  
- *Adopted, not begotten* label with legal metaphor icon

**Key Difference:** Jesus is **fully human**, chosen by God for obedience—not divine, not pre-existent, not crucified for sin.

---

**6. Docetic Christ (Phantom Messiah)**  
**Diagram Title:** *“The Illusion of Flesh”*  
**Visual Structure:**  
- Glowing Jesus floating above the earth  
- Cross as a backdrop with label “Seemed to suffer”  
- Transparent body, no shadow  
- Gnostic-style labels: “Matter = Illusion,” “Spirit = Truth”

**Key Difference:** Jesus **did not truly suffer or die**—his body was an appearance, not material.

---

**7. Revelation Christ (Apocalyptic Warrior King)**  
**Diagram Title:** *“The Rider on the White Horse”*  
**Visual Structure:**  
- Jesus with sword from mouth, eyes like fire  
- Seven lampstands, seals, beasts in the background  
- Emphasis: **Judgment, Cosmic War, Lamb Who Conquers**  
- Time line ending in “New Jerusalem descending”

**Key Difference:** Jesus is a **cosmic warrior and judge**, not a teacher or revealer—but the final victor in the divine drama.

---

**8. Chalcedonian Christ (God-Man Union)**  
**Diagram Title:** *“One Person, Two Natures”*  
**Visual Structure:**  
- Mandala split into gold (divinity) and earth (humanity)  
- Jesus at center with vertical label: “Unconfused, Unchangeable, Indivisible, Inseparable”  
- Theological formulas around: “Truly God, Truly Man”  
- Arrows toward both heaven and earth

**Key Difference:** Jesus is **fully divine and fully human simultaneously**, according to orthodox creeds.
