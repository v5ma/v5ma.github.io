Would Supabase Free work?
Technically: yes. 30–60 MAU is nowhere near any limit.
Practically (for a business): Free is risky because:

The biggest money-saving trick (regardless of platform)
You probably do NOT need to import and manage all 13,400 historical customers inside your new app’s auth system on day 1.

Instead:
- Keep the “customer database” where it already lives (CRM/email list/shop platform).
- In the new diagnosis app, only create accounts for the small subset who actually use the feature (or issue them magic links when they buy).
That keeps your auth and support load low.
