### 2  What Jesus believed about **God**

1. **God is the hidden, living “Father” already present inside creation and inside every person.**  
   *“The kingdom is inside of you and outside of you … when you know yourselves … you will know that you are children of the living Father.”* – Thomas 3 citeturn1view0  
   *The Gospel of Truth* depicts the Father as the **“uncontainable, inconceivable One … who only wishes to be known and loved.”* citeturn6view0  

2. **Ignorance, not guilt, is humanity’s core problem.**  
   Error is “a fog” woven by forgetfulness; salvation is the Father’s self-disclosure that dissolves the fog, not a penal payment. citeturn6view0  

3. **Dualism of Light vs. Darkness** (shared with the Essenes) explains cosmic struggle, but unlike Qumran’s call to *hate* the “sons of darkness,” Jesus pushes the line further—“love your enemies” (a deliberate counter-reading of the Community Rule) - cf. 1QS 1.3-4 vs. Matt 5:43-45. citeturn10view0

### 4  Jesus’ **message of salvation**

1. **Immediate, experiential “Kingdom”** – not a postponed apocalypse but a reality discoverable “right here, right now” through conscious awareness. (Thomas 3, 113). citeturn1view0turn0search0  
2. **Salvation = awakening**: *“He showed them a Way, and the Way is the Truth … they knew and were known.”* – Gospel of Truth citeturn6view0  
3. **Liberation from the archons / world-systems**: human structures of power (political or spiritual) feed on ignorance; enlightened ones become untouchable: *“If anyone receives the light … no one will be able to trouble him.”* – Philip citeturn5view0  
4. **No vicarious atonement is required.** Jesus’ death is interpreted as the dramatic public “publication” of the Father’s edict of knowledge (Gospel of Truth), not as an appeasement of divine wrath—a sharp contrast with later Pauline theology. citeturn6view0

### 6  A compact synthesis in Jesus’ own register

> **“Know yourselves; you are the light.  
> Split a log and I am there, lift a stone and you will find me.  
> Make the inside like the outside, the male and the female a single one,  
> and you will enter the Father’s domain that is already spread upon the earth.  
> Accept the resurrection while you breathe,  
> and no ruler of this world can bind you again.”**

This, drawn from the logia and set against the practices of the Dead-Sea pietists, is arguably the closest we can come to hearing Jesus **without** the later theological overlay of Paul or the Johannine high-Christology.
