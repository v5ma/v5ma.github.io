The claim that “Jesus is God Mazda” likely refers to the idea that Jesus or the Christian God is somehow derived from or identical to *Ahura Mazda*, the supreme deity in **Zoroastrianism**. This claim can be **debunked** or **clarified** as follows:

### 1. **Different Religious Origins**
- **Ahura Mazda** is the highest god in **Zoroastrianism**, a religion founded by **Zoroaster (Zarathustra)** in ancient Persia, possibly around 1000–500 BCE.
- **Jesus** is the central figure of **Christianity**, which emerged in the 1st century CE in Roman-occupied Judea, deeply rooted in **Second Temple Judaism**, not Zoroastrianism.

### 2. **Theological Differences**
- Ahura Mazda is **uncreated**, the source of all that is good, and opposed by Angra Mainyu, the evil spirit. Zoroastrianism is **dualistic**: two opposing forces shape the universe.
- Christianity teaches **monotheism with a trinitarian view**: Jesus is the Son in the Trinity (Father, Son, Holy Spirit) and not in opposition to an equal evil force.

### 3. **No Historical Identification**
There is **no historical or textual evidence** that early Christians identified Jesus with Ahura Mazda. The Gospels and epistles describe Jesus in terms of **Jewish Messianic expectations** and **Hebrew scriptures**, not Persian religious concepts.

### 4. **Possible Confusion or Syncretism**
Some ancient and modern thinkers have tried to **draw parallels** between Zoroastrianism and Christianity (e.g., concepts of good vs evil, heaven/hell, final judgment), but **parallels do not imply identity**. These likely arose from cultural exchanges, not direct derivation.

### Conclusion
The idea that “Jesus is God Mazda” is a **syncretic or pseudo-historical claim**, **not supported by theology, history, or scholarship**. Christianity and Zoroastrianism are distinct religions with different origins, deities, cosmologies, and goals.

---

### 1. **Cosmic Role & Theological System**

- **Ahura Mazda**  
  - Supreme creator god in **Zoroastrianism**.  
  - Exists in a **dualistic** system: good (Ahura Mazda) vs. evil (Angra Mainyu / Ahriman).  
  - The universe is a battleground of moral forces. Humans help Ahura Mazda by choosing good.

- **Abba (God the Father in Christianity)**  
  - A term of **intimacy and relational closeness** meaning “Father” in Aramaic.  
  - Part of a **monotheistic and trinitarian** theology: Father, Son (Jesus), and Holy Spirit are one God in three persons.  
  - There is **no equal evil being**; Satan is a created being and ultimately subordinate to God.

---

### 2. **Relationship to Humans**

- **Ahura Mazda**  
  - Distant, cosmic principle of truth and order (**asha**).  
  - Humans align with him by following good thoughts, words, and deeds.  
  - Does not engage in personal, covenantal relationships like the God of Abraham.

- **Abba**  
  - Invoked by Jesus to express a **personal, loving, and intimate relationship** between God and his followers.  
  - Central to the **New Covenant**, where God enters into a father-child relationship with believers.

---

### 3. **Religious Context**

- **Ahura Mazda**  
  - Originates in **ancient Persian religion**, with possible Indo-Iranian roots.  
  - Worship includes fire temples and rituals of purification.  
  - Texts: **Avesta**.

- **Abba (God the Father)**  
  - Rooted in **Second Temple Judaism**, carried into **Christian theology**.  
  - Worship involves prayer, communion, baptism, and personal devotion.  
  - Texts: **Hebrew Bible + New Testament**.

---

### 4. **Moral Universe**

- **Ahura Mazda**  
  - Exists in a **cosmic dualism**: good vs evil are co-eternal and in constant conflict.  
  - Humans are moral agents in this cosmic war.

- **Abba**  
  - Evil is **not co-eternal**; it’s a rebellion within creation.  
  - God is sovereign and will ultimately defeat evil entirely.
