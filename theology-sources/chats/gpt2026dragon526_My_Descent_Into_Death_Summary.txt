*My Descent Into Death: A Second Chance at Life* is a memoir by Howard Storm, originally published in 2005. In the book, Storm recounts a profound near-death experience (NDE) he had in 1985 when he was a 38-year-old art professor and atheist. While on a trip to Paris, he suffered a perforated stomach and was rushed to the hospital. During the time he was unconscious and near death, he describes having a transformative spiritual experience that radically altered his outlook on life, death, and the divine.

### Key Themes of the Book:
1. **Near-Death Experience (NDE):**  
   Storm details leaving his body and entering a dark, eerie dimension where he encountered malevolent entities. This "descent" into what he describes as a hellish realm sets the tone for the first part of his experience.

2. **Encounter with Divine Love:**  
   After calling out for help in desperation, Storm was rescued by beings of light, whom he identifies as angels or messengers of God. He describes being enveloped by an overwhelming sense of love and peace. This encounter leads to a conversation with Jesus Christ, where he receives answers to many existential and spiritual questions.

3. **Visions of the Afterlife and the Future:**  
   Storm claims to have been shown visions of Heaven and Earth, including glimpses of humanity's potential futures based on collective choices. These visions shaped his understanding of morality, free will, and the consequences of human actions.

4. **Personal Transformation:**  
   Upon recovering from his medical crisis, Storm's life changed dramatically. He abandoned his atheism, became a Christian pastor, and devoted himself to sharing his experience and helping others explore spirituality and faith.

5. **Questions About Life and Purpose:**  
   The book delves into larger existential questions, such as the nature of God, the purpose of human existence, and what happens after death. Storm also shares insights into how people can live more meaningful, loving lives.

### Impact and Reception:
*My Descent Into Death* has been praised for its sincerity and profound exploration of spiritual themes. It resonates deeply with readers interested in NDEs, spirituality, and personal transformation. Critics, however, sometimes question the verifiability of NDEs or view Storm’s account through the lens of personal belief systems. 

For many, Storm’s story offers hope, spiritual insight, and encouragement to reflect on life’s deeper meanings. It continues to be a popular book among those exploring faith, the afterlife, and personal redemption.
