- **Monotheistic or Panentheistic:**  
  Hermetic texts generally teach that the supreme reality (God, The One, or Nous) transcends yet also permeates the cosmos. The universe is understood as a unified living being—a manifestation of the divine Mind.
  
- **Gnosis as Salvation:**  
  Spiritual liberation through acquiring experiential knowledge (**gnosis**) of divine reality, achieved through contemplation, meditation, and philosophical insight.

- **Continuous Creation:**  
  Creation is ongoing; the cosmos continually emanates from divine thought rather than being a singular historical event.

- **Human Divinity (Theosis):**

  Hermes acts as teacher and initiator, guiding followers toward gnosis, but he is never presented as divine in the same sense as Christ (i.e., no incarnation, crucifixion, or resurrection).
