## A. The Gold‑Standard Equivalence & Provenance (GSEP) pipeline

**Goal.** Decide, for two corpora \(A,B\) (e.g., Mesopotamian vs. Hebrew narratives), whether there is (i) *definitional/operational equivalence* at the presentation level, (ii) a **directed genealogic link** (A→B), and (iii) a decisive rejection of independence/co‑development—*with auditable math and known error rates*.

**Inputs.** Digitized sources + citations; domain profiles (mythemes/rituals/law/cosmology); thresholds.

**Outputs.**  
• Constant‑size translators \(F:A\!\to\!B,\,G:B\!\to\!A\) (rewrite rules + sizes in bits).  
• Matched **invariants** (atomic + cluster meta‑invariant).  
• **Directionality** scores (translator asymmetry, trace order, Leitfehler, compression asymmetry).  
• Formal **bisimulation** report on labeled derivations.  
• Optional categorical check where appropriate (math/code domains).  
• Evidence Calculus (EC) totals \(S_{\text{dep/ind}}, S_{\text{dep/cod}}\) with confidence & ablations.  
• Chain‑of‑custody PDF + machine‑readable JSON.
