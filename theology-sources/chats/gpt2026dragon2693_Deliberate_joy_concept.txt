## “The Bowl Practice” — a 10‑minute daily protocol
Use your spinning-bowl image and make it actionable.

**Morning (4 min) – Spin Up**
1. **Three breaths + smile** (tell the face first; the body cues the mind).
2. **Gratitude ramp (90s):** speak 8–10 specifics out loud.
3. **Best‑possible‑self (90s):** see/feel one scene where your prayer/ask is already true. Let the *feeling* lead.

**Midday (3 min total) – Micro‑resets**
- Set 3 alarms titled “Lighten the bowl.” Each time: one stretch, one slow inhale, one sentence of *Active Trust* (e.g., “I’m cared for; I’m guided; I’m ready.”)

**Evening (3 min) – Spin Down**
- Journal 3 bullets:
  - **Lift:** What brightened the bowl?
  - **Load:** What weighed it down?
  - **Let:** One thing you release, one thing you’ll do tomorrow to lighten.

**Boundary cue for interactions**
- Before tough conversations: *“Light bowl, soft jaw, warm eyes.”*  
- After: 30‑second shake, breath, and one kind label for yourself.

Track daily (1–10): Joy, Clarity, Courage. After 7 days, note changes in mood, opportunities noticed, and the tone of your interactions.
