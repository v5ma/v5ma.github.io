# Leo's Guild - Resonance v0.8.0

The next sound-led expansion of the existing game at /leonardos-guild/. The same town, story, 49 multi-level households, two connected traversal layers, earlier commissions, Cycle Works, Lantern Hours, vehicles and save namespace remain. Publication is established by the release PR's merged source, Pages deployment and public-byte receipt, not by this document alone.

## Sound and music

Five original 64-bar compositions: Morning in Vinci, The Artisans' Round, Workshops by Lamplight, The Water Beneath and Across the Copper Roofs. Lute-like plucked strings, dulcimer, flute, bowed strings, bass, bells and frame drums are synthesized from authored note arrangements and harmonic models, not downloaded commercial soundtrack samples. Their 64-bar structures include introductions, themes, answers, sparse bridges and reprises. The score follows outdoor districts, interiors, night, underground rooms and nearby visible rivals, with hysteresis and two-stream crossfades. Music can instead stay on a chosen piece or be disabled independently.

Fifty locally hosted encoded files include five music tracks, five environmental/vehicle loops, three variants each of stone, timber and gravel footsteps, plus action, combat, traversal, UI and reward cues. Footsteps follow actual travel distance and the current surface. Vehicle wheels and chain layers follow movement; no persistent triangle-wave engine is left running. Water, wind, room resonance, clock bells, birds, doors, mounting, jumping, landing, letters, purchases, repairs, staff windups, impacts, bracing, sling shots, reloads and Lantern powers have contextual sounds. Important warnings have optional visual captions and optional controller vibration.

Master, music, effects and environment sliders are independent. Full, balanced and gentler night dynamics are available. Mono listening centers spatial sound effects. Captions are not disabled by muting. Effects are bounded to 24 voices; score playback uses at most two streams; five ambient buffers are shared. Pause ducks the music/environment while retaining menu sounds. Hidden tabs suspend audio. Source and provenance are in scripts/render-resonance-audio.py and assets/resonance/manifest.json and LICENSE.txt. There are no remote runtime audio services or API keys.

Some browsers do not treat Gamepad API activity as trusted audio activation. In that case an unobtrusive message asks for one Enter key press. Menus and gameplay never wait for permission, and no mouse or browser alert is required. This is not a claim to bypass autoplay restrictions.

## Contextual console profile

Left stick moves relative to the camera on foot; right stick independently looks. A jumps, or uses a nearby authorized staircase/hatch. Repeated A taps extend sprint; left-stick click also toggles sprint, so repeated tapping is optional. B dodges. Y mounts or dismounts.

LT aims, bringing the view closer over the shoulder. Optional assistance selects only a visible living rival on the same playable layer and inside the aim cone; walls and locked gates obstruct it. RT uses the equipped tool. Hold LB to open the equipment wheel and slow simulation to 18 percent. Right stick selects a category; D-pad left/right changes its variant; release LB or press A to equip, or B to cancel. Right-stick selection is latched until neutral to prevent a camera jump on closing.

Staff: balanced or heavier slower grip, using the original close-range attack. Sling: six ready pellets and a finite reserve, with firm or softer stunning pellets. X reloads while aiming the sling. Holding X preserves nearby interaction access in that context; otherwise X interacts normally. Reload takes 1.35 seconds and consumes reserve pellets. Leonardo's existing workshop refill and paid market supplies replenish them. Projectiles travel, collide with surfaces, cannot pass between house layers and do not target residents. Rival rewards remain one-time. Letters preserve the original delivery reducer and left/right choice. The Lantern still has to be earned through the existing story; choosing its wheel slice does not grant it.

RB takes cover beside a real nearby street obstacle or leaves cover. Staff aiming also braces. The blue bar is guard reserve, not newly invented body armor. Sustained bracing exhausts it; releasing the defense allows recovery. Green is vitality and yellow is the existing focus resource. Both stick clicks activate the selected discipline for 40 focus with a cooldown. Courier gives faster footwork, Warden gives temporary bracing/recovery, and Artificer gives mechanism scanning and faster reload. Hold D-pad down to choose a discipline in a safe place; these are three abilities for the same apprentice, not separate protagonists.

D-pad up opens Guild Dispatch: Nearby, Adventures, Notebook, Map, Equipment, Music, Audio/controls and Quick save. These invoke real existing activities and menus. View opens the map; Menu pauses. D-pad down briefly opens the notebook. Hold D-pad left for the music wheel. A brief left press uses the earned Lantern on foot or toggles the visible lamp while riding. D-pad right inspects and can be held to operate mechanisms. Right-stick click recenters the camera.

In vehicles, RT accelerates, LT brakes and reverses, RB handbrakes, A ducks the rider, B retains a bicycle hop, and left-stick click sounds the bell. Y returns to on-foot controls. Original bicycle/carriage physics remain; this release does not claim a full stunt/drift simulator or damage-proof crouching.

Every dialog retains D-pad or left-stick navigation, A activation, B back, LB/RB tabs and right-stick scrolling. Settings, cancellation and text entry avoid native blocking dialogs. Audio/controls is reachable from the title, pause screen and dispatch. Select Classic Open Doors to retain the earlier mapping. Keyboard and touch controls remain; U opens dispatch and Tab opens equipment. New touch Use tool and Reload buttons accompany the existing controls. The existing map shows nearby visible rivals and their heading cues, not an invented police-star system.

## Saves, acceptance and remaining scope

The old svgn.leonardos-guild.v1 key and outer save version 2 remain. The bounded resonance record adds only equipment selection, variants, discipline, ammunition and lamp preference. Old saves initialize this record without resetting quests, money or house progress. Audio/controller settings have separate bounded local preferences. Transient projectiles, cover, active abilities and reload timers do not survive into an invalid resumed location.

All 154 local model tests passed, including 23 new equipment/audio-data tests. Fifty actual packaged audio files passed decoding, hash, duration, level and loop-boundary checks. These are not subjective listening or native gameplay claims. tests/resonance-browser.py uses fresh storage, actual WebGL/HTTP, a virtual standard Gamepad API device and one explicitly declared trusted Enter press for audio. It measures actual audio graph output and exercises the controller, wheels, reloads, settings, saves and reconnect. The retained Open Doors journey selects Classic through the visible controller UI before its original route. Other old browser regressions remain required. Native acceptance runs against ordinary committed source and preserves reports, screenshots and hashes. See the release PR for actual outcomes and public verification.

No physical Xbox/Bluetooth, speaker, mobile-performance, commercial animation or subjective soundtrack certification is claimed. Full systemic watch pursuits, armor progression, separate playable protagonists, property ownership, stock trading, a modern phone/browser and new piloted vehicles remain future work. Their interfaces are not faked in this release. The user's console reference is retained as a longer-term direction, adapted to the Renaissance setting and existing game.
