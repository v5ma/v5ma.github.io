This is the first authored replacement opening in the existing Leonardo's Guild, not a new game and not completion of the whole city redesign. New sessions start on Leonardo's porch. Existing saves remain in their older Vinci campaign until the player deliberately enters the Quarter near the workshop at (-5,-6). Dismount, stop and press X. The departure station at (-24,-14) returns to those retained districts. The same houses, original quests, Cinder Hollow and Stillwater content remain supported there.

The Missing Commission is stranded on a shared goods gallery. The cooperative route reaches Marta's workbench, where the clue specifies a 2:1 drive ratio. Wrong ratios consume nothing. The correct repair opens the loading stairs. The independent upper route goes through Ilaria's dye workroom, loft, drying roof and timber bridge. The independent hydraulic route drains the service channel from a dry overlook, follows its actual ramp, and climbs the rear service stairs from the maintenance cellar. None of these routes requires completion of another route's hidden trigger. The station at the raised gallery requires the apprentice's actual proximity, correct floor, grounded feet and line of sight.

The far-side gallery descent reveals the familiar bell bracket and latch. Opening it creates a permanent return beside Leonardo's workshop. Returning the parcel pays 60 florins and 120 experience once. Leonardo then offers a short spindle delivery from Marta's bench to Ilaria's finishing loft, with 15 florins and 40 experience paid once. The opened arch remains useful on this second visit. There is no schedule-based failure, mandatory combat, consumable puzzle cost or new compulsory audio stream.

Twenty-four authored floor/ramp surfaces define the architecture and collision together. Three residents follow short explicit work routes rather than an unlimited crowd simulation. Named stations provide cooperation without requiring a wait for a hidden routine. The water visibly changes over simulation time; it is draining/wading, not swimming. Raised floors, dry control positions and recovery prevent the hydraulic puzzle from trapping a player. Static structural detail is instanced; no reference-game art is imported.

Console Xbox controls retain left-stick movement, right-stick camera, A jump, X interaction or aimed-sling reload, Y ride/dressing in the older regions, LT/RT aim/use, B dodge/back, and LB tap-to-previous-tool or hold-to-wheel. The earlier refinement's slope contacts, turn-in-place steps, timestamp-safe tap/hold, XR wheel stick control, direct aimed reload and release/neutral latches are recovered. Keyboard E/I/X interacts in the Quarter, Space jumps, G opens nearby information, M maps, and Escape returns. Touch action buttons remain. None of the new view settings takes over a frequent gameplay binding.

Select a presentation from the title or pause screen, then activate Enter selected XR view. Third-person diorama VR and diorama AR display the actual same Quarter geometry for each eye, not the theatre render texture. First-person VR uses the same world at metric scale with a tracked eye origin independent of procedural gait bob. Left stick moves, right stick snap-turns in first person, and head motion alone does not move the apprentice. Excessive leaning or wall clipping blocks movement and obscures the view until the head returns. This is smooth virtual walking with snap turns, not a claim of physical room-scale collision safety. Older regions and vehicles retain the labeled theatre fallback.

The XR side panel's View / diorama entry and the exhibit handle open the existing controller/hand-operable view dialog. The three aperture states are top and front open, top open/front closed, and front open/top closed. Closing the last open face opens the other atomically. Invalid preferences return to the both-open state. The exhibit front belongs to its placement, not to the latest glance. The miniature samples movement direction at stick engagement and retains it until the stick is neutral. Scale, rotation and placement release input and never change physical interaction ranges or player coordinates.

The miniature can be raised, lowered, moved closer/farther, scaled within a bounded range and rotated deliberately. AR uses a separate immersive-ar request and transparent environment blending. Where optional surface hit testing is available, Place on detected surface is an explicit action; otherwise manual placement remains labeled. Room mesh occlusion, furniture collision and persistent room anchors are not implemented. No physical-room pose is persisted or uploaded. Hand pinch and controller rays operate the same menu handlers, with release-to-arm and tracking/visibility-loss safety. AR does not silently switch to first-person VR.

Saves retain svgn.leonardos-guild.v1 and outer version 2. The additive quarter record stores only validated accomplishments and district presence; reload arrives safely on the porch. Transient actor poses, water fraction, foot contacts, XR origin and physical room data are not serialized. A stable legacy delivery identity registry decouples past deliveries from future physical layout replacements. Paid rewards cannot repeat, and leaving/recovery retains original inventory, progression and parked vehicles. This first slice retains an explicit supported legacy continuation rather than claiming every old activity has been relocated.
