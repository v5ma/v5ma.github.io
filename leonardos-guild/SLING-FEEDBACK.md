Choose the Artisan's sling through the existing equipment wheel. The unchanged normal Xbox controls are LT aim and RT fire; while aimed X reloads, with held interaction unchanged. The fixed tracked-XR controls remain left trigger aim, right trigger fire and aimed right B reload. Tracked left grip opens the existing tools wheel. No new menu action, automatic aiming mode, free ammunition, forced practice encounter or keyboard remap is added.

Hold Aim to see a short dashed world-space arrow. Its direction is resolved by the same function used for the next real pellet, including eligible lock-on targets. It ends within 4 game metres, stopping before nearby static solid samples. It is a direction indicator, not maximum range, a ballistic arc, an aim-at-headset/controller-ray promise, or confirmation that an enemy will be hit. Current aim steering remains the game's camera/character-relative system. Fixing physical controller-to-weapon alignment remains a separate task, not something this overlay silently claims to solve.

Bright coral pellets and short amber trails show actual simulation positions. Trails connect the previous fixed-step position to the current position; they never extend ahead through an obstruction. A brief cross marks solid contact; a diamond is created only when the existing damage-target path resolves a real hit. Solid-contact marks use the last valid sampled pellet position, not a claimed exact triangle intersection. Their two crossed vertical planes remain visible from several viewpoints. All feedback respects depth testing, so it is not an x-ray overlay through architecture. The guide becomes muted when the pouch is empty or reloading and disappears when Aim is released, another tool is selected or the character is riding. The existing readout still gives exact ammunition.

Implementation and bounded ownership.

sling-feedback.mjs is original Three r177 geometry, shared by the existing town and frontier renderers; it does not replace any Currentworks module. It adds no lights, textures, render targets, independent timers, input, audio or networking. Three fixed instanced meshes represent guide segments, at most 12 pellets and at most 12 step trails. Two reused line buffers represent at most 8 recent impacts. Each active region uses at most 5 feedback draws before normal scene culling. Inactive regional roots remain controlled by the existing scene visibility code. The optional Quarter retains its existing projectile presentation in this pass.

The core's shot damage, velocity, collision, target eligibility, cooldown, reload cost, rewards, movement and firing bindings are unchanged. The direction expression is extracted without altering its rules. The only extra simulation state is prior projectile positions and a bounded impact ledger. Impacts expire in 24 simulation steps, freeze when the simulation pauses, and are discarded on relevant region/floor/room transitions or workshop restock. They are never serialized: the outer save remains version 2 at svgn.leonardos-guild.v1. Loading a save starts with no transient projectiles or marks. No local items become tradable online wealth.
