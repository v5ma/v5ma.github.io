How to play

Xbox: left stick moves, right stick looks. A jumps or climbs; repeated A taps sprint. LT aims, RT uses the selected tool. Tap LB for the previous tool; hold LB for equipment; RB takes cover. X interacts, or reloads while aiming the sling. Y mounts. In vehicles RT accelerates, LT brakes/reverses and RB handbrakes. Up opens guild dispatch. Hold left for music, down for disciplines. Both stick clicks activate your ability. Every dialog supports B to close. Audio and controller settings include the original Classic mapping.

On touch screens: left thumb joystick moves, a second finger drags the camera. Boost, Brake and action buttons are below. Settings adjusts graphics and camera without restarting.

YOUR WAY TO PLAY

Controls & graphics

Left thumb moves and steers. Drag the world with another finger to look. Release the stick to coast; hold Brake to stop. Use and Letter buttons work while steering.

Currentworks water animates the existing Stillwater basin. Still mode removes decorative waves and wind, without stopping the hydraulic puzzle. AR opacity changes water only.

Sling sight: hold your existing Aim control for a short direction arrow. Bright pellets and trails show actual shots; a cross marks solid contact and a diamond confirms a hit. The arrow is not a range or damage guarantee. Town residents remain protected.

Graphics change immediately, without restarting your journey. Keyboard controls remain available.
