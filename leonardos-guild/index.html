How to play

On touch screens: left thumb joystick moves, a second finger drags the camera. Boost, Brake and action buttons are below. Settings adjusts graphics and camera without restarting.

W S Move / brake / reverse
A D Steer · Space Hop / handbrake
Q C Throw left / right
F Dismount / enter nearby ride
X Inspect · Hold H Operate / recover folio
J Staff · Hold K Brace
B Trade at the market
Shift Sprint / pedal harder
M Map · P Pause

YOUR WAY TO PLAY

Controls & graphics

Left thumb moves and steers. Drag the world with another finger to look. Release the stick to coast; hold Brake to stop. Use and Letter buttons work while steering.

Graphics change immediately, without restarting your journey. Keyboard controls remain available.
