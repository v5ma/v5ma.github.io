This grows the same Leonardo city, not a new game. Original Stolen Folio, nine RPG side commissions, joystick, settings, six interiors, basements, Lantern, bicycles and saved progress remain. ## What the encounters do

Repair a cart, crossing bell and cask by following marked mechanisms. Restore a booklet or calibrate a workshop fitting. Carry distinct packets and gather ingredients through existing streets. Hear a musical refrain or read its written notes, then play the controls. Collect evidence before resolving a misread address without accusing an innocent apprentice. Learn a restorative recipe, find Lantern inscriptions and choose a palette for Isabella's exhibition after her original commission. An existing adult partner enables a short optional garden outing; this is not full companion AI or a dating simulation.

These encounters award once, retain completion on reload and record results in Field Notes. Mistakes cost nothing; leaving a panel cancels its current sequence, not completed work. Floor, proximity, walking/stopping, gate and original-quest prerequisites are checked on every action. Courier packets are separate from the original letter inventory and do not overwrite its mission gates. Twenty-two counts named complete encounters, not intermediate markers. Many are deliberately short; the longer 30-50 activity roadmap is not yet finished.

Some outcomes are visible: repaired carts straighten, gathered produce leaves pickup spots, a community table appears, Nero's bowl fills, and the exhibition uses the chosen palette. Others are remembered notes. Recurring autonomous incidents, schedules and systemic policing are not claimed.

## Actual in-game art

32 curated Quaternius CC0 Standard models provide textured modular walls, windows, tile roofs, chimneys, vines, stalls, workbenches, bookcases, bottles, barrels, candles and tools. Paid Pro/Source models are not included. The 49 house positions and usable doors remain. Static facades share material batches and indexed vertices; texture maps are 512px. All 98 packaged art files total 8,990,341 bytes.

Near-detail budgets are 4 Battery, 16 Balanced and 26 Quality facades; distant buildings retain original cheaper shells. Battery uses the imported geometry/color maps with simpler diffuse lighting, a 0.6-pixel-ratio 3D canvas and distant-prop culling. HTML text and touch hit areas stay full resolution. Balanced/Quality retain PBR maps and higher resolution. Recessed infill belongs only behind window modules, not walkable doors. These changes reduce rendering work; they are not a measured physical-phone FPS promise.

ASSET-REGISTER.json records creator, license, original URLs, upstream archives and model hashes, packaged hashes, bounds and modifications. License texts remain beside the files. The local glTF loader matches the existing Three.js r177, with only relative import changes. No paid content or runtime third-party asset fetch is required. The original procedural scene remains playable if detailed art fails to load. `?art=baseline` is an explicit comparison switch, not the default.

Characters still use the existing procedural models. Professional character animation, further connected basements, piloted flight, schedules and multiplayer remain future work. The attached art advice concerned Aether Reach; this pass transfers its art-production approach to Renaissance Vinci without modifying Aether Reach.
