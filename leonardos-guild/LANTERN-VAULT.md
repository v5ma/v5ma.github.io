Lantern Vault survey / v0.16.0 / September 22, 2026.

This is a playable original survey wing in Cinder Hollow, east of Gate Camp. It advances the owner's approved Vinci-artisan/frontier direction and the puzzle/tool proposals in DUNGEON-ADAPTATION.md. It does not replace Vinci, Cinder Hollow, The Drowned Workshop, the optional Quarter, or Vesperfall. No private NerveGear manuscript, franchise creature, artwork or protected dungeon layout is imported.

Start with Play Lantern Vault survey / keep my adventure on the ordinary title screen. The address ?chapter=lantern-vault selects this objective before any of the existing screen, first-person VR/AR or diorama VR/AR entries. It does not move the actor, complete the Lantern Road charter or reset a save. Dismount, meet Leonardo beside the Guild charter, and borrow the inspection lens and counterweight for free. The original expedition gate opens Cinder Hollow; follow the survey marker east from Gate Camp. Players already exploring Cinder Hollow can borrow the same tools at the survey bench. Neither route requires a purchase, class, companion, enemy kill or completion of another chapter.

Use G or I on keyboard, the existing Xbox interaction action (normally X), tracked right B, or the touch interaction controls beside a marked instrument. Objects use the same authoritative proximity and wall checks. Mechanisms act immediately without silently opening a pause. The ordinary pause desk has Lantern Vault / tools and field notes. Inventory links to that desk and displays loan locations alongside the original equipment and trade satchel. Select a tool there, then close the desk and interact in the world. An installed tool can be retrieved at its socket, never by pretending that a second copy appeared in the inventory.

The eight connected spaces are Survey Porch, Shutter Hall, Receiver Hall, Records Chamber, Inscription Walk, Caretaker Walk, Service Approach and Maintenance Return. This first implementation is a roofless, single-elevation ruin on the existing terrain. It is not yet the proposed multilevel gallery, a randomized dungeon, a new guardian encounter or a finished art-production environment. The original badlands encounters remain elsewhere; occupied Vinci and its cellars remain safe.

The northern walk gives information. Carry the inspection lens to the mineral-covered plate to read the optical instruction. A separate caretaker account explains that the workshop served families who could not pay and mentions the maintenance mechanism. These notes are optional; neither is an invisible mandatory waypoint.

The central route uses visible cause and effect. Fit the inspection lens to focus the light, install the one counterweight at the shutter, and rotate the reflector toward the receiver. The beam stops at the closed shutter, changes direction with the reflector, and lights the receiver when the optical gate opens. Wrong equipment explains what is missing and changes nothing. Removing a component reverses the mechanism until the inside return latch has been opened.

The southern route uses the same counterweight at the maintenance socket instead. It opens a different gate and reaches the Records Chamber without solving the optical puzzle or reading either clue. This is a practical route choice, not a second color of the same key. The weight cannot occupy both sockets simultaneously.

Recover the civic optical plans in the Records Chamber. The inside latch permanently opens both return gates and reconnects the workshop to the entry. Return through the original Gate Camp transition, then give the plans to Leonardo. The report awards 40 florins once and restores a visible optical reading instrument beside the Map House. The completed report, bound plans, tool locations and return latch survive normal reload. Reload uses the original safe-town recovery rather than restoring a possibly obstructed actor pose.

The loaned inspection lens lives in vault.lens, not road.items.lens, which remains the separate saleable polished trade lens. Required survey tools and plans have no merchant sell action. The two earlier NPC shops, review/cancel flow and local receipts remain intact. This is still a solo browser-local economy; no authenticated multiplayer, secure player barter, shared reward import or cross-game item transfer is claimed.

Implementation and preservation.

vault-data.mjs owns layout lantern-vault-1, shared walls/gates, stations and route names. The existing frontier collision and camera use the new masonry; the original nineteen trail segments remain clear. The eight-space map is explicitly a survey plan, not a fabricated record that claims the player explored every room. Physical head collision uses the existing complete-scene adapter and the same movement collision. Display transforms never change actor reach or quest ownership.

vault-core.mjs validates a small additive vault record inside svgn.leonardos-guild.v1, outer version 2. Actions build a candidate, revalidate the actual location/custody, persist through the existing guarded writer, then update memory. Failed writes award and move nothing. Stale or double confirmations cannot replay a completed report. No actor, tracking-origin, temporary beam, room scan or camera transform is stored. Switching objectives keeps existing story progress and both inventories intact.

vault-ui.mjs reuses native dialog handlers and the current Xbox/XR bridge. Back preserves a parent pause, and the same existing actual session-end action leaves XR. vault-art.mjs uses static shared batches and revision-driven instrument updates without new lights, sound streams or render targets. road-art.mjs supplies the persistent civic consequence. Original characters, vehicles, household access, camera improvements, water, audio choices and all Vesperfall runtime files are preserved.

The existing dedicated compositor workflow is extended with a source optical journey and a public maintenance journey. Both exercise real keyboard movement, failed-save recovery, actual tools, shortcut, map, reached-scene XR entry, synthetic hand-selected inventory, actual XR exit, town report and reload. They save screenshots and exact source hashes. Read completed artifacts and the separate release receipt before crediting browser or public acceptance; a submitted job is not a pass.

The prior Lantern Road source browser artifact 10720502469 at commit ee03abf8 completed 26 checks with no captured error. This new work does not erase that evidence or claim it verifies the Vault. Physical Quest freezes, ergonomics, sustained frame time and aiming/audio complaints remain open until actually retested. New survey geometry is deliberately bounded rather than hiding those issues under an engine replacement.
