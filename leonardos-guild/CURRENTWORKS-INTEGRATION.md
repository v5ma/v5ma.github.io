They are stylized authored specimens, not a botanic claim. The other town trees and planted garden remain. Controls and graphics has two saved controls: Still/Flowing water and foliage motion, and AR water opacity cycling through 0, 25, 45, 65, 85 and 100 percent. The values extend the existing separate graphical preference record, never the adventure save. Existing DOM handlers remain accessible through the scene desk and tracked-hand bridge.
