# World Portal correction / guild-portal-20260917

This implements the owner's September 17 correction to the same Leonardo's Guild. The exhibit is a window into the ordinary third-person game, not a bounded map or a separate game. Its previous room-space dimensions and placement preferences remain. The active character or ridden vehicle stays centered, including height changes. The original third-person camera heading rotates the world around that center; head translation, yaw and tilt do not steer the apprentice. Game zoom is separate from physical exhibit scale.

The complete live scene is presented: Waterwheel Quarter, supported older Vinci households/vehicles, and Cinder Hollow/Stillwater regions use the same temporary scene adapter. No world, actor, NPC, quest or physics copy is created. This continuously presents the existing world as it moves; it is not infinite terrain or network asset streaming. Existing authored region transitions remain ordinary gameplay.

A per-fragment camera-ray/box aperture provides stencil-style silhouette masking without requiring a stencil attachment from the XR compositor. Rays missing the display and geometry before its entry surface are discarded. Scenery beyond its rear and side faces is deliberately retained. Each actual eye supplies its own view/camera matrices. The frame is thin, far-side glass is lightly tinted, and enclosure faces between either eye and the character are fully transparent for that eye. Automatic visibility takes precedence over the retained top/front presets; closing both remains impossible. These are display faces, not in-game collision walls.

The shader technique is adapted from the owner's released Dino Atlas implementation, dino-atlas/diorama-portal.js blob 03dd03ba56795918905d3dec264843ababb3d5fe. It is kept locally with Leonardo's bundled Three version; no sibling runtime, vendor library, asset, license or private content is changed. Existing shader hooks, sprites and instancing are retained. Original parents, transforms, visibility and character fade are restored in finally before the next physics/IK update. Presentation materials are inactive outside the portal. New effects are included when adopted on subsequent frames.

## Rectangle diagnosis and correction

The reviewed guild-xr source had two permanent rectangular surfaces: a 1.10 by 1.65 side panel and a 2.85 by .7125 controls toolbar, relocated close to the viewer in spatial mode. Head parallax could move these surfaces across the observed game. The fixed miniature also had opaque double-sided enclosure walls. Those are identified code-level obstruction sources; without the user's headset recording, this is not a claim that every observed rectangle has been physically reproduced.

Spatial gameplay now hides both rectangular controls by default. Select the small gold controls sphere or the existing exhibit handle with trigger/pinch to show or hide them deliberately. Real dialogs show the side panel; returning to play hides it. Direct Xbox and tracked-controller gameplay buttons remain available without opening a menu. Hidden panels are excluded from ray tests so they cannot steal trigger actions. Sky/cloud billboards and monoscopic water-refraction captures are excluded from spatial rendering; the same water simulation and procedural surface remain. The portal's own sky is masked and first-person AR uses no opaque background.

## First-person AR

Choose First-person AR / passthrough at the title or pause screen before entering. It requests an actual immersive-ar session separately from immersive-vr and requires non-opaque environment blending. The same metric-scale game geometry overlays passthrough; no browser camera feed is captured or uploaded. Tracked viewing, ordinary movement, snap turning, hands/UI and Xbox fallback use the existing game. The avatar is hidden only for the rendering pass. Excessive head displacement or in-game wall clipping stops movement; in AR the virtual world hides to reveal passthrough rather than surrounding the user with a dark sphere.

This is not a physical room mesh, furniture detector, navigation guarantee or environment-occlusion system. Use a clear stationary play space. No persistent room pose is saved. Hand/controller and visual comfort still need real Quest review. Third-person AR placement continues to offer optional hit testing with a labeled manual fallback; first-person AR never silently turns a VR session into AR.
