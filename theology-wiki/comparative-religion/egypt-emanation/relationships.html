Structural comparison

Egyptian judgment vignettes / Plato's Myth of Er

Historical scope: Egyptian witnesses and fourth-century BCE Greek philosophical writing

Available evidence: Both relate conduct to a consequential posthumous evaluation, but their actors and narrative procedures differ.

What remains missing: A particular source or transmission witness showing that Er's story derives from a specific Egyptian judgment text.

Alternative account: A wider repertoire, mediated exchange or convergence could explain some of the shared structure.
