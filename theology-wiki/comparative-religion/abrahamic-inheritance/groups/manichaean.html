Manichaeism

Relationship to Abraham, Israel or covenant

Manichaeism places Jesus within a wider prophetic history that also incorporates figures such as Buddha and Zoroaster, with Mani presenting a culminating revelation.

Claim type: Progressive / universal prophetic lineage

This is a successor-revelation claim, not an ethnic claim to be Israel.

What this page represents

The two principles, rescue of light and distinct community obligations need to be studied together. Invoking Jesus, Buddha and Zoroaster does not make their teachings interchangeable.

Wiki research status: Comparative study.
