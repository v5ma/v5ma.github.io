Relationship to Abraham, Israel or covenant

Karaite Judaism claims Jewish and Israelite continuity while rejecting rabbinic Oral Law as binding authority.

Claim type: Jewish / Israelite covenant continuity

Its disagreement is over the authoritative form of interpretation, not a rejection of Abrahamic-Israelite identity.

What this page represents

Rejection of rabbinic Oral Law as binding does not entail the absence of commentary, legal interpretation or inherited practice. The community's own reading-room catalogue demonstrates those genres.
