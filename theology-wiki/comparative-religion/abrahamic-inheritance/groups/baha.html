Relationship to Abraham, Israel or covenant

The Baha'i Faith places Abraham within a progressive history of divine revelation that continues through later Manifestations of God.

Claim type: Progressive / universal revelation

This universalizes inheritance rather than claiming that one ethnic community alone replaces Israel.

What this page represents

The community names Buddha and Zoroaster within progressive revelation, alongside other divine educators. This is its theology of religious relationship, not evidence that all earlier figures taught one identical system.
