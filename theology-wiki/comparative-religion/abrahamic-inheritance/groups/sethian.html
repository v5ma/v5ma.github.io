Relationship to Abraham, Israel or covenant

Sethian writings reuse biblical figures and Jewish-Christian materials but do not straightforwardly claim to be the ethnic or covenantal Israel of Abraham.

Claim type: Comparative / Abrahamic textual genealogy

Their importance here is genealogical and interpretive, not a simple chosen-people claim.

What this page represents

A scholarly grouping includes writings with distinctive divine genealogies and a lower ruler. A common classification does not establish one fixed institution or two independent primordial creators in every tractate.
