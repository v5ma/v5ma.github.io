Relationship to Abraham, Israel or covenant

Mandaeans share a scriptural and historical environment with Jewish, Christian and Baptist figures while preserving a strongly distinct religious identity.

Claim type: Abrahamic genealogy but not an Abrahamic inheritance claim

Their tradition is not accurately summarized as 'we are the true heirs of Abraham'; Abraham and several other Abrahamic figures can be treated critically.

What this page represents

A living tradition with distinctive scriptures, rites and community self-understanding. Its Book of John is not a canonical Christian Gospel, and Miriai's narrative requires its own textual treatment.

Wiki research status: Comparative study.
