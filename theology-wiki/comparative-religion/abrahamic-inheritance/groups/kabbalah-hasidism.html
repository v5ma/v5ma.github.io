Relationship to Abraham, Israel or covenant

Kabbalistic and Hasidic traditions operate within Jewish covenantal identity while offering distinctive mystical interpretations of divine life, Torah and the role of the righteous.

Claim type: Jewish covenant continuity

They do not constitute a separate ethnic Israel from Judaism; their continuity claim is nested inside Jewish peoplehood and covenant.

What this page represents

Tanya supplies one account of divine vitality, the other side and repentance. It is not evidence that all Jewish mystics share one cosmology or now accept Christian doctrines.
