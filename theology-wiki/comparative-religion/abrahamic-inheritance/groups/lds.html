Relationship to Abraham, Israel or covenant

Latter-day Saint teaching gives the Abrahamic covenant and the gathering of Israel a prominent role, with participation understood through descent, adoption and covenant.

Claim type: Restored Israel / Abrahamic covenant

This is a particularly explicit restorationist form of Israel language within Christianity.

What this page represents

The church's own introduction affirms additional scripture and a Godhead account distinct from Nicene consubstantiality. Those claims should be represented in their own terms rather than called a surviving ancient Gnostic sect.
