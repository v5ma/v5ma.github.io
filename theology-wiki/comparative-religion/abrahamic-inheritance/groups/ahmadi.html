Claim type: Islamic Abrahamic restoration with later messianic claim

Its Muslim self-identification and other Muslim authorities' disagreements should both be represented without the Wiki deciding a reader's faith.

What this page represents

The community traces its foundation to Mirza Ghulam Ahmad in Qadian in 1889, in British India. Its prophetic and messianic claims and its Muslim self-identification need to be distinguished from other authorities' assessments.
