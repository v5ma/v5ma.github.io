Basilidian traditions belong to the contested early-Christian field and are relevant as rival claims about authentic revelation.

Claim type: Comparative / Christian esoteric inheritance

Most surviving descriptions come through opponents, so a confident reconstruction of their Abrahamic self-understanding requires source recovery.

What this page represents

Irenaeus I.24 reports a system associated with Basilides, but it remains an opponent's account. It cannot serve as an unquestioned transcript of all Basilidian belief and conduct.
