The Druze tradition developed historically from an Ismaili environment and therefore belongs in the wider Abrahamic genealogy while developing a distinct religious identity.

Claim type: Abrahamic genealogy but not a simple chosen-people claim

It should not be forced into the formula 'we are the true Israel' unless a specific source makes that claim.

What this page represents

The Institute of Ismaili Studies lecture description locates formative developments in an eleventh-century Fatimid setting while examining a distinctive tradition. It does not authenticate the supplied list of secret sages.
