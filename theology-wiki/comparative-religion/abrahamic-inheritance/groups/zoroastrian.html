Relationship to Abraham, Israel or covenant

Zoroastrian traditions are important to the Wiki's history of apocalyptic and dualistic ideas but do not derive their identity from Abraham's covenant.

Claim type: Comparative / outside Abrahamic inheritance

They are included as a comparative neighbor, not as a claimant to Israelite chosenness.

What this page represents

The relationships among Ahura Mazda and opposing spirits vary across historical texts and interpretations. One equal-two-gods formula is not an adequate description of every period.
