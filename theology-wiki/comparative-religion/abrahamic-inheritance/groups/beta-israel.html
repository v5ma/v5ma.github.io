Relationship to Abraham, Israel or covenant

Beta Israel belongs to the history of Jewish and Israelite identity in Ethiopia and later Israel.

Claim type: Jewish / Israelite covenant continuity

Its history should be studied through its own textual and communal witnesses rather than reduced to one lost-tribe legend.

What this page represents

ANU Museum's Orit exhibit documents a named sacred scroll, eight-book contents and custodial history. It supplies a concrete starting point rather than a story of an unchanging community completely isolated for a fixed millennium.

Wiki research status: Material witness introduction.
