Relationship to Abraham, Israel or covenant

The sectarian Qumran texts portray a faithful covenant community within Israel rather than a later non-Israelite heir to Abraham.

Claim type: Historical covenant / remnant claim

This is a historical remnant claim reconstructed from particular texts, not a living denomination or proof that every Dead Sea Scroll was produced by one group.

What this page represents

The Dead Sea Scrolls contain multiple works and forms of community organization. Particular sectarian texts can be compared with reports about Essenes without making every manuscript the work of one monastic group.
