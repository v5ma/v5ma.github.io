Relationship to Abraham, Israel or covenant

Jehovah's Witnesses interpret Abraham's promised seed through Christ and a distinct understanding of spiritual Israel.

Claim type: Spiritual Israel / Christian inheritance

Their claim is theological rather than an assertion that all members are literal descendants of ancient Israel.

What this page represents

The cited official account rejects the Trinity and describes Jesus as a created Son. Nontrinitarian belief alone does not establish identity with a Jewish, Islamic or ancient Gnostic doctrine.
