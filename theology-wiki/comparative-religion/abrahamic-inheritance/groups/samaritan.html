Relationship to Abraham, Israel or covenant

Israelite Samaritans understand themselves as preserving ancient Israelite identity, Torah, priesthood and the sacred center at Mount Gerizim.

Claim type: Original Israel continuity claim

This is one of the clearest living claims to direct continuity with ancient Israel and competes with Jewish accounts on important points.

What this page represents

The Samaritan museum presents a distinctive five-book Torah and priestly heritage. The community is not adequately described merely as an unusual modern Jewish denomination.
