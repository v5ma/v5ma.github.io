Exact attribution not established

Supplied claim: Maimonides rejected Jesus because an adversarial Satan was wholly foreign to Judaism.

Locate the alleged passage. Guide III.22 interprets Satan and permission; Laws of Kings 11 treats messianic criteria. Neither should be replaced with an unsourced composite objection.

Sources: Maimonides, Guide III.22, M. Friedlander translation / Maimonides, Laws of Kings and Wars 11, translated by Eliyahu Touger.

Distinguish historical and normative claims

Supplied claim: Every later judgment of an earlier doctrine is anachronistic.

A medieval standard cannot exhaust the earlier historical range of beliefs. A later authority can nevertheless apply its own normative criteria; showing historical precedent does not compel doctrinal approval.

Sources: Maimonides, Guide III.22, M. Friedlander translation / Maimonides, Laws of Kings and Wars 11, translated by Eliyahu Touger / Jacques Duchesne-Guillemin, Dead Sea Scrolls, Encyclopaedia Iranica.

Overgeneralized

Supplied claim: All Zoroastrians believe in two equal uncreated gods.

Different Iranian texts and interpretations arrange destructive spirit, beneficent spirit and Ahura Mazda differently. Compare a specified period and source rather than one timeless formula.

Sources: Gherardo Gnoli, Dualism, Encyclopaedia Iranica / Jacques Duchesne-Guillemin, Ahriman, Encyclopaedia Iranica.

Target is an interpretation

Supplied claim: Isaiah 45:7 explicitly and conclusively identifies Zoroastrianism as its target.

The text names Cyrus and asserts divine sovereignty but does not name Zarathustra. Its contrast between peace and calamity should also be distinguished from a claim that God morally approves every evil act.

Sources: Isaiah 45, World English Bible.

Chronological inference unsupported

Supplied claim: Job's narrative setting establishes the starting date of a continuous evolution through Ezekiel to Kabbalah.

Narrative setting, composition, redaction and surviving manuscript dates are distinct. A sequence selected for comparison is not automatically a demonstrated textual genealogy.

Sources: Job 1-2, World English Bible / Israel Museum, Digital Dead Sea Scrolls project.

Corpus and community conflated

Supplied claim: All scrolls were authored by one Essene community and reveal one doctrine.

The collection preserves different works, copies and rules. The 2024 commentary description explicitly discusses multiple settlements and textual forms.

Sources: John J. Collins and James Nati, The Rule of the Association and Related Texts (2024) / Israel Museum, Digital Dead Sea Scrolls project.

Different origin accounts erased

Supplied claim: Every Jewish adversary comes from the same rebellion story.

Job's accuser, the Community Rule's appointed spirits, Enoch's spirits of the giants and Jubilees' permitted Mastema have distinct narrative functions and origins.

Sources: Job 1-2, World English Bible / Jacques Duchesne-Guillemin, Dead Sea Scrolls, Encyclopaedia Iranica / 1 Enoch 15, R. H. Charles translation / Jubilees 10, R. H. Charles translation.

Supplied composite account not substantiated

Supplied claim: Miriai is straightforwardly Jesus's Mandaean mother, born to Mandaean parents and seduced by a planetary spirit.

The consulted scholarly history and community translation response describe a Jewish-born Miriai. Possible literary connections with Mary traditions must be examined separately with exact textual locators.

Sources: Edmondo Lupieri, Mandaeans i. History, Encyclopaedia Iranica / Community response concerning translation of the Mandaean Book of John (2023) / Charles G. Haeberl and James F. McGrath, The Mandaean Book of John: Text and Translation.

Passage and edition not recovered

Supplied claim: The supplied detailed end-time sequence is the single unambiguous teaching of the Ginza Rabba.

A book, section, translation and treatment of variants are required. Soul-passage accounts and the end of the cosmos must not be merged solely because both concern judgment.

Sources: Charles G. Haeberl and James F. McGrath, The Mandaean Book of John: Text and Translation / Edmondo Lupieri, Mandaeans i. History, Encyclopaedia Iranica.

Contradicted by selected primary witnesses

Supplied claim: Esoteric knowledge universally replaces scripture, community and physical ritual.

Qumran interpretation works through scripture. Philip names ritual mysteries; the Hermetic prayer ends with an embrace and meal; Mandaean community accounts affirm embodied practice.

Sources: Israel's angels and Qumran religious identity, Bible Interp, University of Arizona / Gospel of Philip, translated by Willis Barnstone / Prayer of Thanksgiving, Brashler, Dirkse and Parrott translation / Mandaean Associations Union, Mandaean beliefs.

Overgeneralized

Supplied claim: All compared traditions demand celibacy and rejection of family life.

The Mandaean community account affirms marriage. Text-specific and branch-specific practices are required before assigning a universal ascetic program.

Sources: Mandaean Associations Union, Mandaean beliefs.

Substantive differences erased

Supplied claim: Plato and all Gnostics describe the same evil material creator.

Timaeus calls the maker good; Ptolemy distinguishes a just intermediate maker from both highest Father and devil. Lower or derivative does not always mean independently evil.

Sources: Plato, Timaeus, translated by Benjamin Jowett / David Brons, The Demiurge in Valentinianism.

Reverses the selected passage

Supplied claim: The Treatise on the Resurrection says resurrection is an illusion.

The treatise affirms resurrection's reality while developing a transformative account. This differs from a simple denial of resurrection and requires its own comparison with bodily-resurrection doctrines.

Sources: Treatise on the Resurrection, translated by Willis Barnstone.

Incorrect manuscript provenance

Supplied claim: The Gospel of Judas is a Nag Hammadi discovery.

It belongs to Codex Tchacos. Comparative relevance does not change the place of a text in its actual manuscript collection.

Sources: National Geographic, Gospel of Judas pages' long journey.

Incorrectly generalized textual definition

Supplied claim: John defines every doubter of any miracle as Antichrist.

Distinguish the Gospel from 1 and 2 John. The selected epistolary tests concern Christ, Father and Son, and coming in flesh; a universal test of assent to every miracle is not their wording.

Sources: 1 John 2 and 4, World English Bible / 2 John, World English Bible.

Population and trend not established

Supplied claim: A growing Hasidic/Kabbalistic movement now accepts Jesus and the full Jesus-Scrolls bridge.

Named scholarly theses, individual appreciation and a declaration of cooperation do not establish a communal doctrinal shift. A dated institutional and speaker-level record is needed.

Sources: Paul Mendes-Flohr on Martin Buber, ABC Philosophers Zone interview / Daniel Boyarin, The Jewish Gospels, publisher description / Israel Knohl, The Messiah Before Jesus, publisher description / To Do the Will of Our Father in Heaven, Orthodox rabbinic statement (2015).

Category mistake

Supplied claim: Eastern Christian churches are generally more Gnostic than Western churches.

Compare communion, liturgy and doctrine separately. The Coptic-Catholic declaration explicitly affirms incarnation and Trinity; age and geography do not prove demiurgical cosmology.

Sources: Common Declaration of Pope Paul VI and Pope Shenouda III, May 10, 1973 / CNEWA, The Catholic Eastern Churches.

Counterexamples documented

Supplied claim: Only Mani includes both Buddha and Zoroaster.

Baha'i and Ahmadiyya community sources explicitly recognize both. Their theological interpretations of these figures are not thereby identical to Manichaeism or to the figures' original teachings.

Sources: Baha'i international community, introductory teachings / Ahmadiyya Muslim Community Greece, community and founder account / Manicheism i. General Survey, Encyclopaedia Iranica.

Terminology and cosmology oversimplified

Supplied claim: Manichaean Elect and Hearers are necessarily hereditary castes, and all physical creation is only an evil mistake.

Different obligations do not alone establish hereditary caste. Manichaean cosmogony assigns the light powers a role in constructing a cosmos that serves the rescue of light.

Sources: Manicheism i. General Survey, Encyclopaedia Iranica / Werner Sundermann, Cosmogony and Cosmology iii. In Manicheism, Encyclopaedia Iranica.

Analogy does not establish genealogy

Supplied claim: Subtracting every theological difference proves one continuous ancient school.

Retain differences as variables. Test shared scripture, borrowing, institutional transmission and independent convergence rather than treating all similarity as one kind of evidence.

Sources: John J. Collins and James Nati, The Rule of the Association and Related Texts (2024) / Plato, Timaeus, translated by Benjamin Jowett / Gospel of Philip, translated by Willis Barnstone / Charles G. Haeberl and James F. McGrath, The Mandaean Book of John: Text and Translation.
