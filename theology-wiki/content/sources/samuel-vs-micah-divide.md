### I. **Samuel: The Rise of Kings and Centralized Power**

**Samuel** was a prophet, priest, and judge at a time when Israel was transitioning from tribal confederation to monarchy. His narrative is preserved primarily in **1 Samuel**.

- **Key Themes**:
- **Divine Kingship vs. Human Kingship**: Samuel resists Israels demand for a king (1 Samuel 8), warning that a human king will enslave and oppress.
- **Reluctant Anointer**: Despite warnings, he anoints Saul and then David, signaling a divine accommodation of monarchy.
- **Temple and Sacrifice**: While Samuel criticizes external rituals when they lack obedience (1 Samuel 15:22), his tradition remains tied to centralized worship and the Ark of the Covenant.
