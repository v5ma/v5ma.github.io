### Key facts:

1. **No direct mention of Jesus, Yeshua, Christ, or Messiah Jesus appears in the Dead Sea Scrolls.**
The scrolls were written between approximately **250 BCE and 50 CE**, overlapping with Jesus's lifetime but primarily reflecting **sectarian Jewish thought before and around his time**, particularly that of the **Essenes**, a separatist apocalyptic group.

2. **Some figures in the Scrolls resemble messianic expectations that align loosely with Jesuss portrayal**, such as:
- A **Teacher of Righteousness** who suffered at the hands of a Wicked Priest.
- Prophecies of **two messiahs**: one priestly and one kingly.
- Themes of **end-times, resurrection, forgiveness, and spiritual renewal**.

However, these are **not references to Jesus himself**, but rather **messianic archetypes** present in Second Temple Judaism, of which Christianity later became one trajectory.
