Self Aware Networks GPT:
Thats a sharp summary of Galatians 3:19.
Pauls argument there goes like this:

> *O foolish Galatians! Who has bewitched you? ... Did you receive the Spirit by the works of the law, or by hearing with faith?*
> *(Galatians 3:12)*

He then cites Abraham (Genesis 15:6):

> *Abraham believed God, and it was counted to him as righteousness.*

So Pauls conclusion is:

> *Know then that it is those of faith who are the children of Abraham.*
> *(Galatians 3:7)*

Meaning: spiritual descent from Abraham is determined not by genealogy or law-keeping, but by *faith* trust in Gods promise. In the same way Abraham believed before circumcision or Sinai, Gentile believers become his heirs through the same pattern of faith.
