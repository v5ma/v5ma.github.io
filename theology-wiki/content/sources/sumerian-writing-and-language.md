Gilgamesh did not invent writing. Writing emerged in Mesopotamia around 3500 BCE, most notably with the Sumerians and their development of cuneiform script.

While the Epic of Gilgamesh is one of the earliest known works of literature, dating back to approximately 2100 BCE, it was written in Sumerian, and later in Akkadian, using the already established cuneiform writing system.

Ancient Mesopotamians most likely developed written communication as a way to record economic transactions, keep track of trade agreements, and other bookkeeping duties. Writing subsequently evolved and broadened to encompass literary traditions, as seen in The Epic of Gilgamesh.
