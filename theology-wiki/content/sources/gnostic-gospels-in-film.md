No major feature film has **faithfully adapted** an entire Gnostic Gospel like the *Gospel of Thomas*, *Gospel of Mary*, or *Gospel of Judas* in the same way the canonical Gospels have been adapted in films like *The Passion of the Christ* or *Jesus of Nazareth*.
