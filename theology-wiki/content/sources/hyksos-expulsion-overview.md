The **Hyksos Expulsion** refers to the historical event in ancient Egypt around **1550 BCE**, during which the Egyptian rulers drove out the **Hyksos**, a foreign dynasty that had ruled over Lower Egypt (the northern part of Egypt, including the Nile Delta) for roughly a century...

### Background:
- The **Hyksos**, meaning "**rulers of foreign lands**," were predominantly Semitic-speaking peoples from the Levant (modern-day Syria-Palestine region).
- Around **1650 BCE**, they settled in the Nile Delta and gradually established political dominance. The period of Hyksos rule is known as the **Second Intermediate Period** of Egypt.

### Reasons for Expulsion:
- **Cultural and political tensions** emerged as Egyptians resented foreign rule.
- Hyksos brought advanced military technology to Egypt, notably the **horse-drawn chariot**, improved bows, and bronze weaponry, which Egyptians later adopted.
- Rising nationalistic sentiment and political ambition in Upper Egypt (the southern region) led to rebellion and resistance.
