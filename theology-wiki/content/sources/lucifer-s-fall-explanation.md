In biblical and extra-biblical tradition, Lucifer (or the Day Star, *Helel ben Shachar* in Isaiah 14:12) is cast down for pride for claiming independent wisdom and exalting himself above his proper station.

Heres how the key texts frame it:

---

### 1. **Isaiah 14 and Ezekiel 28: Pride and Self-Exaltation**
- **Isaiah 14:1314:**
You said in your heart, *I will ascend to heaven; I will raise my throne above the stars of God ... I will make myself like the Most High.*
The offense is an act of self-elevation: presuming equal or greater authority than the divine source.

- **Ezekiel 28:17:**
Your heart was proud because of your beauty; you corrupted your wisdom for the sake of your splendor.
Wisdom itself became corrupted through self-reference he knew better than God.

Both passages describe the archetype of a being whose *gnosis* turns into hubris the refusal to stay aligned within the order of creation.
