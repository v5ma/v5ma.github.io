## The Vision Itself

Ezekiel says:

- The hand of YHWH (God) takes him in the spirit to a valley **full of dry bones**.
- The bones are **very dry** long dead, no life, no hope.
- God asks: **Son of man, can these bones live?**
Ezekiel answers, basically: *You know, Lord.* (He refuses to give a cheap yes or no.)

Then God tells Ezekiel:

1. **Prophesy to the bones**
- Ezekiel speaks Gods words to the bones: they will live again.
- The bones **rattle**, come together, bone to bone.
- Tendons, flesh, and skin cover them now they look like bodies, but there is **still no breath**.

2. **Prophesy to the breath (ruach)**
- `ruach` in Hebrew = breath / wind / spirit (deliberately ambiguous).
- Ezekiel calls the breath from the four winds.
- Breath enters them; they **stand up**, an **exceedingly great army.**
