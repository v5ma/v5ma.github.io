### 1. **Different Religious Origins**
- **Ahura Mazda** is the highest god in **Zoroastrianism**, a religion founded by **Zoroaster (Zarathustra)** in ancient Persia, possibly around 1000500 BCE.
- **Jesus** is the central figure of **Christianity**, which emerged in the 1st century CE in Roman-occupied Judea, deeply rooted in **Second Temple Judaism**, not Zoroastrianism.

### 2. **Theological Differences**
- Ahura Mazda is **uncreated**, the source of all that is good, and opposed by Angra Mainyu, the evil spirit. Zoroastrianism is **dualistic**: two opposing forces shape the universe.
- Christianity teaches **monotheism with a trinitarian view**: Jesus is the Son in the Trinity (Father, Son, Holy Spirit) and not in opposition to an equal evil force.
