### 1. **Jesus Own Teachings on Oneness**
In John 17:21 Jesus prays: *That they all may be one, just as You, Father, are in Me and I am in You.*
He invites humanity into the same relational unity He shares with God not as a claim of individual divinity in isolation, but as *participation* in divine life.
Similarly, John 10:34 (quoting Psalm 82) *Is it not written in your law, I said, you are gods?* indicates an awareness that the divine image lives within each person.
