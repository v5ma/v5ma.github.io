The three eschatological figures

The Prophet

This figure is believed to be the "Prophet like Moses" promised in Deuteronomy 18:1819.

Some scholars believe this prophetic figure could be the same as the "Teacher of Righteousness," the founder of the Qumran community, who would return at the end of days.

The Anointed of Aaron (the priestly messiah)

This anointed figure was expected to be the ultimate high priest, descended from Aaron.

Given the Qumran community's priestly origins, this priestly figure was often considered the preeminent messiah, with authority over the royal messiah.

The priestly messiah's arrival would inaugurate a new, eschatological legal order, superseding the temporary rules of the community.
