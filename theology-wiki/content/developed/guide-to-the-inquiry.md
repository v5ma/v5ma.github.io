# A guide to the inquiry

## Four questions, one connected inquiry

A divine name crosses a cultural boundary. A teacher's message survives a change of leadership. A sacred representation becomes a standard for personal action. A political power asks for allegiance. The subjects differ, but each asks how an inherited pattern acquires authority and what people do with it.

This guide is an editorial map of the collection, not a claim that every hypothesis in it depends on all the others being true.

## What is inherited, and what changes?

[[el-in-ancient-egypt|El in Ancient Egypt]], [[kenite-hypothesis-and-yahweh-origins|the Kenite hypothesis]] and [[samaritan-texts-and-sacred-authority|Samaritan texts and sacred authority]] ask how names, practices and claims to continuity move between communities. [[jesus-teacher-of-righteousness-hypothesis|The earlier-Teacher reconstruction]] adds the distinction between a founder, a succession and a transmitted teaching. Shared material is a reason to investigate a relationship, not to erase the differences.

## What makes authority legitimate?

[[antichrist-as-a-pattern-of-conduct|Antichrist as a pattern of conduct]] judges authority through its relation to domination and compassion. [[trump-first-beast-of-revelation|The First Beast article]] applies that framework to a particular political identification. The connection is ethical and interpretive; the historical identity of an ancient teacher does not have to be solved before conduct can be criticized.

## How does a mind change?

[[cognitive-gnosticism|Cognitive Gnosticism]] and [[christ-as-an-inner-model|Christ as an inner model]] ask how a sacred representation can become a working standard rather than a passive belief. [[faith-as-deliberate-joy|Faith as deliberate joy]] concerns practice, while [[religion-for-conscious-robots|Religion for conscious robots]] extends the question to hypothetical self-aware machines. The latter is not a declaration that present-day language models are conscious.

## What should a warning lead people to do?

[[ukraine-russia-forecast-record|The Ukraine and Russia forecast record]] separates a proposed causal sequence from its subsequent assessment. [[apocalyptic-repair-theology|Apocalyptic Repair Theology]] asks how practical care and resistance can develop while danger remains real. [[gnosticism-and-temple-trauma|The Temple-trauma hypothesis]] asks a historical question about how literature responds to catastrophe. Prediction, response and retrospective interpretation should be connected without being confused.

## Follow the evidence without losing the idea
