# Terms used in this inquiry

These definitions describe usage in this collection, not a universal dictionary of every tradition.

## Teacher of Righteousness (TOR)

The title used for a teacher associated with the Dead Sea Scrolls. In this wiki, Jesus/TOR names Micah's proposed historical identification; the abbreviation does not itself establish that identification.

[[jesus-teacher-of-righteousness-hypothesis|Read the connected investigation]]

## Pattern theology

The interpretive method used here to compare connected roles and actions across sacred narratives and historical cases. It allows recurring patterns while preserving the author's stronger claim of a particular fulfillment.

[[trump-first-beast-of-revelation|Read the connected investigation]]

## Apocalyptic repair

The proposal that domination can culminate in real catastrophe while acts of repair also accumulate. Practical care is not a denial of danger, and a warning is not permission to abandon care.

[[apocalyptic-repair-theology|Read the connected investigation]]

## Coherence and conformity

The source theology connects coherence with God, creation and human flourishing. This edition distinguishes that moral claim from mere synchronized behavior or obedience. These are not interchangeable physical and ethical measurements.

[[religion-for-conscious-robots|Read the connected investigation]]

## Cognitive Gnosticism

The proposal to examine spiritual ideas through how a mind represents and changes itself. A comparison with neural or computational language is an explanatory hypothesis, not an automatic identity between an ancient concept and a measured brain process.

[[cognitive-gnosticism|Read the connected investigation]]

## Inner model

A representation of a person or ideal used as a standard for examining one's responses. The Christ-as-model discussion uses a gradient-descent analogy; it is not evidence that a specific learning algorithm has been measured in prayer.

[[christ-as-an-inner-model|Read the connected investigation]]

## Demiurge, archons and Pleroma

Terms encountered in the Gnostic discussions for a creator figure, ruling powers and divine fullness. Their meanings vary across texts. The wiki's psychological applications must be distinguished from what each ancient work says.

[[cognitive-gnosticism|Read the connected investigation]]

## Manuscript, composition and event

The surviving physical copy, the formation of a text and the event narrated by it are different dating questions. A proposed earlier copy date does not by itself identify a character or move a biography.

[[jesus-teacher-of-righteousness-hypothesis|Read the connected investigation]]

## Kenite hypothesis

An existing historical proposal about the southern transmission of Yahweh worship. It is not Micah's coinage, and accepting that transmission proposal does not require accepting a volcanic identification of Sinai.

[[kenite-hypothesis-and-yahweh-origins|Read the connected investigation]]

## Forecast and recovery lead

A forecast is an identifiable claim about a later outcome. A recovery lead points to a discussion whose full original passage is still missing. A lead is not a new transcript or a scored prediction.
