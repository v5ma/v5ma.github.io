
## Scripture concordance: persons, representations and institutions

This September 17, 2026 research supplement develops the author's requested distinction between an individual identification and a recurring pattern. The existing identification remains Micah Blumberg's theological interpretation. The new [148-passage concordance](https://v5ma.github.io/theology-wiki/scripture-concordance/index.html) separates the biblical wording, the actor in the passage, a documented contemporary or historical record, and the question connecting them. It contains 128 biblical verses and 20 numbered sections from a wider textual corpus. That is the size of an editorial selection, not a total of fulfilled predictions or a score assigned to a political figure.

A person and a kingdom are not mutually exclusive levels of imagery in the texts. Daniel 7:17 calls the beasts kings; Daniel 7:23 describes the fourth beast as a kingdom. Revelation 17 interprets the heads in more than one way and distinguishes the beast, rulers who delegate power, and the city represented by the woman. These are textual grounds for examining leadership and institutions together. They do not erase the distinctions among their actions. [Daniel 7](https://ebible.org/engwebp/DAN07.htm) and [Revelation 17](https://ebible.org/engwebp/REV17.htm).

The individual level asks what a person says or does. The persona level asks how that person is publicly represented, including representations produced by others. The institutional level asks which offices, rules, organizations and resources make an action possible. An archetypal reading compares recurring relationships among those levels. A claimed final fulfillment is an additional proposition about a particular historical referent and sequence. Moving from one level to another requires an explanation; it cannot be accomplished simply by changing the meaning of a word whenever a difficulty appears.

The concordance therefore records relations rather than substituting names for every noun. A speech can relate to a claim of authority; a religious endorsement can relate to the promotion of authority; a purchase restriction can relate to material access. Each retains its own agent, date, source and limitation. A passage about the Lamb, the faithful community or divine judgment is not assigned to Trump merely to fill the catalogue. Some passages supply an ethical contrast or a limit on interpretation, and some remain without a contemporary assignment.

## Butler: the aftermath is a distinct subject of evidence

The [Butler reception note](https://github.com/v5ma/v5ma.github.io/blob/master/theology-wiki/event-forecast-register/corpus-review/BUTLER-RECEPTION-20240713.md) now preserves the author's emphasis on what followed survival: public reactions, religious explanations, visible solidarity and later mission language. The research question is not exhausted by the mechanics of the July 13, 2024 attack. It also concerns how survival was subsequently narrated and used. That emphasis is a September 2026 interpretation update, not a newly backdated prediction.

Trump's July 18, 2024 acceptance speech describes spectators thinking he had died, then recognizing survival after his raised fist. It attributes his presence to divine grace and contains recorded audience responses. These are identifiable passages in a primary speech transcript. The account of spectators' thoughts is Trump's retrospective account, not an independently measured belief held by every person present. The [acceptance transcript](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/address-accepting-the-presidential-nomination-the-republican-national-convention-milwaukee) allows the two kinds of evidence to remain distinct.

The January 20, 2025 inaugural address explicitly connects survival to a political mission, while also describing peace-making and unity as the speaker's desired legacy. This is a later use of the survival narrative in an official ceremony. It should be dated separately from the immediate aftermath. The [official transcript](https://www.whitehouse.gov/remarks/2025/01/the-inaugural-address/) documents the statements; it does not establish divine authorization or the reception of every listener.

Revelation 13:3 connects the wound, healing, wonder and following, while verse 4 introduces worship and a claim of military unassailability. Verses 12 and 14 return to the wounded beast within the other beast's promotion. An analysis can examine the reception clauses without replacing the entire passage with a medical claim. Equally, an audience cheering a survivor does not by itself document worship. The [translated chapter](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+13&version=KJV) supplies the clauses; the historical record must supply evidence for each proposed relationship.

An earlier comparison is available without assigning the same meaning to both presidents. Reagan's April 28, 1981 return to Congress acknowledges public prayers, concern for others injured and improved health before moving to policy advocacy. The [Reagan Library transcript](https://www.reaganlibrary.gov/archives/speech/address-joint-session-congress-program-economic-recovery-april-1981) documents that sequence. Similarity supplies a comparative question, not a verdict that either person has a particular prophetic identity.

## Shared images are acts of publication, not only artifacts

The [shared-imagery register](https://v5ma.github.io/theology-wiki/event-forecast-register/x-media/index.html) distinguishes the artist, earliest located publisher, selecting account, added caption and later institutional amplification. Publication remains relevant when somebody else made the image. A screenshot of another person's post, a native repost, and a newly uploaded related version are different kinds of circulation evidence. A visually similar earlier image does not establish every intermediate link in its transmission.

The May 2, 2025 academic archive records publication of the papal portrait by Trump's account. That establishes an account event, not which individual operated the account or created the image. The [publication record](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/truth-social-posts-may-2-2025) and the merged image register preserve the source and those limits. Other images in that register remain at their explicitly stated level of inspection; this supplement does not upgrade descriptions into authenticated image files.

The textual reason to record intermediaries is that Revelation 13:11-15 assigns promotion and the image to another agent. The represented subject need not have personally drawn an image for its circulation to matter. Analysis can ask what authority a portrayal claims and how an account responds to it. Actual audience belief, an instruction to worship and a consequence for refusal require additional evidence. The composition of an image and its social effects are not interchangeable observations.

## A concordance is a network, not a completed jigsaw

The puzzle metaphor is useful when it encourages precise references. It becomes misleading when it requires every verse to have one contemporary counterpart or treats several phrases from one episode as independent confirmations. A source can inform several questions without multiplying into several independent witnesses. Likewise, Daniel, Revelation and later apocalyptic writings may share motifs through textual reception; a repeated motif is not automatically a separate prediction of the same modern event.

The new catalogue preserves the source edition and numbering, including the difference between a biblical verse and a section of the Didache or Ascension of Isaiah. It does not silently merge the first beast, second beast, dragon, Babylon, lawless adversary and Johannine antichrists into a single actor. Their combination belongs to an interpretive argument, not to a single explicit cast list given by every source.

This distinction also governs the forecast record. A forecast needs its own original date, proposed mechanism, geographical scope, time horizon and later assessment. A parallel noticed after an event is a retrospective comparison. Calling a former candidate a predecessor may be part of a typological proposal, but it does not turn a missed dated forecast into a successful one.
