This is the Theology Wiki's supplemental event and forecast register, opened on September 16, 2026.

The register follows the research subjects in Micah Blumberg's existing contemporary interpretation of Revelation: political authority and allegiance, mediated authority and AI, conflict, subsistence, commerce, and permission to buy or sell. It does not replace that interpretation with a competing theological commentary. Documented events, the author's statements, and newly proposed research questions remain separately attributed. An open thematic link can be retained without claiming that it establishes a prophetic identity or fulfillment.

[Open the event and forecast reader](index.html). The reader provides the 2016-2026 chronology, the current energy-scarcity log, the four supplied September forecasts, and six prospective observation scenarios. Search and passage/year filters operate only in this browser. No information is sent to a server.

[Read the September 16 energy-scarcity event log](ENERGY-SCARCITY-2026-09-16.md). The log connects the dated price and supply observations to the author's question about scarcity and purchase authorization, while preserving the geographical scope of the Russian reports.

[Read the canonical historical records and bibliography](events.json). This first edition contains 22 dated entries, eight passage topics and 32 source records. The historical range begins in 2016; it does not claim that the present theological mapping was recorded in 2016. Each event has its own source, geographical scope, first-logged date and comparison question. The bibliography identifies inaccessible full texts and retrospective sources rather than claiming a full review that did not occur.

[Read the canonical author and future register](forecasts.json). Four posts supplied by the author retain their exact words, handles and claimed dates. Direct X retrieval did not succeed, so their platform timestamps are not represented as independently verified. The original categorical language is preserved; the register does not convert it into a finding about an inevitable future. SHA-256 values identify the exact archived text, not the authenticity of a platform timestamp.

The six new scenarios are editorial research formulations registered on September 16, 2026. They cover purchase authorization, the continuation or expiration of emergency credentials, delayed food effects, energy-route recovery or disruption, implementation of AI agreements, and the author's proposed AI-to-conflict sequence. Their dates are review checkpoints, not dates predicted by Revelation and not deadlines supplied by the author. A review checkpoint does not create an automated monitoring task.

[The earlier forecast ledger remains unchanged](../data/forecast-ledger.json). Its March and April 2025 records, October 2025 interpretation update, March 2026 deterrence argument, and July-August 2026 recovery leads remain in their original chronology. A newly clarified September mechanism must not acquire an earlier date by association. Related posts in one forecast family are not separate fulfilled-prediction counts.

[The existing First Beast article](../san-reader.html?page=trump-first-beast-of-revelation), [the fuel-to-food discussion](../san-reader.html?page=ukraine-russia-forecast-record), and [the dated ledger](../san-reader.html?page=forecast-ledger) provide the surrounding authorial narrative. This companion is additive. It does not modify the original conversations, generated principal articles, narration, roadmap workbook, shared reader shell, or private archives. Its records are not yet entries in the principal reader's generated search index.

Future editors should add an observation with a new date and source rather than overwrite an earlier snapshot. Preserve the exact author text. Any correction should identify the superseded record, reason and new evidence. Record the issuer and actual eligibility rule before treating a card as a purchase requirement. Distinguish an optional payment instrument, a quantity limit, a permit for designated services and a rule conditioning purchase on allegiance. Record the cancellation of restrictions and successful implementation of agreements as well as their extension or failure.

The canonical files are events.json and forecasts.json. index.html renders them directly and escapes record text through DOM textContent. ENERGY-SCARCITY-2026-09-16.md is the authored event note. No build of the principal wiki is required for this additive companion. Run node --test theology-wiki/event-forecast-register/register.test.cjs from the repository root for local integrity checks. These checks establish data and interface contracts, not the truth of a theological interpretation. The main repository's release workflow remains the publication gate.
