This is a selected, dated archive of Donald Trump's Truth Social statements on war, loyalty, punishment, negotiation and commerce, added to the Theology Wiki on September 16, 2026.

[Open the searchable reader](index.html), or [read the canonical records](posts.json). The initial selection contains 22 posts dated from August 2023 through September 2026. It is a starting collection, not a complete account history and not a measurement of how often any topic appears in all of the account's output.

Micah Blumberg requested this material for the wiki's existing contemporary Revelation narrative. The records connect to that narrative through separately labeled research questions about authority, allegiance, conditional access, conflict, subsistence and commerce. The First Beast and Antichrist identification remains the author's interpretation. The archive does not assign political fit scores or issue a fulfillment verdict. The event-specific author-annotation fields are empty until the author supplies them.

Each record distinguishes a short exact quotation from a summary and contextual reporting. Original-platform URLs are retained wherever recovered. Most text witnesses are the American Presidency Project's academic transcriptions of primary posts. Earlier and recent records sometimes depend on an independent archive or a news report quoting the post. The source type and direct-access limitation are visible on every record. A linked original is not claimed to have been independently captured merely because an archive supplies its URL.

The collection preserves selected wording, not complete copies of every post. Only three very short posts are marked as complete short texts. SHA-256 values identify the exact excerpt bytes in this edition; they do not authenticate platform timestamps or prove that an original remains unchanged. No screenshot, deletion claim or platform API capture is included.

Dates are displayed in Eastern time. The academic collection uses Pacific-day groupings. The munitions-and-leakers entry is therefore an August 6 Eastern post in an August 5 Pacific collection. Where only a report date is recoverable, no hour or minute is invented. The two September 14 reports link original posts that returned a JavaScript-only shell during direct retrieval.

A statement is not an observed outcome. Accusations of treason or sedition remain the speaker's allegations. Reported calls for punishment are distinguished from an actual prosecution or sentence. The lawmakers-video episode retains both the reference to illegal orders in the video and the later White House clarification. The June 17 restraint clause, June 23 ceasefire announcement and July 14 fee replacement remain linked with the related demands and threats, preserving revisions and qualifications as part of the same historical record.

Future additions should use a stable post ID, exact source URL, date basis, subject, short excerpt and contextual note. Preserve original text when adding a correction or new source witness. Record subsequent policy, military or judicial developments separately with their own evidence. Related posts from one episode are not independent fulfilled predictions. Full-post and screenshot capture, missing original URLs, any actual consequences, and integration with the main wiki's generated search index remain separate tasks.

[The scarcity report catalogue](../research-reports/README.md) records the full report as awaiting its original export. [The surrounding event reader](../index.html) and the earlier forecasts are unchanged. This companion adds no background monitor, server-side editor, analytics, browser storage or external script.

The tests check data contracts and preservation boundaries, not the historical or theological truth of a conclusion.
