A sacred name cannot make an authority immune to correction.

From cosmic cognition and the divine ground to ancient polemic, Samaritan continuity, the ethics of command and Gaza's humanitarian crisis.

September 9, 2026 / Six working essays, 4875 words of main argument

Today's discussion, developed into arguments

The series preserves the distinction between a possible thinking universe and a transcendent God. It then asks how finite interpreters claim authority, how competing communities inherit sacred material, and what a theology of life requires when institutions cause harm.

Historical continuity does not transfer moral guilt. These studies criticize interpretations, specific conduct and accountable institutions, not the inherent worth or authenticity of an ethnic or religious population.

The Gaza essay distinguishes the August 2025 famine confirmation, subsequent changes, and the July 2026 assessment and projection. Current facts have review dates; metaphysical proposals are not recorded as observed events.

The philosophical work needs independent arguments connecting consistency, existence, agency and goodness. This release does not claim those investigations complete.
