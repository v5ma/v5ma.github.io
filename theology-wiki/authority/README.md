# Authority, inheritance and repair

Six supplemental Theology Wiki studies developed on September 9, 2026. The full HTML articles and their Markdown manuscript sources are readable independently of JavaScript. The hub has optional title/topic search. Sources and access limits are available in HTML and JSON.

## Scope

These new working essays develop the current author direction. They distinguish cosmic cognition from a transcendent divine ground, compare two failures of authority, test Qumran/Gospel/rabbinic polemics, examine Samaritan continuity without a monopoly-of-descent verdict, criticize sacred extermination commands, and apply a repair framework to dated Gaza humanitarian evidence.

They do not assign demonic allegiance or hereditary guilt to Jews, Orthodox communities or any other ethnic/religious group. They do not establish secret supernatural intervention. Criticism addresses specified claims, actions, policies and institutions.

## Evidence and dates

The July 23, 2026 IPC release assesses April 16-June 30 and projects July-December; a projection is not a September census. The OCHA report is titled September 4 but displays September 8 metadata. Its Gaza reporting interval is August 24-30 except dated updates. A prior famine classification is not silently carried forward.

Access notes mark limited abstract, indexed-translation and mediated access. These essays are not a completed critical-manuscript collation or a judicial finding. The historical articles deliberately retain counterevidence in Gospel, rabbinic and prophetic texts.

## Preservation

The cluster is linked from the existing reader navigation and selected relevant article views. It does not enter the main generated page/search index. Original conversations, principal authorial texts, listening products, shared workbooks, ranked assessment inputs and sibling applications are unchanged. No new account, third-party runtime, analytics or storage writes are introduced.

## Publication

Canonical new essay text is provided in both static HTML and manuscripts/*.md. manifest.json records titles, source IDs, reading relationships and word counts; sources.json records source scope. The verification test checks that manuscript and HTML prose agree and that internal links resolve. A read-only workflow validates the new cluster, exercises both current readers and verifies the actual deployed bytes after master publication.

The labels working essay and AI-assisted do not imply final author approval, completed peer review or worldwide novelty. Publication and test success are separate from proof of any philosophical or theological conclusion.
