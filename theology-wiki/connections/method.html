How these connections are selected

Existing editorial and authored links first; narrow shared subjects require textual evidence at both endpoints. Shared generic religious words and category membership are excluded.

A shared subject is a reading comparison, not proof of influence, identity, agreement, fulfillment or historical dependence.

Connections were checked against the existing public wiki and its preserved discussions. This directory publishes links, subject labels and relevance explanations, not copied conversation excerpts. Original source text and speaker attribution remain on the existing pages. A linked discussion is not an endorsement.

Editorial connections preserve their stated reasons. Existing links gain a route back. Shared-subject comparisons use named works, figures, places, or narrowly defined questions with source references at both ends. Broad category membership, navigation boilerplate, old automatic repair candidates, and generic words such as God or religion do not qualify by themselves.

The first twelve links are visible immediately; more appear in an expandable list. The complete directory works without JavaScript. Individual reader pages use the same data. No source text is rewritten.

Some source previews are short or concern incidental non-theological matters. Those pages can have fewer links, or none. This is preferable to inventing relevance. The available source record is not a certification of every claim it contains.
