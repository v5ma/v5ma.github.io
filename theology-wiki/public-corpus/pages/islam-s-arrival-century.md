Micah Blumberg:
did Islam arrive in the 5th or 6th century AD?

Self Aware Networks GPT:
Islam as a religious system formally began in the **7th century AD**, specifically in **610 AD**, when the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) received his first revelation from Allah (God) through the Angel Gabriel in the Cave of Hira near Mecca, in present-day Saudi Arabia.

- **5th century AD (400–499 AD)**: This was before Islam's founding, during a period known as the "Jahiliyyah" (Age of Ignorance) in the Arabian Peninsula, marked by polytheism and tribal practices.
  
- **6th century AD (500–599 AD)**: This was still the pre-Islamic era. Muhammad was born in **570 AD**, an event known as the "Year of the Elephant." However, Islam had not yet been revealed or established as a religion during this century.

So Islam formally began in the **7th century AD**.
