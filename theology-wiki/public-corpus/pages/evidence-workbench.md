## James receives and transmits knowledge

Mediated ancient report / James and succession. Locator: Eusebius 2.1.4, quoting Clement, Hypotyposes 7.

Knowledge is reported as passing through named recipients and subsequent transmitters.

Interpretive boundary: The complete Hypotyposes is not independently recovered here, and the knowledge is not identified as Thomas.

Next investigation: Compare transmitted content and authority without equating knowledge with membership in a later Gnostic school.

Surviving channel: eusebius.
