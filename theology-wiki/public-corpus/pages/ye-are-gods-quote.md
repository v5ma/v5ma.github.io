The quote you're looking for is from the Bible, specifically from **John 10:34**. In the King James Version (KJV), it reads:

**"Jesus answered them, Is it not written in your law, I said, Ye are gods?"**

This is a reference to **Psalm 82:6**, which says:

**"I have said, Ye are gods; and all of you are children of the most High."**

In John 10, Jesus is quoting the Psalms in response to accusations of blasphemy, using this passage to argue that calling himself the Son of God is not blasphemous if scripture itself refers to others as "gods."
