Evil, Agency, and the Repair of the World

A connected study of Iranian influence, Job, Maimonides, Kabbalah and the survival of religious memory.

Research series and constructive bridge / September 9, 2026

Where is evil located, what does redemption change, and who gets to preserve the evidence? These questions connect the study of religious development to the Theology Wiki's work on inner models, authority and repair.

The historical investigation asks how ideas travel and change. The philosophical comparison asks how accounts of divine unity relate to human responsibility. The constructive argument asks what these comparisons can contribute to a shared future without erasing the differences among traditions.

This series preserves that distinction throughout. It does not treat resemblance as proof of borrowing, assign unsupported influence counts to biblical books, or present a chosen fictional mantle as a certified religious fulfillment. Begin with the chronology study, or read the synthesis first and follow its supporting comparisons.
