September 9, 2026 bridge edition / Twelve studies and two evidence records

The inquiry now begins with Micah Blumberg's explicit reconciliation proposal: the Theology Wiki seeks a bridge between rational understanding and mystical participation, not a victory of one over the other. Natural causation need not cancel spiritual meaning. A sacred interpretation need not end the investigation of a natural cause.

The six new studies develop that thesis through prayer, Maimonides' accounts of angels and resurrection, Hasidic contemplation, a fourteen-book passage review, and an explicitly interpretive political application. The six earlier studies remain intact below. The evidence records distinguish the author's proposals, the surrounding pasted AI answers, inspected texts, and new editorial synthesis.
