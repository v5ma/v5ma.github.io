# Sources and access scopes for the Exodus artifact audit

These are the sources used for [the audit](REPORT.md), not 24 independent proofs of one event. Primary artifacts, scholarly interpretations, ancient literary witnesses, a bibliography entry and an unsuccessful retrieval are different records.

## brooklyn-kitchen

K. A. Kitchen, A recently published Egyptian papyrus and its bearing on the Joseph story, Tyndale House Bulletin 2 (1956-57), pp. 1-2. Full article retrieved. The next, unrelated article starts on its second page. Dates are the author's 1956 scheme; the complete Hayes 1955 critical edition and original papyrus have not been independently collated.

https://www.tyndalebulletin.org/article/32324.pdf

## albright-names

W. F. Albright, Northwest-Semitic Names in a List of Egyptian Slaves from the Eighteenth Century B.C. (1954), pp. 222-233. Publication metadata recovered; full article not accessible. This is a bibliographic research lead, not a completed name-by-name verification.

https://www.jstor.org/stable/595513

## brooklyn-museum

Brooklyn Museum object 3369, Papyrus 35.1446. Repeated page retrieval failed. Counts attributed elsewhere to the museum were not authenticated directly, and the 95/79 discrepancy remains unresolved.

https://www.brooklynmuseum.org/opencollection/objects/3369

## rekhmire-met

The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Brickmakers, Tomb of Rekhmire, Nina de Garis Davies, 30.4.77. Catalogue of a modern facsimile identifying the ancient scene as Dynasty XVIII, Thutmose III to early Amenhotep II. The modern reproduction and original tomb have different dates.

https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/544649

## rekhmire-anthony

Flora Brooke Anthony, Levantines in 15th and 14th Century B.C.E. Egyptian Art. Authored iconographic analysis and caption discussion concerning foreign captives and building for Amun at Thebes. The depicted group is not independently identified as Israelite.

https://www.thetorah.com/article/levantines-in-15th-and-14th-century-egyptian-art

## ipuwer-tla

Roland Enmarch and contributors, Recto: Admonitions = Ipuwer, Thesaurus Linguae Aegyptiae, text GJA5VMSJDFAOPG6TP6ZYY3KCRI. Metadata places the surviving copy palaeographically in probably late Dynasty XIX. Initial sentence pages were consulted; requested later pages failed. No full original-language collation is claimed.

https://thesaurus-linguae-aegyptiae.de/text/GJA5VMSJDFAOPG6TP6ZYY3KCRI

## ipuwer-oraec

ORAEC digital transliteration, with scholarly source attribution. The river/blood clause corresponding to conventional 2.10 was located. This does not authenticate every plague or medical claim in the supplied transcript.

https://oraec.github.io/corpus/oraec21_text.html

## ipuwer-rosell

Pablo Martin Rosell, A short note about The Admonitions of Ipuwer and the work of Alan Gardiner (2014). Abstract and full one-page PDF consulted. Distinguishes New Kingdom manuscript from proposed earlier Middle Kingdom composition, not a date for the biblical plagues.

https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/handle/10915/89722

## amenhotep-khm

Kunsthistorisches Museum, Elephantine-Stele des Koenigs Amenophis II, inventory 5909. The catalogue was retrieved during this pass; later access was intermittent. Its Year 3 monument and high-chronology calendar assignment must not be silently combined with a different accession scheme.

https://www.khm.at/kunstwerke/elephantine-stele-des-koenigs-amenophis-ii-324305

## amenhotep-cefb

Centro di Egittologia Francesco Ballerini, Amenhotep II. This research organization distinguishes campaigns in years 3, 7 and 9. All original inscriptions and the co-regency/numbering questions have not been newly collated in this audit.

https://www.cefb.it/en/amenhotepii-en/

## amenhotep-ucl

UCL Digital Egypt, Amenhotep II. University guide to dated reign and distinct inscriptions. It gives approximately 1428-1397 BCE in its chronology; that date does not erase alternatives used by other catalogues.

https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/chronology/amenhotepii.html

## soleb-kennedy

Titus Kennedy, The Land of the Shasu (Nomads) of yhwA at Soleb, Dotawo 6 (2019), pp. 175-192. Full peer-reviewed article and published inscription photograph consulted. Its argument for an Israelite identification is kept distinct from the actual inscriptions. No independent epigraphic collation is claimed.

https://escholarship.org/content/qt07x6659z/qt07x6659z.pdf

## soleb-adrom

Faried Adrom and Matthias Mueller, The Tetragrammaton in Egyptian Sources - Facts and Fiction (2017), pp. 93-114. Full author-uploaded rendered chapter consulted, including conclusion. The competing geographical, linguistic and ethnic interpretations are preserved rather than resolved by ignoring one publication.

https://www.researchgate.net/publication/318675278_The_Tetragrammaton_in_Egyptian_Sources_-_Facts_and_Fiction

## avaris-isotopes

Chris Stantis et al., Who were the Hyksos?, PLOS ONE 15 (2020), e0235414. Primary bioarchaeological results and sampling limitations consulted. Forty of 75 sampled people had nonlocal signatures; this is not a population census or an Israelite identification. Cemetery selection and elite bias remain relevant.

https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0235414

## seqenenre-ct

Sahar N. Saleem and Zahi Hawass, Computed Tomography Study of the Mummy of King Seqenenre Taa II (2021). Primary research. Craniofacial trauma is observed; capture, restraint and the battle sequence are reconstructions. The authors explicitly state that the exact location of death is unknown.

https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2021.637527/full

## ahmose-ebana

Autobiography of Ahmose son of Ebana, in an attributed Miriam Lichtheim translation reproduced online. The reproduced inscription, not the site's introductory biography, is the cited witness. It names repeated combat and captives at Avaris, followed by Sharuhen; it does not name an Ephraimite defection.

https://ancientegyptonline.co.uk/ahmoseebana/

## sallier

British Museum, Papyrus Sallier I, EA10185,1. Collection record identifies a literary Tale of Apophis and Seqenenra, New Kingdom/Dynasty XIX(?). It is not a contemporary diplomatic dispatch.

https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA10185-1

## sanhedrin

Babylonian Talmud, Sanhedrin 92b, Steinsaltz edition. Rav's Ephraim identification and alternative identifications/parable readings were consulted together. The page quotes 1 Chronicles 7 but supplies no Ahmose/Avaris synchronism.

https://steinsaltz-center.org/portal/library/Talmud/Sanhedrin/chapter/92.b

## shemot

Shemot Rabbah 20:11, Hebrew and English primary passage embedded in a Sefaria source sheet. Only that passage is used, not the compiler's unrelated political commentary. It specifies a thirty-year difference and the scattered-bones route explanation.

https://voices.sefaria.org/sheets/535446

## ezekiel37

Ezekiel 37, World English Bible. Verse 11 identifies the bones with the whole house of Israel; verses 16-22 concern the joining of Joseph/Ephraim and Judah. Ahmose and Gath are not named as the vision's historical coordinates.

https://ebible.org/engwebp/EZK37.htm

## exodus7

Exodus 7, World English Bible. Verse 7 supplies the age eighty. The text does not assign a modern BCE date or name Seqenenre.

https://ebible.org/engwebp/EXO07.htm

## acts7

Acts 7, World English Bible. Verses 23 and 30 supply the explicit forty-year life stages used in the conditional arithmetic. These are not independent Egyptian chronological records.

https://ebible.org/engwebp/ACT07.htm

## wiki-current

Theology Wiki, canonical Moses/volcano article at the inspected baseline. Read through the GitHub connector. Its older conquest-first sequence and link to the newer pre-Ahmose variant were both preserved.

https://github.com/v5ma/v5ma.github.io/blob/f6d3867df89ae9dea32f763b1d55c84212c41cc6/theology-wiki/editorial/authorial-articles/moses-volcano-and-exodus-chronology.md

## wiki-report

Theology Wiki, Hyksos Avaris and the Moses Prosopography. Earlier AI-assisted report used as hypothesis context, not an independent ancient or Egyptological witness. Its individual identifications and degrees of certainty remain subject to review.

https://v5ma.github.io/theology-wiki/research-reports/hyksos-avaris-20260922/index.html
