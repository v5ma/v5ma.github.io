# Exodus artifact and chronology audit: active research checkpoint

This continuation was requested by Micah Blumberg after supplying two narratives: an uploaded Dynasty of Slaves / Ephraim-Ahmos I reconstruction and a pasted Ryan Henry artifact transcript. This checkpoint is not a completed evidence report.

The current published Moses article preserves both the conquest-first sequence and a linked pre-Ahmose/Seqenenre variant in the completed Hyksos report. Those are different causal orders and must not be silently merged.

Research targets are Papyrus Brooklyn 35.1446, the brickmaking scene in Rekhmire's tomb, the Admonitions of Ipuwer, the several Amenhotep II inscriptions and the Shasu-yhw lists at Soleb and Amara West. The earlier narrative also requires checking the Ephraim/Gath tradition, Ezekiel 37, the taking of Avaris, and the claim that an Ephraimite army's flight caused mass enslavement.

For every claim retain the supplied wording, the actual artifact or textual witness, object/copy date, proposed event or composition date, what is verified, what remains an inference, and the consequence for the author's preferred pre-Ahmose model. A Semitic name is not by itself an Israelite ethnic identification. A later analogy is not a contemporary witness to a sixteenth-century BCE event.
