# Sources and interpretive scope

These notes accompany “What if both the Gnostic Gospels and the Canonical Gospels are telling the truth?” Sources were consulted on 11 October 2026. The shared readings are constructive theological proposals, not claims of scholarly consensus or demonstrated common authorial intention. The article paraphrases ancient texts rather than reproducing extended modern translations.

## Claim-to-source map

1. Opening classification paragraph: Christopher Skinner, NASSCAL Thomas entry; Emily Laflèche, NASSCAL Philip entry; Judith Hartenstein, NASSCAL Mary entry; NASSCAL Judas entry. These are specialist reference summaries with scholarly bibliographies. They establish differentiated literary/theological profiles. No claim that all noncanonical gospels are Gnostic, or that Thomas is uncontroversially Gnostic.
   - https://www.nasscal.com/e-clavis-christian-apocrypha/gospel-of-thomas/
   - https://www.nasscal.com/e-clavis-christian-apocrypha/gospel-of-philip/
   - https://www.nasscal.com/e-clavis-christian-apocrypha/gospel-of-mary/
   - https://www.nasscal.com/e-clavis-christian-apocrypha/gospel-of-judas/
2. Faith/knowledge: Gospel of Truth opening, Grant translation; John 17:1–3. Ethical comparison: Truth exhortation near end (Mattison translation, section identifiable by hungry/sick language), Matthew 25:31–46. The analogy is relational and ethical; it does not equate their entire soteriologies.
   - https://www.earlychristianwritings.com/text/gospeltruth.html
   - https://www.gospels.net/truth
   - https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+17&version=NRSVUE
   - https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+25&version=NRSVUE
3. Cross: Gospel of Truth cross/living-book paragraphs; John 12:23–33; Mark 8:31–35. All directly read. Neither article nor sources are used to assert complete agreement on atonement or incarnation. 'The cross can accomplish through revealing' is the proposed theological synthesis.
   - https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+12&version=NRSVUE
   - https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+8&version=NRSVUE
4. Sacraments: Philip Codex II p. 67, continuing into p. 68 for rites; John 3:5. NASSCAL notes disputed bridal-chamber ritual interpretation. The locator is 67–68 because the list of mysteries falls at the boundary. This discussion makes no claim about Jesus' marital status.
   - https://www.gospels.net/philip
   - https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+3&version=NRSVUE
5. Resurrection: Philip p. 73 for acquiring resurrection during life; p. 56 for the Lord's reversed sequence; John 5:24–29; Luke 24:36–43. The article acknowledges the difficult explicit sequence and does not claim Philip's entire resurrection teaching has been harmonized. Philip's body language is internally complex and should not be summarized simply as 'no body can rise.'
   - https://www.earlychristianwritings.com/text/gospelphilip.html (Isenberg translation)
   - https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+5&version=NRSVUE
   - https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+24&version=NRSVUE
6. Mary/authority: Gospel of Mary pp. 10, 17–19; John 20:17–18; NASSCAL for missing manuscript pages. The shared role of a woman's testimony does not make the visions identical, establish historicity, or imply authorship by Mary. Missing pages are not reconstructed.
   - https://www.gospels.net/mary
   - https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+20&version=NRSVUE
7. Creation: John 1:1–14; Philip p. 75; Judas pp. 33–36 and 47–53. Isenberg uses 'mistake'; Mattison uses 'transgression' for Philip, but both retain creator's frustrated intent and lack of imperishability. Article cites Isenberg and paraphrases without claiming the English term is uniquely correct. Judas interpretation and translation disputed: the simplistic 2006 'Judas the hero obeyed Jesus' account should not be treated as settled. NASSCAL supplies the modern scholarly caution; primary Mattison translation illustrates the lower-ruler cosmology. Article does not adjudicate all Judas translation disputes.
   - https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+1&version=NRSVUE
   - https://www.gospels.net/judas
