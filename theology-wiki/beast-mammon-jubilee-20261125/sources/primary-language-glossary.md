# Primary-language glossary: Mammon, Beast, Jubilee, and Qumran terms

*Editorial status: Source-verified lexical reference dated November 25, 2026. This glossary supplements [[glossary|Terms used in this inquiry]] with primary Greek and Hebrew terms verified directly against SBLGNT (`morphgnt/sblgnt`), the Hebrew Bible, the Septuagint, and critical Dead Sea Scrolls editions—correcting common spelling and attribution errors.*

## Verified Greek and Hebrew reference table

| Term | Language & Primary Anchor | Verified Meaning & Textual Notes |
|---|---|---|
| *charagma* (`χάραγμα`) | Greek — Revelation 13:16–17 (SBLGNT) | A stamp, engraved brand, imperial seal, or coin-like imprint required to buy or sell. Explicitly corrects garbled spellings such as "Chagma" seen in informal online summaries [1]. |
| *mamonas* / dat. *mamona* (`μαμωνᾶς`) | Greek from Aramaic — Matthew 6:24; Luke 16:9, 13 (SBLGNT) | Wealth, property, or money personified as a rival master (*kyrios*) directly competing with service to God. SBLGNT prints a single *m*; double-*m* (*mammonas*) appears in later witnesses and English usage [1]. |
| *arithmos anthropou* (`ἀριθμὸς ἀνθρώπου`) | Greek — Revelation 13:18 (SBLGNT) | "A human number" or "number of humanity." The anarthrous construction (no definite article before *anthropou*) permits both individual and generic/corporate readings [1]. |
| *therion* / gen. *theriou* (`θηρίον` / `θηρίου`) | Greek — Revelation 13:1–18; Daniel 7 LXX (SBLGNT) | "Wild beast." Transliterated into Hebrew consonants, nominative *therion* (`תריון`) sums to 666 (Beale 1999) and Revelation 13:18's exact genitive *theriou* (`תריו`) sums to 616 (Oberweis 1986; P. J. Williams 2007) [2]. |
| *agorazo* (`ἀγοράζω`) | Greek — Revelation 13:17; 14:3–4; 18:11 (SBLGNT) | "To buy, purchase, or redeem in the marketplace." Verbally links Beast commercial coercion (13:17), the 144,000 redeemed from the earth (14:3–4), and Babylon's unsold luxury cargo (18:11) [1]. |
| *deror* / *aphesis* (`דרור` / `ἄφεσις`) | Hebrew (Leviticus 25:10) & Greek LXX / NT (Leviticus 25:10; Isaiah 61:1; Luke 4:18) | Jubilee liberty, debt cancellation, and release of captives. Luke 4:18 uses the Septuagint Jubilee term *aphesis* twice in Jesus's Nazareth manifesto [1][3]. |
| *kikar zahav* (`ככר זהב`) | Hebrew — 1 Kings 10:14; 2 Chronicles 9:13 | "Talents of gold." Records Solomon's annual gold intake as exactly 666 talents of gold at the hinge where his reign violates Deuteronomy 17:16–17 [4]. |
| *lo yarbeh* (`לא ירבה`) | Hebrew — Deuteronomy 17:16–17; Talmud *Sanhedrin* 21b | "He shall not multiply" (horses, wives, or silver and gold). Central Torah limit cited by Qumran's Temple Scroll (`11Q19` cols. 56–59) and the rabbinic accusation against Solomon in *Sanhedrin* 21b [5][6]. |
| *hon ha-risha* / *hon ha-resha* (`הון הרשעה`) | Hebrew — Damascus Document `CD 6:15` | "Wealth of wickedness" defiled by vows, curses, or Temple corruption. Anchored verbatim in `CD 6:15` (not `1QM` Column 7) and thematically paralleled in `1QpHab 8:8–12` [7]. |
| *melek ha-kittim* (`מלך הכתיים`) | Hebrew — War Scroll `1QM 1:4–5`, `15:2–3` | "King of the Kittim." A recurring structural title for the dominant foreign imperial adversary allied with Belial, shifting historically across Hellenistic and Roman eras rather than locking onto a single birth name [8]. |
| *ha-kohen ha-rasha* (`הכוהן הרשע`) | Hebrew — Habakkuk Pesher `1QpHab 8:8–12` | "The Wicked Priest." Functional sectarian title condemning corrupt Jerusalem priestly leadership accused of forsaking God for wealth and plundering the peoples [7]. |
