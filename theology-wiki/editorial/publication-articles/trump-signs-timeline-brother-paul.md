# Trump and Prophetic Signs: A Documented Timeline

## Purpose and attribution

This chronology develops a question already central to this wiki: whether Donald Trump's public conduct, representations and surrounding structures of allegiance instantiate the pattern described in its [[trump-first-beast-of-revelation|First Beast interpretation]]. Its starting point is the transcript supplied for **“2026 Update on the Antichrist,” by Bible Prophecy with Brother Paul**. The transcript revisits the creator's earlier fifteen-verse argument and adds later events. Brother Paul deserves explicit credit for that selection and its proposed connections. This article reorganizes those connections into an independently sourced chronology rather than reproducing his script.

“Signs” here means **proposed historical correspondences**, not a finding that Trump has performed supernatural miracles. The research asks what happened, what was said or published, who made the representation, and why the interpreter connects it with a biblical passage. The existence of the record and the proposed prophetic identity are different propositions.

The existing wiki argument is not simply a list of similarities. It examines a possible system linking personal authority, religious legitimation, public devotion and material dependence. Brother Paul's account overlaps that argument but is a separate author's interpretation. Agreement on one parallel does not imply adoption of every claim in his video. See [[antichrist-as-a-pattern-of-conduct|Antichrist as a pattern of conduct]] for the ethical standard and the distinction between a recurring type and a proposed final instance.

### Source status

- **Transcript consulted:** complete user-supplied text attachment, received October 24, 2026 UTC. It has no timestamps and combines a purported early-2023 presentation with later commentary. Dates such as “last year” cannot safely be carried into the chronology without verification.
- **Credited creator:** [Bible Prophecy with Brother Paul](https://www.youtube.com/@realBrotherPaul); [the creator's website](https://antichrist45.com/) independently links that channel.
- **Original video:** the exact original YouTube URL and upload date remain unverified in this edition. The user-supplied title is retained rather than silently replaced.
- **Corroborating circulation record:** a [mirror titled “The Antichrist – 2026 UPDATE”](https://old.bitchute.com/video/1eQ8jGClYxyr/) displays August 16, 2026 and credits the same creator with a matching description. That is a mirror's date, **not proof of the original upload date or an authenticated frame-by-frame match**. The mirror's surrounding publisher and other material are not adopted as authorities.

## Reading the evidence labels

**Documented** means the narrow event or wording is supported by a primary record or identified reporting. **Reported** means the factual entry rests on reporting, not independent inspection of its underlying evidence. **Interpretive** identifies the proposed biblical relationship. **Open** marks a missing source, unresolved date or unestablished inference. These are not numerical scores for identifying an Antichrist.

## Chronology: the public record and its proposed meaning

### June 16, 2015: a branded political entrance

Trump announced his presidential candidacy at Trump Tower. The [campaign announcement archived by the American Presidency Project](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/trump-campaign-press-release-donald-trump-presidential-announcement) fixes the date; the [contemporary speech transcript](https://time.com/3923128/donald-trump-announcement-speech/) records a presentation emphasizing his business experience and exceptional capacity to lead.

**Interpretive connection:** Brother Paul connects established personal fame and self-promotion with John 5:43 and the lion-mouth/boastful-mouth imagery of Daniel and Revelation. The inauguration of a campaign under one's existing name is documented; the claim that this uniquely identifies a prophetic individual is not established by the announcement itself. The useful institutional question is when a personal brand becomes a standard of allegiance that overrides independent judgment.

### July 18, 2015, with a June 2016 qualification: forgiveness

Asked by Frank Luntz whether he had asked God for forgiveness, Trump expressed uncertainty and said he generally tried to correct wrongdoing himself. He also described communion as a form of seeking forgiveness. [CNN's July 21 transcript reproduces the exchange](https://prod.transcripts.cnn.com/show/sitroom/date/2015-07-21/segment/01). A [June 8, 2016 report of his Cal Thomas interview](https://time.com/4361777/donald-trump-cal-thomas-forgiveness/) records a subsequent statement that he would ask for forgiveness, while hoping not to require much.

**Interpretive connection:** Brother Paul reads the 2015 answer against 1 John 2:22 as practical rejection of the need for salvation. That theological inference can be discussed, but the record should not be compressed into an unqualified claim that Trump has never acknowledged forgiveness or explicitly denied Jesus is the Christ. His private faith is not directly observable from these exchanges.

### January 28, 2016: greed described as a qualification for national bargaining

At Des Moines, Trump described his earlier pursuit of money and pivoted to wanting to exercise that acquisitiveness for the United States. The [speech transcript, approximately 12:18–12:56](https://rollcall.com/factbase/trump/transcript/donald-trump-speech-des-moines-ia-january-28-2016/), preserves both parts.

**Interpretive connection:** Brother Paul associates this rhetoric with 1 Timothy 6:10. The ethical question is not removed by the nationalist qualification: it is whether personal acquisitiveness can be morally transformed simply by changing its stated beneficiary. Conversely, omitting that qualification would misrepresent the speaker's argument. This is evidence of public rhetoric, not an independent measurement of every motive behind later decisions.

### July 21, 2016: personal indispensability

Trump's convention acceptance speech presented him as uniquely able to repair the political system. This wiki already examines its context, including its references to cooperation with other officials, in [[trump-first-beast-of-revelation|the First Beast article]]. The [full primary speech transcript](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/address-accepting-the-presidential-nomination-the-republican-national-convention-cleveland) permits that argument to be inspected.

**Interpretive connection:** this is a concrete example for Brother Paul's general themes of boasting and self-exaltation. It is a wiki-added anchor, not a newly discovered specific episode explicitly dated in the supplied transcript. Several themes can draw on the same speech without becoming several independent confirmations.

### December 27, 2017: a proposed Trump railway station

Israel's transport minister announced a proposed Western Wall station named for Trump. Contemporary [Reuters reporting](https://www.aol.com/article/news/2017/12/27/israel-wants-to-build-trump-station-near-western-wall/23317803/) described a plan still needing approvals.

**Interpretive connection:** Brother Paul treats naming honors as evidence of reception “in his own name.” The proposal establishes an official gesture of honor. It does **not**, on that source alone, establish that an operational station existed. The action belongs to the naming authority, not to Trump merely because it honors him, and it cannot establish how every Israeli or Jewish person regarded him.

### February 2018: Trump and Cyrus on a temple coin

The Mikdash Educational Center's commemorative coin paired Trump with Cyrus. [Contemporary AP reporting carried by CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/israel-trump-coin-honours-recognition-jerusalem-as-capital/) documents the object.

**Interpretive connection:** this is a specific artifact of biblical-political legitimation. A private commemorative coin is not ordinary Israeli currency, a government confession, or proof that its subject worships the deity or embodies the role represented. The producer, imagery and recipient of praise should remain separate actors in the analysis.

### August 21, 2019: chosen-one rhetoric and amplified religious praise

In remarks about confronting China, Trump described himself as “the chosen one.” The [archived White House transcript](https://trumpwhitehouse.archives.gov/briefings-statements/remarks-president-trump-marine-one-departure-60/) fixes the trade context. On the same date his account amplified Wayne Allyn Root's comparison of Israeli enthusiasm for him with royal and divine-return imagery; [the archived posts](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/tweets-august-21-2019) identify that attribution.

**Interpretive connection:** Brother Paul connects these episodes with 2 Thessalonians 2:4 and a developing savior persona. Repeating another person's praise is an act of amplification worth studying, but it is not the same event as originating those words. A claim to be the person to confront China, a claim to divine mission, and an explicit claim to be God must not be treated as interchangeable quotations.

### September 15, 2020: Abraham Accords

The [signed Abraham Accords Declaration](https://2017-2021.state.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Abraham-Accords-signed-FINAL-15-Sept-2020-508-1.pdf) promotes coexistence, dialogue and expanding diplomatic relations. The [Bahrain–Israel declaration](https://trumpwhitehouse.archives.gov/briefings-statements/abraham-accords-declaration-peace-cooperation-constructive-diplomatic-friendly-relations/) records the particular parties and Trump's facilitating role.

**Interpretive connection:** Brother Paul sees this initiative as a foundation for Daniel 9:27's covenant with many, not the completion of every predicted feature. The documents inspected do not establish a seven-year covenant, a midpoint breach, a rebuilt temple, or the termination of sacrifice. The future expansion anticipated in the transcript must remain a forecast rather than be credited as an event already accomplished. A chronology must not silently start a seven-year clock merely because a peace agreement was signed.

### July 13 and July 18, 2024: Butler survival and its narration

The existing [[trump-first-beast-of-revelation|First Beast study]] and its [Butler reception dossier](https://github.com/v5ma/v5ma.github.io/blob/master/theology-wiki/event-forecast-register/corpus-review/BUTLER-RECEPTION-20240713.md) distinguish the attack from subsequent meaning-making. Trump's [July 18 convention speech](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/address-accepting-the-presidential-nomination-the-republican-national-convention-milwaukee) narrates survival in religious terms.

**Interpretive connection:** the wiki examines Revelation 13:3's reception sequence, not only the wound. Cheering a survivor, attributing survival to providence and worshiping a ruler are different propositions requiring different evidence. The supplied transcript uses Butler chiefly in connection with later mission language; the broader reception analysis belongs to the wiki's prior work.

### August 15, 2024: survival joined to a world-saving mission

At Bedminster, Trump connected the survival discussion with God and with a collective mission to save the country and the world. The [recorded press-conference transcript, 45:11–45:33](https://rollcall.com/factbase/trump/transcript/donald-trump-press-conference-bedminster-new-jersey-august-15-2024/) dates the statement.

**Interpretive connection:** Brother Paul's savior-persona argument has a real statement to examine here. The date matters: later recirculation does not make the quotation a new 2025 or 2026 utterance. Saying that “we” want to save the world also differs from claiming divinity or asserting that world salvation has already been completed.

### October 19, 2024: the “little horn” phrase in a trade speech

The [Latrobe campaign speech](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/remarks-campaign-rally-latrobe-pennsylvania) contains the phrase excerpted in the supplied transcript. Its surrounding subject is cars manufactured abroad, their route to the United States, tariffs and the automobile industry.

**Interpretive connection:** Brother Paul regards the verbal overlap with Daniel 7:8 as striking. The speech does not explain itself as a reference to Daniel or identify Trump as its horn. Its date is October 2024, whatever “last year” meant in the video's narration. The separate English association of “trump” with trumpet is wordplay, not proof that Daniel's animal-horn image was a prediction of an English surname.

### January 20, 2025: golden-age and providential mission language

The [official inaugural address](https://www.whitehouse.gov/remarks/2025/01/the-inaugural-address/) combines a promised golden age with an account of survival as divine preservation for making America great again.

**Interpretive connection:** Brother Paul connects gold, personal mission and the savior persona. The source establishes the rhetoric, not God's endorsement. “Golden age” is a political metaphor; its use cannot by itself establish literal gold worship or the identity of Revelation's beast.

### February 25–26, 2025: an imagined golden Trump in Gaza

Trump's account shared a synthetic resort video featuring a gigantic gold-colored statue of himself. [A fact-check preserving screenshots and the posting context](https://leadstories.com/hoax-alert/2025/02/fact-check-trump-gaza-ai-video.html) identifies the imagery; the [linked account post](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/114068387897265338) is the publication reference. The date spans U.S. evening and UTC dating.

**Interpretive connection:** this is useful evidence for the gold-and-persona theme. It is an image of a statue, not evidence that a statue was built in Gaza. Account publication, artistic authorship, intended meaning and audience belief remain separate questions.

### By March 16, 2025: additional gilded Oval Office decoration

[Contemporary CNN reporting](https://kvia.com/politics/cnn-us-politics/2025/03/16/flags-figurines-and-gold-everywhere-trump-transforms-the-oval-office-into-a-gilded-gallery/) described gold-colored ornaments, mirrors and other additions to the Oval Office.

**Interpretive connection:** the repeated aesthetic supports a study of luxury and royal presentation. It does not establish that every object is solid gold, that the room replicates Solomon's temple, or that Trump worships Apollo. The theological meaning assigned to an aesthetic is an additional argument.

### June 21, 2025: strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities

Trump's [June 23 notification to Congress](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202500715/html/DCPD-202500715.htm) records U.S. strikes on three Iranian nuclear facilities on June 21 U.S. time.

**Interpretive connection:** Brother Paul juxtaposes peace-making claims with military force under Daniel 8:25 and the god-of-fortresses theme. A comparative argument should examine the stated justification, actual action and consequences. The fact of a strike does not establish the transcript's broader assertion that Trump privately wants to attack the entire world.

### October 18, 2025: the crowned-pilot video

Trump shared a fabricated video portraying a crowned version of himself in an aircraft dropping brown material, apparently fecal matter, on protesters. [Axios's account](https://www.axios.com/2025/10/19/trump-no-kings-post-jet-protesters) links [the publication](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/115398251623299921). The original media could not be independently rendered in this research pass; the visual description rests on reporting.

**Interpretive connection:** the act of sharing degrading imagery provides a specific public event for the transcript's conduct argument. It was not an actual aerial attack. Interpreting it morally does not require transforming a fictional depiction into a physical event.

### December 15, 2025: comments following Rob Reiner's death

After Rob Reiner and his wife were found dead the previous day, Trump published disparaging comments linking the deaths to Reiner's opposition to him. [Reuters reporting](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/trump-mocks-rob-reiner-suggests-without-evidence-his-liberal-politics-led-to-his-death-4408909) explicitly states that this causal connection was unsupported.

**Interpretive connection:** the conduct under examination is Trump's response to the deaths. The accusation embedded in that response must not be repeated as an explanation of the deaths themselves. This distinction permits a forceful moral assessment without spreading the unsubstantiated allegation.

### December 18, 2025, followed by June 4, 2026: the Kennedy Center name

A board vote added Trump's name; [his contemporaneous official remarks](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202501205/pdf/DCPD-202501205.pdf), pages 11–12, acknowledge the action. **Interpretive connection:** the earlier honor is relevant to self-magnification and institutional personalization. The reversal is equally relevant to whether institutional restraints remain operative. The transcript's example should not become a timeless assertion of an uncontested permanent renaming.

### February 28 and April 7, 2026: Iran war and a temporary ceasefire

**Interpretive connection:** these are separate actions, not a single event called permanent peace. They help contextualize the transcript's peace-and-force theme but do not establish which announcement its undated phrase “on the verge” refers to. This entry is not a claim that a temporary April ceasefire describes the situation as of October 24, nor a verification of a subsequent permanent agreement. No seven-year term is established by these sources.

### March 21, 2026: comments following Robert Mueller's death

[AP's obituary and contemporaneous account](https://apnews.com/article/7aca939dc25d4652815376f73e0f9aaf) reports Mueller's death on March 20, its family announcement on March 21, and Trump's expression of pleasure at the death.

**Interpretive connection:** this gives the conduct theme a dated public statement. Its factual status differs from the transcript's broader claim that everything about a person is vile. The moral argument should identify the act being judged and the standard applied.

### March 26, 2026: signature on future paper currency

The [Treasury announcement](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0425) states that Trump's signature will appear on future paper currency.

**Interpretive connection:** this is a concrete proposal to place personal presidential identification on monetary objects, relevant to the naming-and-material-symbols argument. The announcement concerns a signature, not a portrait, and is not by itself evidence that the bills were already circulating. It also does not establish a worship requirement or restriction on buying and selling.

### April 12–15, 2026: Christ-like imagery, deletion and explanation

Trump posted a synthetic image of himself as a robed healer on April 12 and removed it the following day. He said he had understood it as portraying a doctor. On April 15 he shared a different image of Jesus embracing him. [ABC's report](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-shares-image-jesus-embracing-continues-lash-pope/story?id=132052713) distinguishes these events; [AP's April 13 account](https://apnews.com/article/02f6b4554ea4b83af02af15987ae1f2d) records the controversy and his explanation.

**Interpretive connection:** the imagery can support examination of a healing or sacred persona even if its subject disputes the Christ-like reading. The interpretation and the denial both belong in the record. Neither image is an explicit spoken declaration “I am Jesus,” and an image of Jesus alongside Trump is not the same composition as an image of Trump in a healing role.

### April 21, 2026: America Reads the Bible

Trump appeared in a prerecorded Oval Office reading of 2 Chronicles 7:11–22 for the America Reads the Bible marathon. [OSV's completed-event report](https://www.osvnews.com/trump-participates-in-bible-reading-after-ai-depiction-resembling-jesus-pope-leo-controversies/) documents the passage and setting.

**Interpretive connection:** Brother Paul contrasts the passage's humility and repentance with political self-exaltation. That is a moral reading of the juxtaposition. Reading a passage about Solomon and the temple is not an announcement that the reader is Solomon, that America is ancient Israel, or that a new temple has been built.

### June 30, 2026: disclosure concerning 2025 finances

Reporting on Trump's annual financial disclosure places his 2025 receipts at at least $2.2 billion, with cryptocurrency-related activity prominent. [PolitiFact's October 5 source review](https://politifact.com/factchecks/2026/oct/05/markwayne-mullin/trump-wealth-financial-gain-crypto/) links the [Office of Government Ethics release](https://www.oge.gov/web/oge.nsf/Resources/Now%2BAvailable:%2BThe%2BPresident%E2%80%99s%2Band%2BVice%2BPresident%E2%80%99s%2Bcertified%2Bannual%2Bfinancial%2Bdisclosure%2Breports).

**Interpretive connection:** this is relevant to the transcript's wealth theme, but this edition has not independently recalculated the full filing. Reported receipts, company revenue, net personal profit, change in net worth and presidential salary are not equivalent. Dividing an annual figure by 365 produces an average, not proof of cash earned every day. Nor does the total by itself prove a specific unlawful transaction or establish the motive behind a particular policy. The ethics question requires those links to be documented rather than assumed.

### July 9, 2026: Palm Beach airport name change takes effect

**Interpretive connection:** unlike an unbuilt railway proposal, this source documents implementation. The institutional actor, date and status make the naming evidence more precise, but still do not establish population-wide religious devotion.

**Interpretive connection:** portraiture, a signature and a gold collectible are different monetary artifacts. None alone proves the coercive buying-and-selling system described in Revelation 13.

## The complete fifteen-theme ledger

The numbered themes below preserve the structure of Brother Paul's argument without treating its rhetoric as independently verified findings.

1. **Coming in one's own name — John 5:43.** Public branding and naming honors are researchable. The transition from those records to this verse's modern referent is interpretive. Proposals must remain distinct from completed namings.
2. **Lion-like mouth — Revelation 13:2; Daniel 7:4.** The transcript compares combative speech and lion imagery. An illustration produced by supporters is reception evidence, not proof that all speech by its subject fulfills the imagery. Daniel's first beast and Revelation's composite beast should not be collapsed without explanation.
3. **Self-magnification — Daniel 8:25; 11:36.** Particular boasts, honors and demands can be dated. The transcript's universal assertions about what Trump always wants or why he praises anyone are claims about motives, not independently demonstrated events.
4. **Boastful speech — Daniel 7:8; Revelation 13:5.** Speeches supply direct evidence. This overlaps theme 3; repeating the classification does not multiply independent evidence.
5. **Truth cast down — Daniel 8:12.** A strong case needs specific assertions compared with the contemporaneous record. This edition does not adopt the transcript's unsupported superlative that no world leader has behaved similarly. A generalized reputation is not a substitute for a dated factual ledger.
6. **Denial of Father and Son — 1 John 2:22.** The 2015 forgiveness exchange is real, with qualifications and later remarks retained above. Interpreting conduct as denying the practical significance of Christ is not identical to documenting an explicit doctrinal denial.
7. **Vile conduct — Daniel 11:21.** Public statements can be evaluated ethically without repeating unsupported personal accusations. The supplied script's claims about a niece, daughter and sexual affairs need separate quotations, dates and reliable sourcing before entering a factual timeline. They are not established by this article.
8. **God of forces/fortresses — Daniel 11:38.** Military glorification, architecture and mythology require separate records. [Art-historical analysis of Guido Reni's Aurora](https://smarthistory.org/reni-aurora/) confirms the painting's Apollo imagery. It does not establish that Trump worships Apollo, that Daniel's deity is Apollo, or that every decorative classical image is a profession of religious allegiance. The exact artwork in his residence needs its own authenticated visual record.
9. **Gold, Solomon and 666 — Revelation 13:18; 1 Kings 10:14; 11.** The numerical and moral comparison is a proposed typology. Solomon's gold and later religious deviation are scriptural material, but the equation of every pagan deity with one sun god is not demonstrated here. Gold-colored décor or branding does not independently establish occult worship.
10. **Little horn and “trump” — Daniel 7:8.** The Latrobe verbal overlap is documented with context. An English lexical resemblance is weaker than a demonstrated textual-historical connection and does not identify a unique person. Daniel's chapter interprets horns in political terms; it does not simply define them as musical instruments.
11. **Covenant with many — Daniel 9:27.** The 2020 agreements are documented. A specific seven-year term, midpoint violation and temple sequence remain unestablished. The transcript's purported 1987 Manhattan, Inc. Israel-peace quotation also remains open pending the original interview; other accounts of that interview concern nuclear diplomacy, which must not be silently substituted.
12. **Appearance as a force of light — 2 Corinthians 11:14–15.** Religious staging and endorsements can be documented. The passage concerns Satan and deceptive ministers in a discussion of false apostles; applying it to a president requires an argument. The supplied transcript cites verse 15, while the explicit angel-of-light wording is in verse 14. Bible reading alone cannot prove deception or sincerity.
13. **Love of money — 1 Timothy 6:10.** The January 2016 rhetoric and financial records are relevant. This verse is an ethical warning, not a passage explicitly naming one future political figure. The moral application should be distinguished from a unique identification.
14. **Falling away — 2 Thessalonians 2:3.** Brother Paul interprets religious allegiance to Trump as displacement of Christ. Specific leaders, statements, audiences and changes over time need documentation. Election support does not, by itself, establish every voter's theology or that an entire religious population has abandoned its faith.
15. **Self-exaltation as God — 2 Thessalonians 2:4.** The source advances a trajectory toward a future claim to have saved the world. Its prediction must not be rewritten as an already documented declaration that Trump is God seated in a temple. Public rescue rhetoric, religiously charged images and explicit divine self-identification remain distinct categories.

## Textual controls that strengthen rather than silence the inquiry

Several texts are being brought into conversation: [Daniel 7](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/DAN07.htm), [Daniel 8](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/DAN08.htm), [Daniel 9](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/DAN09.htm), [Daniel 11](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/DAN11.htm), [Revelation 13](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/REV13.htm), [1 John 2](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/1JN02.htm) and [2 Thessalonians 2](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/2TH02.htm). They contain distinct actors, settings and narrative sequences. Their combination is the interpreter's theological construction, not a single list of fifteen modern identification tests printed in the Bible.

[John 5](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/JHN05.htm), [2 Corinthians 11](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/2CO11.htm) and [1 Timothy 6](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/1TI06.htm) add questions of reception, deceptive religious appearance and acquisitiveness. [1 Kings 10](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/1KI10.htm) and [1 Kings 11](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/1KI11.htm) supply the Solomon comparison. Preserving those contexts makes the proposed bridge inspectable without requiring the author to abandon typology.

The strongest extension of the wiki's argument asks how the records connect: Does exceptional-rescuer rhetoric authorize exemption from ordinary moral standards? Do institutions convert personal imagery into a demand for allegiance? Does peace language conceal or restrain actual violence? Do material relationships make principled refusal costly? These questions invite evidence about mechanisms, not only resemblance.

Conversely, evidence that a representation was rejected, a naming reversed, a claim qualified, or a proposed event never occurred belongs in the same chronology. Such records constrain the interpretation rather than disappearing from it. There is no need to weaken the moral question in order to keep the historical record accurate.
