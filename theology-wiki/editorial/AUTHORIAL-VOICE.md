# Authorial voice and theory development

Developed encyclopedia articles, their summaries, narrations and episode scripts present the argument in first-person, theory-centered prose. They are not a biography of their author. Prefer "Christic Self-Formation Theory proposes...", "I distinguish..." or a direct account of the mechanism to "Micah proposes...", "he writes..." or "the author believes...". Naming the theory should sharpen its claim, not replace its explanation with a label.

Do not convert third-person reporting into repeated autobiographical reporting such as "I said in an earlier discussion...". Describe the idea, evidence, causal sequence and consequences. A hypothetical example illustrates the theory; it must not introduce the author as a fictional character or invent personal experience. Preserve strong constructive theological judgments and the full mechanism of a conjecture rather than reducing it to a vague motif or a list of caveats.

The working name is Christic Self-Formation Theory. Its existing public slug, christ-as-an-inner-model, remains stable. The name is proposed for editorial use, not claimed as a previously accepted label, an established scientific result or a globally unique coinage.

Do not alter archived conversations, speaker labels, quoted words or exact source excerpts. Historical persons, the biblical prophet Micah, credited researchers and translators retain their names. Bibliographic author credit is not biographical framing. Archival provenance and project-administration records may describe a source's author when attribution actually requires it. First-person edited prose is not a newly discovered verbatim quotation.

The canonical bodies for every developed article are now editorial/authorial-articles/<slug>.md; article metadata and new references are in editorial/authorial.json. Earlier edition, expansion, depth and foundation bodies remain legacy editorial records, not the current article inputs. The build uses authorial.cjs and exact reviewed metadata rewrites in authorial-metadata.json. Edit the canonical body rather than adding a divergent replacement to a legacy body. New prose must pass authorial.test.cjs and be rebuilt into every listening and search derivative.

Connect SAN through its actual distinctions: living body-brain, learned self-model, momentary registered state, maintained prediction pattern, recurrent bodily feedback and time-extended choice. Do not flatten constructed selfhood into nonexistence or causal impotence. No-self traditions can illuminate a comparison without being equated with SAN or used as evidence of undocumented historical transmission. Neural rendering is a proposed physical mechanism; contemporary studies of attention, compassion or decision preparation do not automatically establish it.

Historical compatibility is not confirmation. An interpretation can remain viable alongside alternatives, but a specific date, identification or proposed transmission link must remain answerable to evidence that could contradict it. Keep narrative dates, work composition, surviving copy dates, excavated context, modern publication dates and model assumptions distinct. Do not call every relationship direct transmission. Record competing accounts and identify what could distinguish them.

Use primary texts, institutional object records and original research. State when only an abstract was consulted. Record intellectual debts where supported, not inferred retrospectively from a newly found source. Put sources and access notes after the argument, and link related full investigations rather than substituting compressed promotional claims for them.

Changing a current text invalidates old narration hashes. Rebuild scripts and regenerate affected recordings; do not change a manifest hash to disguise unchanged third-person audio. The stock synthetic voice, exact spoken transcript and measured cue timings remain declared. Authorial point of view and approval of a final recording voice are separate decisions.

Generated navigator introductions and source-access prose use the exact reviewed substitutions in authorial-presentation.json, applied only inside the generated-page builder. This also keeps the research-method, product-plan, forecast and source-access prose in authorial voice without editing archived turns, source records, biblical names or other scholars' attribution. Canonical article bodies remain unchanged by that presentation map.
