# James, Thomas, and the continuity of a founder

Status: research-development note. This file is not a claim of a newly published reader edition or a completed critical-text comparison.

The next investigation should connect the Earlier-Teacher and Displaced-Biography Hypothesis to the authority of James and the transmission of Thomas. The central question is not merely whether two teachers sound similar. It is whether a founder's memory, a successor's authority, a durable institution, and a later narrative frame can be distinguished and then related through evidence.

The existing Daniel and Oniad studies remain the starting point. Their different chronology proposals must not be silently merged. The Egyptian branch supplies a proposed institutional history; it does not by itself supply a genealogical identification of James or an author for Thomas.

The primary comparison should keep Galatians 1:18-19 and 2:1-14, 1 Corinthians 15:7, Acts 15 and 21:17-26, Thomas saying 12, Josephus Antiquities 20.200, and the Hegesippus material quoted in Eusebius Ecclesiastical History 2.23 as distinct witnesses. The source trail for a quotation preserved by another writer must identify both levels. A passage about authority, a passage about kinship, and a passage about ritual standing answer different questions.

Each passage record needs its exact locator, consulted edition, access scope, wording relevant to the claim, proposed interpretation, competing interpretation, and what additional evidence would change the assessment. Dates must distinguish the narrated event, the proposed composition, and the surviving witness. Unknown dates stay unknown.

The affirmative reconstruction should be developed before objections are applied. Its major propositions are founder identity, institutional continuity, succession, transmission of sayings, and relocation of biography. A challenge to one proposition should not be reported as a refutation of every other proposition. Conversely, evidence that strengthens succession should not be described as proving founder identity.

The resulting investigation belongs in the existing book routes for Teacher identity, Thomas, and priestly continuity. Its museum counterpart should let a visitor move between each short exhibit explanation, the full argument, and the particular evidence record. A new narration can be generated only from the actual integrated article; existing recordings must not be relabeled.
