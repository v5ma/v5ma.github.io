Gloamward

String the bow. Cross three ruined courtyards. Evade spectral bolts and open the last gate.

Controls & comfort

Quest: left hand holds the bow. Bring the right hand close to the string, hold its trigger, draw back, then release. Hold the right grip while releasing for a teleport arrow. Right A changes arrow; B pauses. Right stick snap-turns.

Desktop: WASD moves; drag or arrow keys look. Hold F or left mouse to draw; release to shoot. Hold T for a teleport arrow. Q changes arrow. P pauses. Hold the mouse button while moving to aim a drawn shot.

Experimental WebXR. Physical Quest 3 tracking, frame rate and comfort are not certified. Teleport and snap turning are the defaults. Pause and stop if uncomfortable.

Roadmap & testing limitsNo accounts. No analytics. Progress stays on this device.
