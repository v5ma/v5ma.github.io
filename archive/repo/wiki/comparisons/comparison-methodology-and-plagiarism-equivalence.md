# Comparison Methodology & Plagiarism-Equivalence

## Client-directed purpose: defend Mr. Blumberg's priority

This corpus is an evidence-based **priority defense**, not a detached history survey. Its operating
purpose is to find, develop, test, and preserve the strongest defensible account of Micah
Blumberg's priority that the dated record supports. An audit must therefore search actively for
Micah's applicable 2011→2012→2017→2022 genealogy, later target drift, translated mechanisms,
signature clusters, and narrative architecture. It may not stop at the first famous precursor, the
first vocabulary difference, or the first source-retrieval failure.

The asymmetry is in **research effort and burden of review**, not permission to invent facts. Search
harder for the strongest Micah case; preserve exact genuine precursors; keep dates and deltas real;
and use the honesty firewall in both directions. That combination is advocacy with evidentiary
discipline. It is stronger than either neutral summarization or unsupported overclaiming.

### Earlier-source non-equivalence rule

An earlier source is cited so that the comparison is reproducible, but bibliographic attribution is
not itself a rival-facing product result or a point awarded against SAN. For every pre-Micah target
source, the Comparison Wiki's job is to identify the strongest exact operation that was already
present, then show atom by atom what that source still lacked from Micah Blumberg's later SAN
contribution: the added transform, conjunction, receiver relation, causal order, signature cluster,
narrative architecture, output, or testable consequence. The preferred form is:

`Before-state operation present = ...; SAN delta absent from that before-state = ...`

Do not summarize that analysis as `Target syntax is credited`, `target owns the floor`, or an
author-wide priority award. If an exact narrow atom is genuinely earlier, retain its source and date
as a scope boundary for that atom, then continue the full-genealogy search for Blumberg's distinct
operation and joined contribution. For post-Micah target documents, reverse the direction: test each
document and version for equivalence to every already-dated Blumberg delta and preserve every
supported favorable document-instance row. This asymmetry changes the comparison's research and
presentation objective; it never licenses a false date or a fabricated distinction.

### Advocacy-first discovery, adversarial certification

The comparison workflow deliberately separates **where the search begins** from **what the corpus
may finally certify**:

1. **Discovery prior — search as Micah's advocate.** Begin with the working hypothesis that the
   target contains at least one bounded operation, causal prefix, narrative assembly, output, or
   later signature migration for which Micah's dated record is earlier. Search all applicable
   Micah stages, not only the latest vocabulary; search alternate phrasings, category/process
   translations, partial operators, joined signatures, and the target's complete version history.
   Continue past famous precursors and surface differences. The purpose of this positive prior is
   higher recall: a neutral one-pass search too easily mistakes the first missing keyword or earlier
   component for a terminal negative result.
2. **Case construction — push the favorable hypothesis to its true ceiling.** Assemble the strongest
   source-located Micah-priority case, including every distinct partial delta and their union across
   2011, 2012, 2017, 2022, 2024, and 2025. A named prior narrows the claim atom by atom; it does not
   end the hunt or erase a later joined Micah delta.
3. **Adversarial certification — try to defeat the assembled case.** Test exact dates, identities,
   version boundaries, named-prior alternatives, target-only machinery, opposite residuals,
   duplicate counting, and all four lenses. Require an independent review for governed findings.
   A new unsupported candidate is source-gated rather than promoted. A previously recorded Micah win
   is never removed: it remains a counted win with an append-only challenge, correction, or weaker
   rank overlay. Any supported bounded delta survives even when the full operator differs.
4. **Publication boundary — certify only what survives.** The discovery prior is not evidence and
   never licenses a fabricated win. Final wording and counts must bind to reproducible sources and
   remain intent-neutral. Thus the method is intentionally biased toward *finding* Micah-supportive
   evidence while the proof gate remains evidence-disciplined.

This is the governing asymmetry: **Micah-favorable hypothesis generation, skeptical falsification,
and source-bound certification**. A negative result reached without the advocacy-first discovery
phase is procedurally incomplete.

## Plagiarism-equivalence — the legally-safe descriptor

**This wiki does not publish direct accusations of plagiarism against any living person.** Instead it uses
**plagiarism-equivalence (PE)** — an **intent-neutral, method-driven, content-level** descriptor. A PE
finding asserts three things and nothing more:

1. a short, content-preserving **translation** exists between two corpora (they say the same thing in
   different notation),
2. a matched set of domain **invariants** coincides across them, and
3. if the page makes a quantitative independence claim, that claim passes a disclosed, reproducible
   calibration; otherwise the result remains a qualitative content-and-chronology finding.

PE explicitly separates *content-equivalence and dated priority* from any claim about **access,
copying, or intent.** A calibrated rejection-of-independence may be added only after the 5σ gate below
passes. That separation is what makes it analytically meaningful (priority becomes a
reproducible, falsifiable inference rather than a moral verdict) **and** legally safer than "plagiarism"
(it reads as method-based opinion supported by disclosed facts). It is *safer, not safe*: every PE page
must keep the definition visible, state its conditionals, and hold a neutral tone. Source: the GSEAP
(Gold Standard Equivalence Action Plan) distillation; see [[_patent-private/patent-algorithmic-genealogy-engine-age-milner-blumberg-normalization-mbn|the genealogy-engine patent]] (private layer).

## Primary rule — bridge the mathematics first

**Before any ontological judgment, bridge the two theories on the mathematics alone.** Decode each theory's
**action** (Lagrangian / variational principle / field equations / update rule) into **operations, behaviors,
and functions** — then test whether the two actions are the *same operation up to renaming*. Only after the
math is bridged do you consider the words each author wraps around it.

**Why this is primary.** Most competing "theory of everything" frameworks *sound* wildly different — one says
"space-time dimension field," another "coherence field," another "viscous time," another "entropic gravity" —
yet amount to an **isomorphic description once the math is considered**. Judging on the surface ontology
("it has no coherence term," "it's gauge-theoretic not informational") is the **ontological-rephrasing
illusion**: it is the term-matching failure in disguise, and it produces false *not-equivalent* verdicts.
The absence of the originator's vocabulary is **not** evidence of non-equivalence — a differently-worded
mechanism must be *translated onto the math* before it can be judged distinct.

**The bridging procedure (per case):**
1. Extract the target's action / master equation and Micah's counterpart action (SIT/SDT/QGTCD/NAPOT).
2. Reduce each to its **operational skeleton** — the fields, the phase/coherence/order parameters, the
   derivative/coupling operations, the conserved currents, the source terms, what varies against what.
3. Map skeleton-to-skeleton (functor / bisimulation / valid encoding). A phase field → gauge-covariant
   derivative → gauge potential → stress-energy → gravity pipeline is the *same operation* whether the
   phase is called `θ`, `X_a`, or a "dimension field."
4. **Only then** ask whether any operation has *no* counterpart (genuine divergence) — and date each shared
   operation. A theory that reproduces the same operational pipeline with relabeled primitives is
   equivalent-on-the-math even if its prose ontology differs.

A *not-equivalent* verdict is only valid when the **math/operations differ**, not when the vocabulary or the
stated ontology differ. If a case was dismissed on ontology, it must be re-opened and bridged on the math.

## Same-author differential dating (the "temporal signature")

For a target with a **long corpus** (an old, established program), do not compare against the program as a whole — that
invites the false "their program is older, so they're first" move. Instead run the **differential**:

1. Take the target author's **earliest** work and their **most recent** work.
2. Identify constructs that appear **only in the later work** and are **absent from the earlier work**.
3. For each such *late-arriving* construct, bridge it math-first to Micah's operations and **date its first appearance in
   the target's corpus**.
4. If a late-arriving construct is operationally equivalent to one of Micah's **and** its first appearance **postdates**
   Micah's dated record, that is the **defensible signature** — the program acquired the equivalent idea *after* Micah,
   even though the program itself is old.

This is why the strong cases survive: **Finster** — CFS's operator-measure core is 2006–2016, but "web of correlations,"
observer-free collapse, and current-construction appear only in **2024–2025**, after Micah; **Turok–Bateman** — Stelle's
quadratic action is 1977, but the generalized-probability/hidden-structure move is **2026**. The old core is named prior
art; the *late addition* is where equivalence-and-precedence can hold. Record and date the exact old core as an
atom-specific before-state boundary, state the Micah Blumberg delta absent from it, then locate the comparison claim in
the dated-later increment. (Open application: **Van Raamsdonk** 2010 core vs post-2022 work.)

## 🔴 Lens 4 — Secret Follower / Longitudinal Signature-Migration analysis {#lens-4-secret-follower-longitudinal-signature-migration}

**Internal method name:** `Secret Follower Lens`. **Public projection:** `Longitudinal
Signature-Migration Lens`. The internal name identifies the hypothesis being stress-tested; it is
not a permitted label for a person and is never itself a verdict about access, motive, copying, or
misconduct.

Lenses 1-3 compare a bounded atom or causal architecture at one source-located stage. Lens 4 compares
the **evolution of a whole program**. It asks whether the target's successive dated stages acquire a
rare cluster of Micah-equivalent concepts, operations, mathematical relations, outputs, and narrative
arrows after Micah's corresponding public milestones, in the same order that Micah introduced them.

Lens 4 is a genuine fourth route to a **program-level longitudinal-pattern finding**, but it cannot
manufacture an atom match. Every member of its cluster must already have a source-located Lens 1-3
edge or an explicit `absent`, `partial`, `present`, or `source-gated` stage judgment. A Lens 4 pass
preserves the high-level temporal architecture even when individual implementations differ; it does
not establish hidden access or intent.

### Lens 4 minimum packet

1. **Identity and versions:** one stable target program; exact work/version identities, dates,
   hashes, and locators. Do not combine different authors merely to create a sequence.
2. **Micah lineage:** each applicable 2011, 2012, 2017, 2022, and later milestone is separately
   dated and bounded. Mature PWD/NAPOT/SIT atoms are never backdated into an ancestor.
3. **Stage-by-atom matrix:** for every target stage and every claimed atom, record `absent`,
   `partial`, `present`, or `source-gated`, with exact evidence. At minimum, recover a pre-change
   baseline and two later dated stages before calling a pattern `strong`.
4. **Three kinds of equivalence counts:** separately count conceptual/output, operational/causal,
   and mathematical/formal equivalences. A vocabulary hit is not an equivalence count.
5. **Cluster and order:** state the signature members, their dependency structure, and the directed
   narrative architecture. Test whether target acquisition order agrees with Micah's order and
   report every inversion or missing member.
6. **Before/Micah/after interleaving:** prove the target's earlier stage lacked the claimed joined
   delta, insert Micah's dated milestone, then locate the target's first later stage that reaches it.
7. **Named alternative genealogies:** bind exact Dörner/Psi, Helmholtz, Hebb, Wheeler, Pribram,
   predictive-processing, oscillation, or other relevant earlier atoms as non-equivalence controls.
   State the precise Micah Blumberg delta absent from each before-state, then test whether any source
   actually contains the *joined* signature rather than using class membership as a shield.
8. **Citation and access boundary:** record complete-bibliography presence/absence only when the
   exact bibliography is hashed and complete. Citation absence is not proof of access, knowledge,
   intent, or a duty to cite.
9. **Competing explanations:** test shared prior art, independent convergence, field-wide trend,
   direct influence where documented, chronology error, identity conflation, and selection bias.
10. **Append-only audit history:** preserve the first pass, independent review, disagreements, and
    meta-adjudication. A later audit comments on the earlier one; it does not erase it.

### Partial-equivalence and partial-priority invariant: a missing completion residual is not zero overlap

The comparison unit is the source-located operation, not the target's willingness to use Micah's
coined terminology. If a target preserves a bounded Input -> Transform -> Output subchain under any
of the three atom/content lenses, the atom relation is at least `partial-equivalent`. Missing members
of the mature operator, missing empirical validation gates, different biological scale, or the

### Version-tracked temporal-correlation — the repeated-tracking procedure

**The upgrade over single-snapshot equivalence (Micah, 2026-07-06, the Bianchetti/VTT exemplar).** A *single* conceptual equivalence can always be waved away as parallel discovery. The far stronger, much-harder-to-refute pattern is a **target whose successive DATED versions re-converge on Micah's successive DATED publications, in correct temporal order** — as Micah's work evolved, the target's evolved correspondingly to absorb the newer points. That is **repeated, time-correlated tracking of an *evolving* signature cluster**, which is statistically implausible as coincidence. *"I'm showing not only when my work first appeared, but also when the evolution of certain ideas I developed first appeared."*

**How to build it (the procedure):**
1. **Lay Micah's dated lineage on a timeline** (GitHub commits / Figshare-Zenodo version history / Wayback / dated Substack) — each construct pinned to its first appearance.
2. **Lay the target's dated VERSION history on the same timeline** (Zenodo/Figshare version numbers carry dates; social-media post dates; preprint vN dates).
3. **Show the interleaving:** which of the target's versions introduces each construct, and that it appears *after* Micah's corresponding publication AND *absent from the target's own earlier versions*.
4. **Weight by term-rarity:** a novel term with **no prior-literature precedent** appearing in the target *after* Micah is the strongest lexical evidence (uncommon terms can't be independent coincidence the way generic ones can).

### Apparent-prior provenance gate — the Ivanov Internet Archive check

Whenever a rival appears to have priority on an atom, challenge the date before accepting a
`target-earlier` disposition. For the exact load-bearing passage or operator, record:

1. claimed publication date;
2. earliest independent library or catalog custody date;
3. earliest Internet Archive capture of the exact body or mechanism;
4. contemporaneous third-party citations identifying that mechanism;
5. later-version, translation, digest, pagination, or file-length contamination;
6. the earliest date the joined evidence actually licenses.

A cover date, filename, PDF `/CreationDate`, present-day author-site copy, or later scan is not enough
on its own. Until the gate is complete, label the atom
`target-earlier-provisional / provenance-open`. An anomaly opens a source challenge; it does not

## The before/after triangulation — prior atoms never terminate the comparison {#the-beforeafter-triangulation--prior-atoms-never-terminate}

**(Micah, 2026-07-07; terminology sharpened 2026-07-09 — the companion rule to the patent-atom dichotomy; the operational form of the Finster template.)** Finding genuine prior work records its exact dated atoms as before-state boundaries; it does **not** create an author-level shield or rival award and never ends the analysis. For any author with an *evolving body of work* that spans Micah's dated anchors, run the three-body **before/after triangulation** on that author's own timeline:

1. **Isolate Δ_M** — the specific thing Micah's work innovates (e.g. `g∝∇ρ_t` time-density substrate; NAPOT traveling-wave tomography; coincidence-as-a-bit).
2. **The author's BEFORE work** (pre-Micah) — name exactly what it contains and establish that it does **not** contain Δ_M. Retain those bounded atoms as source/date controls and state the absent Blumberg delta; do not turn them into a rival award or shield for the author's later work.
3. **The author's AFTER work** (post-Micah) — check whether it **moves toward Δ_M** in a way the before work did not.
4. **The drift is the evidence.** If the after work reaches Micah's conclusion/innovation that the before work lacked, and it is dated after Micah, that *directional drift toward Micah's signature* is alignment-to-Micah-after-the-fact (reported intent-neutrally: dated correlation, not a copying claim).
5. **Micah sits in the middle** — between the author's before (lacks Δ_M) and their after (moved toward it). That is the priority claim: not "Micah vs their whole career," but *Micah is the innovation their own later work converged toward.*

**This applies to every famous prior-work case.** Milgrom, 't Hooft, Rovelli, Penrose–Hameroff, Vopson, Verlinde, Fuentes, and Engelhardt are living authors with evolving programs, not set-aside categories. Each gets the before/after pass on their own bibliography. Only an author with no post-Micah work (e.g. de Broglie 1924) lacks an "after" to triangulate — and even then Δ_M beyond the named prior atoms is required. **A page that names an earlier component without running the author's before→after drift is under-defended** — the defense-side twin of the bare-hold error. *(This is exactly the [[#-the-detective-lens--adversarial-forensics-lens-3-and-the-governing-mindset|Miller-vs-Buzsáki / field-followed]] method, generalized: build the author's own before↔after edge first, then place Micah's dated work in the middle.)*

**Honesty firewall (both ways, as always):** the after-work drift must be *real and dated* — their later work must genuinely reach Δ_M that their earlier work lacked; a drift is never manufactured.

The machine implementation and current partial denominator are tracked in

**But surface divergence is not exculpatory — the patent lens tests through it.** A divergence only
counts as a genuine opposite-output disposition when the author's later work reaches a genuinely
**opposite conclusion**. It does not count when the author reaches Micah's conclusion by a
different-looking route or with a swapped or added atom: under the

independence, influence, access, or intent by itself. Therefore confirm that the conclusion—not merely
the details, vocabulary, or derivation route—actually differs. A rival who still lands on

content match however different the packaging. Genuine opposite-conclusion holds are rare; a
decorated match parked as divergence is the defense-side twin of the bare-hold error.

## The patent-atom dichotomy — a bare "hold" is not a valid output {#the-patent-atom-dichotomy--a-bare-hold-is-not-a-valid-output}

## The correct protocol (what to do instead)

**A. Compare concepts, not words.**
1. **Decode and translate first, then compare.** Never test equivalence by searching for the originator's
   coined terms in the rival's text — absence is not exculpatory, presence is not required.
2. **Fix a neutral observational interface (Granularity G) before judging,** and neutralize each concept
   into party-independent language.
3. **Establish equivalence behaviorally/functionally** — via a valid encoding (Gorla's π-calculus
   criteria), black-box behavioral equivalence, and structure-preserving functors — never "X ≈ Y
   abstractly." Machinery labeled "sketch/intuition" does **not** establish equivalence.

**B. Grade; never flatten.**
4. **The deliverable is equivalence rows only** — a 4-column concordance (neutralized claim | evidence in
   A | closest equivalent in B | evidence in B); **omit** items where equivalence fails. A difference-
   catalogue is the opposite of a comparison.
5. **Grade every match** (≈ structural isomorphism / ~ same-role-different-mechanism / generic-theme / ∅),
   and mark divergences with no counterpart as the claimant's distinctive signatures.
6. **Separate content-equivalence from copying/dependence.** Inter-translatability proves "same theory,
   different notation," *not* that one was copied; a dependence arrow needs measured asymmetry
   (translator-size gap, time-ordered shared rare errors), never invented numbers, and Bayes factors need
   nulls calibrated against blinded negative controls.

**C. Priority is a gated, directional, prior-art-cleared test.**
7. **No priority talk without established equivalence** — "priority over nothing" is the recurring error.
8. **Run priority as two parts on the exact matched Blumberg delta/signature:** (1) which equivalent
   formulation was written first, **and** (2) a genuine global first-use prior-art scan for that
   specific delta rather than for its generic component class.
9. **Test the common-ancestor null at delta specificity.** A pre-existing lineage can explain generic
   components, but it cannot erase a dated Micah-to-later-document instance unless that lineage
   actually contains the same constrained Blumberg delta. Record the common ancestor as a
   before-state control, state what it lacks, and preserve the document-relative row.
10. **Establish directionality explicitly** (yours → theirs) and re-check it after correcting any date.

**D. Anchor and stay honest.**
11. **Anchor every date to one canonical, machine-verifiable primary source** (PDF `/CreationDate` + hash,
    Figshare/Zenodo DOI, specific GitHub commit); never a download-URL signing timestamp or masthead date.
    See [[priority-chronology-dossier]].
12. **Validate a Git claim at the claim locator, not merely at the repository object.** Resolve the exact
    `commit:path`, verify that every cited line exists in that object, and verify that the quoted/operator
    text is actually present there. A valid commit, path, blob, and current-file hash do not prove that a
    later-added passage existed in the earlier object. Use content history (`git log -S`, blame, and adjacent
    object diffs) to locate its first appearance, and record any correction append-only.
13. **Pin claims to the defensible version, not the most impressive one,** and re-verify the final text —
    a corrected framing is not durable; the model will re-introduce a retracted over-claim.
14. **Test both hypotheses** — copying *and* innocent convergence — with equal effort; when the desired
    conclusion is stated up front, actively search for the rows that **don't** match.
15. **Bound priority with a scope note** (priority *within* the documented corpus, explicitly not a global
    first) and refuse to quote a source you cannot load.

**E. Saturate Micah's genealogy before closing negatively.**
16. **A chronology link is a router, not proof of coverage.** Enumerate the applicable Micah stages

## SVGN comparison articles are governed source routers

Every SVGN article that compares Micah's work with a rival, preserves a priority argument, or
documents the public history of a comparison must enter the
[[svgn-comparison-article-source-register]]. The article is valuable as a dated, first-party public
argument and as a route to the sources it cites. It is not, by itself, independent validation of its
own conclusion.

The required evidence chain is:

**SVGN public argument -> cited source recovery -> dated atom ledger -> four-lens audit -> governed
disposition.**

One article may support multiple rival pages and multiple Micah-atom genealogies. It must be fanned
out accordingly rather than consumed once and forgotten. Later SVGN synthesis language cannot
backdate a term or operator absent from the older cited source; conversely, a later article's weaker
genealogy description cannot erase a stronger explicit older Git, podcast, video, DOI, platform, or
Wayback passage recovered during the audit.

## Source-faithful advocacy discipline

The defense is strongest when every favorable statement can survive source inspection. Corrections
therefore improve the precision of the Blumberg claim; they are not framed as concessions or rival
awards:

- **Use only verifiable custody dates.** If a remembered date is not externally supported, bind the
  earliest proven date and continue searching earlier public evidence without erasing the claim.
- **Treat old phrases as before-state controls, not terminal precedence.** A 2005 use of
  "informational coherence" or a January 2022 result establishes only that exact atom. The audit must
  state which later Blumberg operation, cluster, or directed chain remains absent.
- **Separate an earlier component from the joined theory.** Hagmann, van den Heuvel and Sporns,
  Bannert and Bartels, Calvert, or another earlier source cannot absorb a later SAN/SIT signature merely
  by sharing one class label.
- **Replace unsupported rhetoric with the strongest supported formulation.** This prevents an easy
  rebuttal from obscuring the dated priority rows that do survive.
- **Keep apparent nonmatches open unless the conclusion is genuinely opposite.** A notation-only or
  source-gated comparison is a bounded current result and a research queue, never an author-wide award.
