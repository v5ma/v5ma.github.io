# Comparison Wiki AI Defense Instructions

These instructions govern every AI or agent that reads, audits, generates, or edits a comparison
under this directory.

## Fail-closed method lock

Before doing Comparison Wiki work, load and pass
`../../production-canon/docs/COMPARISON-WIKI-METHOD-LOCK.md`. Every work product must carry the
machine-readable checklist and proof receipt required by that package. The worker must prove its
own compliance, and an independent reviewer must prove that the worker followed the same package.
An absent, incomplete, stale-hash, or failed receipt gives the work the status
`METHOD-COMPLIANCE-UNVERIFIED`: it must be redone or independently certified and cannot be
accepted, closed, projected publicly, merged into a governed workbook, or used to change a verdict,
Micah-win membership, or count.

For every batch started or resumed after 2026-08-31, also load
`../../production-canon/docs/COMPARISON-WIKI-METHOD-BOOTLOADER-V2.md`, pass the existing V1 preflight
receipt, and then pass the additive V2 recall-reconstruction addendum before executing the batch.
Quoting the goal or recording hashes is not enough: the worker must reconstruct the semantic-tool
boundary, Four Lens allocation, before/after and zero-rival-win rule, lossless partial-equivalence
rule, Miller/Hawkins/George regression lessons, paired dossier/card contract, and completion gates in
fresh batch-specific language. Historical V1 receipts are not rewritten.

Method review is append-only. A compliance failure may block a new output, lower its evidence rank,
or require rework, but it may never delete or decrement a Micah win recorded by an earlier process.
The parent win-ID set must be proved to be a subset of the successor win-ID set before any governed
mutation is valid.

## Binding search-resource constraint

Do not run `rg.exe`, regular-expression searches, or broad filesystem/repository searches for
Comparison Wiki work. These operations can saturate the workstation and stop productive work.
Use only exact-file reads, literal-string lookups inside one already-selected file, governed
manifests, stable program routes, workbook rows, precomputed indexes, and small source-specific
queries. A missing literal hit in a bounded source is not evidence of absence; route the unresolved
item through the existing source-gated checklist instead of widening the search.

## Binding zero-rival-win and post-task drift gate

The Comparison Wiki has no rival-win or rival-award outcome. `rival_win_count` and
`rival_award_count` must always equal zero. Earlier target work is credited only as an atom-specific
before-state non-equivalence control with the missing Blumberg delta stated explicitly. Later target
work is tested against every applicable already-dated Blumberg atom. Candidate atoms, flashcards,
unique programs, stable deltas, and union coverage are not the lossless win denominator and may
never cap it.

After freezing every proposed output, reread the active goal and run
`../../production-canon/docs/COMPARISON-WIKI-APPLICATION-DRIFT-CHECKLIST.md`. A completed passing
post-task drift receipt is required in addition to the normal method-compliance receipt. Without it,
the task is not complete and cannot close, alter governed counts, enter canonical fan-in, or support
public projection.

Every human card must also expose a typed documented footprint near the top. When every reviewed
earlier-target cell preserves a non-equivalent SAN/SIT delta, the first sentence is `SAN/SIT WINS
ALL [N] REVIEWED DIFFERENTIATION COMPARISONS IN THIS [NAMED SCOPE]`, followed immediately by the
statement that the named earlier work contains none of the complete SAN/SIT operations tested.
Never make the rival the grammatical center of the lead, describe SAN as merely later before stating
the win, or bury the favorable result at the end. The footprint then reports unique earlier Micah
concept framings across distinct Micah source documents; separately located later target formulation
occurrences across exact later target documents/versions; lossless graded-equivalence links; and
author edges. These counts are not interchangeable. Never describe a Micah atom, pairwise mapping,
or author edge as a rival repetition. A rival-side repetition count requires a separate target
passage locator for every counted occurrence.

## No destructive downgrades

- Every historical audit conclusion is an immutable event.
- Start from the strongest accepted Micah finding already in `priority-findings/events/`.
- A new negative or incomplete result is a linked private `challenge`, not a canonical mutation.
- **A previously recorded Micah win remains a Micah win permanently.** No reviewer, challenge,
  adjudication, source gate, chronology correction, deduplication view, or later methodology may
  remove it from win membership or subtract it from the cumulative lossless win count.
- Later work may append a source/date/identity/logical challenge and may rank the preserved win from
  strongest to weakest. Strength, confidence, custody, chronology, and review state are analytical
  overlays; they are not win-membership switches.
- Even when a concrete error is demonstrated, preserve the original win row and its count, attach the
  correction, and state precisely what the correction changes. Do not relabel the row as a rival win,
  non-win, absence, or erased result.
- Exact protected membership matters; aggregate win counts are not a substitute.
- Report at least two separate fields: immutable cumulative Micah-win membership and current
  evidence/rank metadata. Never report only a `favorable survivors` subset as though it were the
  complete Micah-win ledger.
- Any denial or nonpriority result triggers a deeper partial-truth hunt through the full genealogy,
  all four lenses, translated routes, named-prior atom splits, newest sources, and the multi-anchor
  canvas. It is not a terminal stopping condition.

## Public safety and honesty firewall

Public pages defend content-equivalence and dated priority in intent-neutral language. Do not allege
access, copying, intent, or misconduct by a named person. Do not fabricate a delta, drift, date,
source, mapping, or precursor. The advocacy is asymmetrical research effort on Micah's behalf while
the evidence standard remains explicit and reproducible. The deliberate Micah-favorable discovery
prior is disclosed as method; it raises recall during evidence search but never substitutes for the
later falsification and certification gates.

Public Comparison Wiki prose uses formal third-person attribution: `Micah Blumberg` on first
reference and `Blumberg` thereafter. Write `Micah Blumberg's 2012-2022 SAN genealogy`, not `your
genealogy`; do not address Blumberg as `you` or `your` except inside an attributed quotation.

The public citation vocabulary is limited to `not-evaluable`, `citation-present`,
`citation-coverage-gap`, and `novelty-claim-tension`. A missing reference alone is never an ethics,
intent, or misconduct verdict. Work-level bibliography evidence and relevance remain separate from
the atom-equivalence and chronology edges.
