## Dense execution lock

Before any new or resumed Comparison Wiki batch, complete and verify the Preflight Recall Receipt by reloading and hashing the active goal; the full Four Lens Comparison Stack; the bounded Jigsaw, TurboVec, Operational-Equivalence Census, Fit-Pattern Census, Major Cluster Signature Census, JigsawRank, Micah-genealogy, and Book Atlas routes; the `before target -> missing Blumberg delta -> after target` and categorical zero-rival-win rule; the exact Micah-stage, target-version, target-atom, and parent-win denominators; the current Miller, Hawkins, and Dileep George regression packets; and the paired exhaustive-dossier plus eighth-grade flashcard/timeline contract—then materialize every in-scope Micah-stage x target-version x target-atom cell, preserve every full or partial favorable mapping and author edge, treat automated `no-fit` as a non-veto and every unresolved cell as open, and finish only after exact-passage Lenses 1–3, the separate program-level Lens 4/Longitudinal Signature-Migration analysis, source and citation adjudication, independent review, meta-audit, and post-task application-drift verification; without a passing preflight receipt, the batch is invalid and must not begin.

This sentence is intentionally dense. It is an executable index into the full protocol, not a substitute for reading it.

## Why this gate exists

Application drift has occurred when a worker remembered the broad mission but silently dropped one of its operational requirements: a semantic-search route, an older or mature Micah formulation, a target version, the before/after asymmetry, partial-equivalence preservation, a methods-level atom, the distinction between exhaustive evidence and a short card, or a regression lesson learned from earlier programs. A post-task checklist can detect some of that damage, but the cheaper correction is to force the complete method back into working memory before the batch begins.

The gate does not require memorizing a stale win count. It requires loading the latest authoritative receipt or packet by exact path and hash, recording the current count snapshot when one exists, and recalling the methodological lesson that the regression case protects.

## Regression fixtures are instructions, not score targets

- **Miller** protects against representative-set compression and the false assumption that an initial small result is a defensible ceiling.
- **Hawkins** protects the atom-splitting and before/after rule: a generic earlier component does not swallow a later joined Blumberg delta.
- **Dileep George** protects against application drift during the current work: target registers must include methods and implementation operations, and inherited semantic `no-fit` labels are non-authoritative.

Load the current authoritative fixture through the [goal link map](COMPARISON-WIKI-GOAL-LINK-MAP.md) or the active program packet. Do not hard-code an old count into the standing goal.

## Dual-output contract

The exhaustive comparison and the short flashcard are paired projections of the same evidence graph:

1. **Expert dossier:** lossless row ledger, exact passages, all source versions, translations, Four Lens proofs, residuals, citations, chronology, signatures, author edges, validations, and open cells.
2. **Human card:** the strongest visible sentence pair or double pair, the chronology result in plain language, graded equivalence after translation, the decisive residual or earlier missing delta, a typed documented-footprint panel, and direct expansion into the dossier and longitudinal formulation ladder.

The card is not the evidence denominator. The dossier is not allowed to become unreadable public copy. Neither output may contradict the other.

## Validity states

- `PREFLIGHT-RECALL-VERIFIED`: every required document and route is loaded and hashed, all fourteen checks pass with evidence, prohibited counters equal zero, the worker certifies the receipt, and batch execution is explicitly authorized.
- `PREFLIGHT-RECALL-UNVERIFIED`: anything is missing, stale, unfilled, unavailable without an open-cell boundary, or not yet certified. Work must not begin.
- `PREFLIGHT-RECALL-FAILED`: a forbidden premise is present—for example rival wins, a candidate ceiling, a predecessor floor, an automated `no-fit` veto, an ontology/context exclusion, destructive membership loss, or broad-search authorization. Work must not begin and the proposed setup must be repaired.

Preflight proves that the complete method was reloaded before execution. It does not certify the later output. Method compliance and the post-task application-drift gate remain mandatory after the work is frozen.
