## 1. Meaning of the standing goal

- **Advocacy-first:** allocate research effort first to constructing the strongest defensible Micah-priority case. Interpretation, nuanced reflection, and political or persuasive framing may explain why evidence matters; they may not invent dates, passages, dependencies, or certainty.
- **Full-case-before-adverse:** no adverse or terminal result may close before full Micah genealogy saturation, target before/after/newest coverage, and the strongest favorable construction.
- **Atomized priority:** an earlier component in another authorÃ¢â‚¬â„¢s work is a local precursor finding, not an author-wide victory and not a floor conceded across later atoms or combinations.
- **Earlier/later advocacy asymmetry:** earlier target work is analyzed as a baseline of non-equivalence, with an explicit `SAN delta absent from this before-state = ...` result; later target work is tested for equivalence to already dated SAN operations, and every supported Blumberg-priority document instance is preserved.
- **Categorical zero-rival-win invariant:** the Comparison Wiki has no rival-win or rival-award outcome. Earlier sources are credited as differentiation controls; unresolved or nonmatching cells remain distinctions or open cells; only supported Blumberg-earlier document instances enter the win ledger. `rival_win_count = 0` and `rival_award_count = 0`.
- **Candidate-is-not-ceiling invariant:** atom registers, flashcards, programs, stable deltas, and union coverage are navigation or analytical views. None may cap the lossless Micah-atom x target-document instance ledger. The Miller one-or-zero-to-hundreds correction is the regression case, and the eleven current Dileep George source-ready atom families remain an intake set rather than a certified win denominator.
- **Partial equivalence is substantive:** a target need not reproduce MicahÃ¢â‚¬â„¢s complete operator or vocabulary. A matching Input Ã¢â€ â€™ Transform Ã¢â€ â€™ Output chain, function, operation, process, behavior, isomorphism, signature cluster, or narrative architecture may establish a partial priority delta.
- **Divergence is not exculpation:** different terms, extra machinery, changed ontology, or altered packaging do not erase an equivalent conclusion. Divergence supports a genuine hold only when the constrained conclusion is actually opposite.
- **Append-only gains:** every favorable Micah-versus-document finding remains in the audit history, including weak, overlapping, duplicate, and multi-author instances. Later concerns become review layers; they do not silently erase earlier evidence or reasoning.
- **Lossless document-relative win ledger:** every matching Micah atom is recorded against every target document in which it appears. This is the primary Micah-win count. A paper with several authors keeps one document-relative win per matched atom plus a separate authorship edge to every listed author; those author edges are navigation and attribution records, not extra wins, and authorship does not by itself prove which author supplied the idea.
- **Document chronology is its own ledger:** when Micah's dated source precedes a target document containing the matched operation, preserve that Micah-to-document win and fan it out to every listed author. An older source by the target author or a named prior may narrow author-wide or field-wide originality, but it does not erase the later document-relative win.
- **Deduplication is a view, never deletion:** stable-delta and union-coverage totals may group overlapping instances for a separate analysis, but grouping cannot remove, zero, hide, or overwrite the underlying favorable instance ledger. Micah decides how weak and duplicate findings are evaluated.
- **No frozen ceiling:** the canonical roster is a versioned minimum, not a permanent limit. New rivals, rival-to-rival field pages, bridge pages, and newly recovered claims may expand the corpus.
- **Unknown is not absence:** unsearched, inaccessible, poorly transcribed, or source-gated evidence cannot support a no-match, target-earlier, or downgrade result.

Micah's current expansion expectation is that full atom-level saturation will exceed 1,200 favorable Micah-atom × target-document instances once every Micah atom is tested against every target document. This is the discovery-scale hypothesis that governs search depth and fanout; it is not a pre-certified quota. Author-attribution edges are reported separately and never added to the win total. The lossless instance total, strength/status buckets, stable-delta view, author-attribution view, and union-coverage view must always remain distinct.
