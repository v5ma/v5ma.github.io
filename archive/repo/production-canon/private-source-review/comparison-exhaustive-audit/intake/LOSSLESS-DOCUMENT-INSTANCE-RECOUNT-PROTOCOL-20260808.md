# Lossless Document-Instance Recount Protocol

Status: binding private-audit protocol; corpus-wide reconciliation open  
Owner: DSR - Comparison Wiki Expansion  
Date: 2026-08-08  
Protocol ID: `LDIR-1`

## Purpose

Earlier Comparison Wiki work often summarized a result at the author, theory,
program, or paper-family level. That representation can be useful for reading,
but it systematically undercounts the dated relations that matter to Micah
Blumberg's priority record.

Every legacy comparison must therefore be **reconciled**, but it does not need
to be researched from zero. The recount has two speeds:

1. **fast deterministic reprojection** of already source-bound findings; and
2. **slow substantive re-audit** only where the preserved evidence is
   incomplete, collapsed, source-gated, or methodologically obsolete.

The objective is the strongest favorable case the complete dated record can
support, followed by adversarial source and method checks. No evidence may be
invented, no date may be moved backward, and no unknown cell may be used as an
adverse finding.

## Counting unit

The primary lossless unit is one source-bound relation:

```text
Micah Blumberg source stage
  x target document or separately dated version
  x target atom, operation, arrow, output, or bounded assembly
  x lens result
```

Preserve all supported instances, including:

- separate 2011, 2012, 2017, 2021, 2022, 2024, 2025, and 2026 Blumberg stages;
- separate preprint versions, journal versions, books, chapters, talks, posts,
  videos, and other independently dated target documents;
- weak, partial, overlapping, repeated, and duplicate document instances;
- every atomic row plus any additional supported signature-cluster row; and
- every named-author attribution edge as a separate navigation view.

Author-attribution edges are not added to the document-instance win count.
Grouped, deduplicated, stable-delta, program, and union-coverage views are
analytical projections of the lossless ledger. They may never erase its rows.

## Fast path: deterministic reprojection

A legacy comparison qualifies for the fast path when its relevant claims are
already bound to exact sources and dates. Reuse the existing:

- source hashes and custody receipts;
- extracted passages and page or line locators;
- Micah Blumberg genealogy stages;
- target-document manifests and version histories;
- target atom registries;
- four-lens mappings and formal translations;
- accepted favorable findings and challenge history; and
- validation receipts.

Then perform these mechanical steps:

1. Enumerate each separately dated target document or version.
2. Enumerate each applicable dated Blumberg stage.
3. Expand every supported relation into a distinct stage-by-document-by-atom
   row.
4. Preserve atomic rows and additive signature-cluster rows separately.
5. Fan each document row out to its listed authors in the attribution-edge
   view without multiplying the win count.
6. Mark every unreviewed matrix cell `unknown` or `source-gated`, never
   `absent`, `no-match`, `target-earlier`, or adverse.
7. Recompute the grouped and union views without deleting the lossless rows.
8. Validate unique identifiers, source resolution, date order, row counts,
   and append-only continuity.

This path should be much faster than the original comparison because it
reformats and checks preserved evidence rather than rediscovering it.

## Slow path: substantive re-audit triggers

A comparison must enter the slow path for every affected cell if any of the
following is true:

- the result exists only as an author-level or program-level summary;
- one target paper stands in for multiple dated versions or documents;
- the target's before, after, or newest work is missing;
- Micah Blumberg's full applicable genealogy was cited but not passage-tested;
- early vocabulary was not translated into neutral operations;
- a predecessor was treated as an author-wide or theory-wide floor;
- a partial match was denied because the full later operator was absent;
- divergence or renamed machinery was treated as exculpatory;
- the four lenses or their associated formal translations were not applied;
- the comparison depended only on a title, abstract, snippet, old low-quality
  transcript, or unavailable source;
- the target document, date, authorship, or provenance is uncertain;
- an earlier favorable result was overwritten, collapsed, or omitted; or
- a challenge exposes a questionable mapping, date, or interpretation.

The slow path starts with source-faithful intent recovery and full-genealogy
saturation. It then reruns equivalence, narrative architecture, detective, and
Secret Follower longitudinal analysis, with category-theory and process-
calculus translations where applicable.

## Advocate-first order of operations

For every target:

1. Construct the strongest source-backed Blumberg-priority case.
2. Search every applicable Blumberg era using era-appropriate vocabulary.
3. Compare every target stage, including the newest source.
4. Work backward from matching conclusions and assemblies to detect renamed or
   redistributed operations.
5. Preserve each favorable document instance immediately in an append-only
   candidate ledger.
6. Apply adversarial checks to its date, source, mapping, named priors, and
   alternative explanations.
7. Add limitations or challenges as metadata and new events; never silently
   delete the earlier row.
8. Promote only the conclusions that pass the required review gates.

Predecessor evidence is a differentiation control. It identifies what an
earlier source contains and, crucially, which Blumberg distinction it lacks.
It cannot terminate the search for later document-relative Blumberg priorities.

## Required outputs per comparison

Each reconciled comparison must expose:

- a target-document and version manifest;
- a Micah Blumberg source-stage manifest;
- a target atom registry;
- a complete applicable stage-by-document-by-atom matrix;
- a lossless favorable candidate ledger;
- an additive signature-cluster ledger or rows;
- a separate author-attribution edge view;
- grouped stable-delta and union-coverage views;
- before/after/newest chronology;
- four-lens and formal-translation review;
- challenge and revision history;
- source, method, and meta-audit status; and
- a deterministic validation receipt.

## Regression rules

- Prior favorable rows are append-only evidence records.
- A later challenge adds review metadata through a new event; it does not erase
  the earlier finding, remove its Micah-win membership, or subtract its count.
- A grouped summary may shrink through deduplication, but the lossless ledger
  cannot shrink. A source-integrity correction is appended to the affected win
  and may alter its wording, confidence, or rank, never its preserved count.
- A missing or unsearched source cannot support an adverse conclusion.
- An older aggregate is not an authoritative corpus total until it has passed
  this recount.
- A recount is not permission to inflate evidence strength: weak and partial
  rows remain labeled weak and partial while remaining visible for Micah
  Blumberg's evaluation.
- Rank preserved wins from strongest to weakest in a separate analytical view.
  `strong`, `medium`, `weak`, `challenged`, `source-gated`, and `unranked` are
  evidence or review overlays, not membership-removal states.
- No public accusation about copying, access, intent, misconduct, or fraud may
  be inferred from content equivalence alone.

## Corpus scheduling

Process the corpus in neuroscience-first order. Within that lane, prioritize:

1. high-profile close rivals;
2. comparisons known to contain author-level or theory-level compression;
3. before/after/newest cases;
4. published comparison papers that may have inherited an undercount;
5. source-rich cases suited to fast reprojection; and
6. source-gated cases requiring slow recovery.

Batch fast-path comparisons through deterministic tooling. Keep slow-path
research bounded to the flagged documents, genealogy stages, and matrix cells.

## Completion test

A legacy comparison is reconciled only when:

- every applicable target document and version has its own identity;
- every applicable Blumberg stage has been passage-tested rather than merely
  cited;
- every supported atomic and cluster relation has a lossless row;
- unknown cells remain explicit;
- all four lenses and associated methods have recorded dispositions;
- source, date, and identifier validation passes;
- prior favorable findings remain recoverable; and
- independent review and meta-audit status are visible.

Until then, any prior author-, paper-, theory-, or program-level number is a
navigation summary, not a ceiling on Micah Blumberg's priority record.
