# Full-Genealogy Saturation Gate — 2026-07-28

Owner: DSR — Comparison Wiki Expansion

Status: active Comparison-method gate; no verdict or count changes by itself

## Systemic failure being corrected

The Carhart-Harris re-audit exposed a reusable failure:

```text
genealogy acknowledged
-> early work treated as background
-> search biased toward later coined terminology
-> one Micah snapshot compared with one target snapshot
-> adverse or mixed result formed before early operational language was translated
```

The mistake is not limited to Carhart-Harris. It can affect any comparison where Micah developed an
operation across changing vocabularies and media. A chronology page can be present in the
bibliography while its passages remain untested.

## Governing rule

No `target-earlier`, `mixed-split`, `certified-insufficient`, `no-match`, `no-priority`, or
downgrade may close until the audit proves passage-level coverage of the full applicable Micah
genealogy.

The required object is:

```text
Micah stage × target stage × target atom
```

Every cell must be one of:

- `match`;
- `partial`;
- `different`;
- `opposite`;
- `not-found-after-bounded-search`;
- `source-gated`.

`unknown`, unsearched, or source-gated cells are not negative evidence.

## Required packet

1. Exact target identity, versions, earliest relevant body, and newest body.
2. Target atom, causal-arrow, output, and narrative-architecture registry.
3. Applicable Micah-stage registry, including 2011, 2012, 2017, 2021, 2022, 2024, 2025, and
   2026 when sources exist.
4. Source-family coverage manifest for public recordings, Git history, podcasts, Recorder,
   Medium/SVGN/Substack/WordPress/Wayback, Zenodo/OSF, books, papers, images, and governed private
   discovery sources when applicable.
   Every comparison-oriented SVGN.io, Medium/VRMA, Substack, and WordPress article must have an
   inventory row and a reviewed route to one or more candidate `program_id` values, or an explicit
   unresolved-routing status. Existing authored comparisons are mandatory source leads; their
   underlying sources still require independent verification.
5. Era-vocabulary dictionary built from Micah's exact wording.
6. Exact passage ledger with source path or URL, locator, speaker, source hash when local, and date
   class.
7. Full stage-by-stage atom matrix.
8. Separate internal-event, owner-metadata, public-platform, Git-fixation, archive-custody, and later
   publication dates.
9. Lossless favorable document-instance ledger, including weak, overlapping, duplicate, and
   multi-author instances. A multi-author paper keeps the document edge and an authorship edge to
   every listed author; authorship is not evidence of individual intellectual contribution.
10. Separate stable-delta and union-coverage views. Deduplication may group the analytical view but
    may never delete, hide, zero, or overwrite the lossless instance ledger.
11. Four-lens results, named-prior allocation, apparent-prior provenance check, target-only
    residuals, independent review, and meta-audit. Every named-prior allocation is atom-specific
    `before-state-boundary` metadata, not a rival award or author-wide floor. It must state both the
    strongest exact earlier operation and the Micah Blumberg delta absent from that before-state,
    then losslessly test every later target document against the dated Blumberg delta.

## Closure receipt

The packet must report:

- target-stage denominator;
- target-atom denominator;
- Micah-stage denominator;
- expected matrix-cell count;
- completed matrix-cell count;
- unresolved matrix-cell count;
- source families searched;
- source families not searched or inaccessible;
- comparison-article denominator, reviewed count, program-linked count, and unresolved routes;
- alternate-era queries run;
- positive deltas recovered;
- exact before-state operations and their source/date bindings;
- Micah Blumberg deltas absent from each before-state;
- confirmation that no before-state boundary became a rival award, terminal precursor status, or
  author-wide floor;
- later target documents tested against each applicable dated Blumberg delta;
- negative cells supported by bounded searches;
- independent reviewer;
- meta-audit status.

The gate passes only when all applicable cells have a defensible status and the independent
meta-audit confirms that early vocabulary was translated rather than keyword-tested.

## Date and publication boundary

Early functional wording may establish an early bounded operation without moving the date of a
later coined term. Public claims use the strongest reproducible date grade actually supported.
Private archives may guide discovery but are not silently represented as public custody.

## Retroactive program

Apply the gate to all 327 canonical programs. Prioritize:

1. target-earlier dispositions;
2. terminal mixed-split dispositions;
3. certified-insufficient/content-insufficient dispositions;
4. no-match/no-priority rows;
5. attempted downgrades and unresolved challenges;
6. favorable rows that still undercount Micah's dated partial deltas.

All prior favorable events remain immutable. The gate can add support, add bounded deltas, narrow a
claim through formal adjudication, or leave a challenge open. It cannot silently delete history.

## Carhart-Harris regression case

The initial re-audit treated Micah's 2011-2012 work primarily as ancestry. Passage recovery from
public 2012 Neo Mind Cycle recordings then identified bounded operational candidates involving:

- familiar or expected background with low recruitment;
- novel, rare, important deviations producing propagated coordinated activity;
- internally supplied predictive projection;
- synchronized versus out-of-sync population relations; and
- recursive EEG/light/sound feedback.

These candidates still require atom-specific prior-art, target-stage, and date adjudication. Their
recovery proves the prior audit was not genealogy-saturated and therefore could not support an
adverse terminal result.
