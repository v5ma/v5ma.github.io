# Comparison Encyclopedia research archive

This branch preserves work on Micah Blumberg's SAN and SIT Comparison Encyclopedia and provides a theoretical continuation handoff. It is a standalone archive branch, not a website deployment or authorization for an outside agent to operate the original workstation or services.

**Archive assembly is in progress. This first tranche is not a complete backup of the working corpus.** It contains the exact goal, its directly linked authorities, core method dependencies, the 53-file reader preview, and selected current research/review records. Remaining corpus inventory, disclosure review and private-backup transfer must be completed and verified separately.

## Research status

The archive includes reviewed material and work in progress. A preserved record is not automatically a verified priority, an independent invention, a citation omission, proof of influence, or validation of a whole theory. Historic instructions and review events are retained as records of the project's development, not presented as established scientific conclusions. Current evidence qualifications and correction overlays must be read alongside earlier records.

The new reader branding and introduction passed static review; browser acceptance and final release remain open. The Motivation comparison has seven independently reviewed and meta-reviewed pairs: four bounded partials and three weaker components or analogies. This is not ten distinct certified priorities and not completion of the seven-subject expansion.

## Disclosure boundaries

Third-party PDFs, full-paper extractions/page images, credentials, browser profiles, unrelated private conversations and unresolved disclosure material are not intentionally included. Original papers must be obtained from their publishers or repositories using the recorded citations, versions and hashes. This archive does not grant rights to third-party works.

Research records may retain original local paths for provenance. A local path is not a public download link. Use the file index and the linked goal; missing dependencies remain missing until recovered, not scientifically absent. No claim is made that all internal links or original workstation commands are portable yet.

No blanket open-source or third-party redistribution licence is granted by this backup. Permission and licence classification remains a separate task.
