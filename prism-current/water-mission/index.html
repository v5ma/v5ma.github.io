NEXUS AQUATIC RESEARCH / RECOVERY ASSIGNMENT

The water is the way in.

Restore auxiliary power, drain the intake basin and recover a prism from the submerged maintenance chamber. Return it to the arrival console to complete the mission.

You can walk, wade and swim. Your air refills at the surface. Running out returns you to a safe checkpoint without losing recovered objectives. Use the arrival ladder or the sloping ramp to leave the pool.

Return to rhythm game
Controls, water quality and sound

Xbox: left stick moves, right stick looks, X interacts, RT or A rises, LT or B dives, RB runs on dry land, Y toggles the torch. Menu pauses. View opens the mission briefing. In menus, use the D-pad, A to select and B to return. An initial tap or keypress may be needed to enable browser audio.

Keyboard: WASD moves; arrows look. Hold and drag on the scene to look, or click "Capture mouse" for first-person mouse look. E interacts, Space rises, C dives, Shift runs, F toggles the torch. P or Escape pauses. No jumping or combat is needed.

This browser mission uses its own checkpoint save. Rhythm scores, lessons and Practice Lab records are not changed. Headset locomotion is not included.
