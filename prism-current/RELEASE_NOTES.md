# Prism Current v0.7.0 / Spectral Observatory

Open Controls, sound & comfort and choose Spectral atmosphere. Opal Aurora adds layered jade/violet curtains. Solar Silk adds amber/rose orbital filaments. Deep Current adds flowing blue/turquoise interference. Classic Jewelbox restores the original look.

A luminous diffraction halo surrounds the distant orbit, while tiny inner jewel seeds gain shifting opal-like interference. Successful hits create hand-colored floor rings and a secondary echo. Note positions, outer crystals, direction glyphs, collision geometry and scoring remain unchanged.

Theme and hit-ripple choices persist separately from scores and are reachable through standard-controller navigation. Quiet mode freezes the new shader motion and disables its hit reactions. Light, zero intensity and immersive sessions disable the new effects. AR/VR retain their original lightweight rendering and transparent AR composition.

All four songs, Practice Lab, First Steps, mixer controls and records are retained. Three shared planes, five materials and six bounded impact slots add no new post-processing targets, sampled camera textures or per-hit lights. These resource limits do not establish a consumer-hardware frame-rate guarantee.
