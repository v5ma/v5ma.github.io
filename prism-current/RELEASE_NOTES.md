Select Tidal Bloom from the four-track catalog. # Prism Current v0.3.0 / Control Room

This upgrade continues the existing Jewelbox game. It does not replace the three original tracks, the scoring rules, the blade-collision system or the stored local records.

## Play with a standard controller

Choose Xbox practice, or press Menu from track selection to start that mode. The four lanes from left to right are LT, LB, RB and RT. This is timing practice, not an emulation of tracked physical sword direction. Its scores are stored separately from keyboard, mouse/touch, AR and VR records.

Use D-pad or left stick to move focus, left/right to adjust a setting, A to select, B to go back or pause, Menu to start/resume, and View to open the sound mixer. A disconnected controller pauses its run and never automatically resumes it. Release held controls after reconnecting. Standard gamepads do not drive immersive saber gameplay.

Browser or operating-system security dialogs may require a genuine initial tap or keypress to unlock sound. That is separate from in-game controller navigation.

## Control the mix

Music and sound effects have separate saved volume settings. Effect frequency and hit-text frequency are independently adjustable. Calm mix reduces hit feedback; Music only removes hit sounds and routine hit messages without disabling important status/error notices. Mute affects both audio buses.

Opening the mixer pauses a running song. Closing it does not start the song again. Mixer focus stays inside the dialog and background controls cannot be clicked through it. The soundtrack transport rejects obsolete resume requests and keeps a single music source; hit effects have a simultaneous-voice cap.

## Follow the production path

The game links to `AAA_CHECKLIST.md`, which organizes reliability, original music/chart production, accessibility, visuals/performance, progression/creation and release readiness. It includes task IDs, priorities, responsible roles, dependencies, completion criteria and explicit physical-testing gates. `ROADMAP.md` preserves the history and links to this checklist. `QA.md` explains tests and points to the release receipts in PR #112.

Local-best quality targets at 50%, 75% and 90% provide an immediate practice goal. They reflect the saved best-score record for the selected track/chart/input, not a new permanent-unlock system.

The next major content milestone is one flagship original arrangement with intentionally authored movement phrases and breathing space. More tracks should follow that quality bar, not merely add overlapping sound layers.

Physical Xbox and Quest acceptance remain open. AAA-quality is a production goal, not a claim of console certification, a studio-scale budget or completed hardware qualification.
