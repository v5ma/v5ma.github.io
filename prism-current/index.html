AR preview: Quest-compatible WebXR browser required. Clear your arm’s reach and use your headset boundary. No room scan, physical occlusion or hand tracking. No automatic walking or ducking.

Controls, sound & comfort

Mouse: hold left mouse for mint / L, right for coral / R. Swipe in the arrow direction. A dot accepts any direction. Touch: choose a blade with the L/R button, then drag. Keyboard: select keyboard mode and tap D, F, J, K as notes reach the near line; direction is not graded in this separate mode. P / Esc: pause.

Quest: physically swing the two tracked controllers. Trigger selects menu buttons. Grip or B pauses. Recenter only from the paused spatial menu, after checking your real space. You do not need to squeeze the trigger while slicing.

Three original locally synthesized scores. No imported commercial music or Moon Rider song downloads. Scores stay on this device and are separated by input mode. Headset performance and comfort need physical testing.
