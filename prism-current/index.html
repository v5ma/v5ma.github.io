Controls, sound & comfort

Cinematic / reflected studio light

Mouse: hold left mouse for mint / L, right for coral / R. Swipe in the arrow direction. A dot accepts any direction. Touch: choose a blade with the L/R button, then drag. Keyboard: select keyboard mode and tap D, F, J, K as notes reach the near line; direction is not graded in this separate mode. P / Esc: pause.

Xbox practice: the four lanes, left to right, are LT, LB, RB, RT. This grades timing, not physical sword direction, and has separate local records. D-pad or left stick moves menu focus; left/right adjusts settings; A selects; B goes back or pauses; Menu starts or resumes; View opens the mixer. Menu starts Xbox practice from track selection. Release buttons after connecting. Browser security may require one keyboard press or tap to unlock audio; operating-system and headset permission dialogs remain outside game control.

Quest: physically swing the two tracked controllers. Trigger selects menu buttons. Grip or B pauses. Recenter only from the paused spatial menu, after checking your real space. You do not need to squeeze the trigger while slicing. Standard gamepads are disabled during immersive sessions.

Three original locally synthesized scores. No imported commercial music or Moon Rider song downloads. Scores stay on this device and are separated by input mode. Headset performance and comfort need physical testing.
