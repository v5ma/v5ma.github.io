Controls, sound and comfort

Mouse: hold left and drag through fruit arrows. F swaps L/R blades. Right mouse fires a laser. Q/E hold left/right shields. A/D sidesteps; S or Space crouches. P or Escape pauses. Arrow keys choose a slash direction; J/K also swing the left/right blade at the cursor.

Xbox: right stick aims; A/X swings left/right saber. D-pad selects cut direction, including diagonals. LT/RT fires lasers; LB/RB holds shields. Left stick sidesteps; B or stick-down crouches. Menu pauses. D-pad, A and B navigate menus.

Quest: actual controller swings cut the fruit. Trigger fires from that saber's aim. Grip replaces that saber with a shield; release to cut or shoot again. Lean or duck within your clear space to dodge. B/Y starts or resumes from the menu, and pauses during battle. Point and squeeze a trigger to select, or use either thumbstick to highlight an option and press A/X to confirm. Hand pinches can operate the paused menu; combat needs both controllers. No room scan or forced camera motion.

The red projectiles are hazards, not fruit. Shoot bombs at range or shield them. Raise a shield just before impact for a stronger reflection. Holding a shield blocks that hand's saber and laser.

Both chapters use the original 132 BPM Undertow score. Headset comfort and performance require real-device playtesting. Clear your arm's reach and use your headset boundary.
