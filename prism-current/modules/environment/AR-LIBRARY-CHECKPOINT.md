# AR-first module library checkpoint / 2026-09-22

Latest owner direction: AR is Prism's primary design and acceptance mode. Author floating islands, trees and cloud pockets for transparent-room play instead of treating the removal of all scenery as the final AR presentation. Preserve the existing Easy gameplay, health, healing, late bosses, controls and saved records. VR and screen are retained alternatives, not reasons to deprioritize AR.

This checkpoint saves two original reusable foundations: Toon 0.1.0 and Cloudlets 0.1.0, each with a classic/UMD script and an ES facade. Toon supplies bounded shared light-band materials. Cloudlets supplies seeded opaque mesh clouds with three prebuilt detail levels, central-viewer XR detail selection, bounded host-clock motion, near-viewer suppression and owned-resource cleanup. They are not Disney VDB assets, a volume renderer, Gaussian splats, a commercial-library copy, or a replacement game.

The new modules are intentionally NOT loaded by index.html and do not change the currently played game. AR island placement, runtime selection, contrast review and native Quest acceptance remain separate next integration work. The known source/public frame-pause failures are still open; no new rendering expense is silently added to that build.
