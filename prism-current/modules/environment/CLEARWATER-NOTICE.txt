The unpolarized dielectric Fresnel helper in water-detail.js and its GLSL
counterpart in water-optics.js are adapted from Clearwater's fresnel function.
The refracted-light flux bake is an original CPU implementation inspired by
Clearwater's documented light-space focusing. The seeded wave spectrum,
procedural pebble textures, module integration and tests are original here.
No embedded Clearwater images, camera, standalone renderer, FFT pipeline,
postprocessing or assets are included. This is not the complete Clearwater
renderer or a claim of identical realism/performance.
