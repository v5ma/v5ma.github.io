# Floodgate Recovery / Prism Current v0.8.0

This is a playable first-person expedition reached through Play the water mission in the existing Prism Current menu, or directly at water-mission/index.html. It adds an optional exploration mode; it does not replace the rhythm game or use the earlier generated concept image as the environment.

Restore the amber auxiliary console on the right of the arrival deck. Turn the red pump valve on the left to drain the intake pool and open its floodgate. Cross the shallow basin into the deep reservoir. Dive below the far arch, follow the cyan submerged guide lights and recover the prism. Surface, return through the intake and use the sloping ramp or arrival ladder to reach the return console. Installing the prism completes the mission.

Movement changes between walking, wading and swimming. Rise with Space/RT/A and dive with C/LT/B. Air lasts 32 simulation seconds underwater and replenishes at the surface. Air depletion returns to a safe checkpoint without losing completed objectives. A pause-menu checkpoint option provides the same recovery without deleting progress. Restart has an in-game confirmation. No jumping, enemy combat, VR locomotion or multiplayer is included in this first mission.

Xbox controls are left stick to move, right stick to look, X to interact, RT/A to rise, LT/B to dive, Y for the torch, RB to run on dry land, Menu to pause and View to open the briefing. D-pad navigation and A/B work in the mission menus. Keyboard controls are WASD, arrow-key or mouse-drag looking, E to interact, Space/C for vertical swimming, F for the torch and P/Escape to pause. Optional mouse capture is in settings; drag-look remains available when capture is denied. Touch movement, drag-look and action/rise/dive/torch buttons are included, but physical-device usability testing remains open.

The facility is real procedural 3D geometry: tiled pool walls and bottoms, sloped ramp, ladder, pump pipes, consoles, catwalk rails, signs, guide lights, floodgate and a recoverable prism. The water uses one reused 512x512 planar reflection target, wave normals, six movement-ripple slots and analytic refracted floor tiles. Tile caustics are stylized shader lighting, not ray-traced optics. Underwater fog and sound filtering distinguish submerged play. Light disables reflection rendering; the still-water option freezes decorative water/caustic motion. This is not a promise to reproduce the concept image pixel-for-pixel or a measured AAA hardware-performance claim.

Only prism-current.v1.water-mission and prism-current.water-settings.v1 are written by this route. Checkpoints store a validated stage, never arbitrary imported world positions. Rhythm scores, Practice Lab passes, lessons, the music catalog and spectral presets remain separate. Returning to the rhythm game unloads the expedition resources.

The Node suite tests an entire mission route, locks, dive clearance, air recovery, pause, malformed saves and exit ladders alongside the existing rhythm tests. The native browser suite navigates the full facility through emulated Xbox input while reading positions only; it does not assign progress, coordinates, oxygen or clocks. It checks shader compilation, actual drainage, pickup/return, disconnect/reconnect, save isolation and reload, and records a separate full-resolution pool screenshot. Local browser HTTP/WebGL restrictions mean production-renderer acceptance runs in GitHub Actions. Physical Xbox/touch, broad browser, sustained GPU and human readability/comfort acceptance remain open.

The source and publication receipts are in PR #141. The water workflow verifies exact deployed hashes and repeats the full mission against GitHub Pages. A failed run is not a passing test; consult the recorded conclusions. Rollback is a scoped revert on current master, never a reset that discards other games.
