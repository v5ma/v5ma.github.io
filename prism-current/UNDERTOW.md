# Main rhythm repair: Undertow 0.9.0

The owner rejected the result of the prior upgrades: the music was not enjoyable, the default notes were down arrows or dots, and requested improvements were not evident during ordinary play. Those observations remain the creative acceptance baseline. Prior automated pass counts did not establish enjoyment.

Diagnosis against repository master 686a0fd803ea5b0c20b5663fff3d792264428943: First Light/Flow was still the launch default. Its generator deliberately alternated down arrows and dots. The water expedition was a separate route. Spectral effects were disabled in all immersive sessions. The headset menu had no chart selector. This was a product-design failure, not evidence that the user had missed a menu.

This repair changes the MAIN rhythm game. Undertow is the new default at 132 BPM, with a newly synthesized electronic arrangement: punchier drum transients, snare/clap layers, syncopated bass, short chord stabs, a repeating lead motif, stereo delay, kick-responsive music ducking and contrasting sections. The source is undertow.js, not a renamed copy of First Light. It is still locally synthesized music, not a licensed commercial recording. Whether the music is enjoyable remains for the owner to judge.

Default Pulse has 286 targets. Flow has 164. Both have eight directional values and zero dots. Old direction index 6 still means a dot for old songs/lessons. New indices 7 and 8 add upward diagonals to the actual slice evaluator and glyph atlas. Hits require the correct hand and direction; keyboard/gamepad practice remains timing-only. Alternating hand assignments, paired return strokes, per-hand gaps of at least 0.45 seconds and quiet sections are implemented; they are not physical comfort certification. First notes follow a four-beat count-in rather than the old eight-beat count-in.

The pool hall is part of Undertow's rhythm venue in browser and VR. It uses shared tiled geometry, analytic Fresnel ceiling reflections, refracted tile-floor patterns, moving caustics, bounded cut-driven water ripples and the existing stationary runway. It is not a new traversal map, a physical water simulation or ray-traced refraction. AR retains transparent passthrough without opaque pool walls. The pool and its optical coordinates follow the recentered note frame. The older spectral venues remain for the old songs; Floodgate Recovery remains an optional expedition.

Play and XR entry are moved before the catalog. Idle headset A/X cycles Flow/Pulse; existing trigger-selection and grip/B pause controls are retained. The idle preview follows the selected chart rather than playing the old down/dot loop. Worker and modified runtime URLs advance together. Existing song, practice, lesson and water saves are preserved; Undertow uses its own track ID in the existing score format.

Static venue boxes are batched by material into five instanced draws. Fixed opaque hilt meshes are combined by shared material with their transformed bounds checked. Small decorative hilt rings use lower tessellation; blade endpoints and collision geometry remain unchanged. Undertow XR prioritizes the note glyphs and facets over per-note halos and sparkles. These explicit resource reductions are not a measured hardware-performance guarantee.

Source tests explicitly select First Light/Flow where a test is intended to preserve the previous experience; the new suite separately tests an untouched fresh launch. It exercises the new full song via normal emulated gamepad input, default chart, record persistence, VR pool rendering, headset chart changes and tracked saber contact, plus transparent AR. Pure tests exercise all directional slices, reversed strikes, chart coverage and finite audio. Completed browser contexts are closed before the next independent renderer case; the test does not alter game clocks, scoring windows or accepted hit thresholds. Failed traces remain recorded in PR #189.

The relevant shared-library direction is the Prism Current section of GAME-RECOMMENDATIONS.md: stationary embodied rhythm, phrase preparation and exit state, intentional lower-density mapping, headset note visibility and recoverable input. No missing library brief or unverified recommendation ID is treated as authority. Old checklist work remains recorded, but does not outrank the owner's rejected core experience. Next acceptance is actual owner listening/play feedback, plus physical Quest/Xbox comfort and ordinary-resolution performance. No further side mode is proposed by this repair.
