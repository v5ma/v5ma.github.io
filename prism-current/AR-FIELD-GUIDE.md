# AR Field Guide 0.1.0 / live-game integration checkpoint

This is an actual entry-point upgrade, not another library-only module or replacement showcase. The ordinary Prism index now loads the existing FlexSurface 0.1.0 and a thin river/field-guide.js adapter. Three original illustrated cards explain fruit, purple blocks and mint health supplies above the AR Rotunda. Guidance reads the actual difficulty profile, including healing amounts and direction requirements.

Cards are static curved geometry, attached to the existing world-anchored menu. They appear before an AR battle and on Controls while paused. All cards disappear during combat, busy loading, ordinary paused Battle/Sound views, screen play and VR. They do not own input, accept clicks, intercept a controller, move the camera or obscure the numerical health display during combat. Start/Resume remains the original tested panel, not a newly bent hitbox. The three textures are prepared in the existing loading path; unchanged menu frames do not redraw or deform them.

The entire guide uses 288 triangles across six prebuilt sheet geometries and three 512x384 textures. It does not add a full-screen shader, background, compositor pass, third-party UI package or new renderer. The borrowed materials and owned geometries/textures are released once at teardown. These are resource budgets, not measured Quest frame rates.

Friendly Current remains host 0.12.2 and AR Tide remains 0.1.1. Easy, all four difficulties, slower fruit/block launchers, late bosses, healing, old records, soundtrack, stage placement, menus and tracked input are unchanged. The existing islands, trees, clouds, grass and water optics are retained, including saved minimal scenery and transparent room visibility. No private hub, portal, other game or shared site infrastructure is changed.

Local checks before publication: 410 Node model/integration tests and 19 real bundled-Three resource/lifecycle observations pass. The local WebGL2 probe is unavailable. These are not rendered AR or physical-device claims. A dedicated short source/public native check exercises the actual guide, existing difficulty/ray controls, brief start/pause/Controls/music/resume/exit, stable placement, preserved records and an actual high-resolution emulated stereo capture. Public testing must first match the main entry and every loaded local script/style to the expected bytes. It is not a full-battle certificate; existing longer AR and boss journeys remain independently required and earlier frame-pause failures remain open.

Publication outcome will be recorded in this file's companion receipt and FUTURE-DIRECTION.md after actual checks. This source checkpoint is not advance approval. Physical Quest readability, sustained performance and owner acceptance remain separate.

Future HTML-backed settings, gesture ownership and the nine new chapter concepts remain unfinished.
