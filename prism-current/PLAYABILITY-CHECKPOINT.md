# Friendly Current 0.12.0 / integrated candidate

The owner's latest physical playtest changes the priority from scenic polish to easier, readable play. The first durable checkpoint 2d69f5ae50ec26c9603aa23713e688422dabbe04 saved profiles without loading them. This checkpoint integrates core, UI, models and records together. No PR or staging branch is used.

Easy is the new first-play default. Easy, Normal, Hard and Ultra Hard differ in object/enemy spacing, enemy residence, item travel, block damage, healing supply and boss endurance. Easy/Normal accept either blade and any fruit-cut direction; Hard/Ultra retain fruit direction while accepting either blade. There is no blade-color switching or color-match bonus yet. Easy and Normal omit spiked bombs; Hard and Ultra retain shootable/shieldable spiked bombs that should not be cut.

Ducks, toy boats and aircraft all throw repeated fruit and purple blocks during longer passes. No new red bolt is generated. Purple blocks can be cut, lasered or shielded. Health boxes are mint cases with a plus symbol; cut, shoot or touch them to heal once up to 100. They never punish a miss. Health supplies cannot resurrect a failed run. Easy/Normal/Hard/Ultra boxes restore at most 30/25/20/15 respectively, and easier modes supply more boxes.

Bosses have no actor or model in play before beat 152 (about 69 seconds into the current soundtrack). Admiral Quack has a crown, paddle wheels and twin fruit mortars; he enters from upstream, then exposes his engine. Mothership similarly arrives only in the final phrase. A clear still requires a real boss defeat and completion of the song.

A large numeric HEALTH gauge and thick fill bar are anchored in the scene below the action. The compact controller/floor display also shows explicit health. Damage and healing deltas name their cause. The gauge is not head-locked or pasted on the browser window. Difficulty has four buttons on a page of the existing spatial menu and a semantic select alternative. Current battle difficulty is immutable; changing it requires returning to chapter selection.

Prior River records under prism-current.river.records.v1 are never read or overwritten by the new writer. Difficulty-specific clears use prism-current.river.pacing.records.v1 with chapter/input/difficulty/Arcade-or-Cruise keys. Classic records and preferences remain. Water/fire/trees sources, shoreline, XR input collection, audio lifecycle, the renderer reset guard and timing safeguard are preserved.

All 348 local model/lifecycle/integration tests pass. Existing assertions for assigned-hand-only cuts, a boss at time zero and old-shaped new score keys were deliberately updated to the owner's superseding requirements, not hidden or bypassed. New tests simulate both chapters across all four modes, verify increasing pressure and no bolts, repeated aircraft throws, health collection/caps, legacy-data isolation, late boss damage and failed-state safety. The real Three object suites pass (coherence 19, water 37, fire 64, trees 83) plus eight health/model ownership checks. Local Chromium WebGL2 is unavailable, so this is not native gameplay or physical Quest acceptance.
