# Friendly Current 0.12.2

The owner's physical playtest supersedes the previous high-pressure encounter tuning. The goal is to play and learn the toybox comfortably, see remaining health, and choose higher pressure deliberately. This release changes gameplay on purpose, while retaining the scenic modules, music, control scheme and saved previous records.

Easy is selected when no valid difficulty preference exists. Choose Difficulty from the chapter menu before starting: four direct buttons offer Easy, Normal, Hard and Ultra Hard. The semantic controls also have a four-choice select. A saved valid preference is restored. Difficulty cannot change during a battle or relabel its score; return to chapter selection for a different encounter.

Easy sends fewer items and enemies, gives them longer passes and allows any-direction fruit cuts with either blade. Normal increases activity while keeping the same forgiving cut rule. Hard and Ultra Hard require the displayed fruit direction, use denser volleys and have fewer healing supplies. Either blade still earns the ordinary cut reward in all modes. Color switching and color-match bonuses are separate unfinished work. Ultra Hard is a new dense profile, not a byte-identical copy of the old missile version.

Ducks and aircraft now throw fruit and purple blocks repeatedly. All four profiles keep resident enemies on screen for 22 to 28 musical beats, instead of the prior 9 to 10 beats. The soundtrack itself is not slowed. Purple blocks may be sliced, shot or blocked by a shield. They hurt only on actual contact. No red bolt/missile is spawned. Spiked bombs remain only on Hard and Ultra Hard: shoot them from a distance or shield them, rather than cutting them. Easy and Normal omit those explosives.

Mint health cases display a plus symbol and heal when cut, shot or touched. They cannot damage the player and an expired box has no health penalty. A box heals once, capped at HEALTH 100. Easy provides five boxes restoring up to 30 each; Normal three at up to 25; Hard two at up to 20; Ultra Hard one at up to 15. Damage and healing are applied by the game rules, never by the HUD. A failed run cannot be revived accidentally by a later box in the same update.

A thick numeric HEALTH gauge is now attached to the game stage below the central action. It stays visible during a battle, is not attached to the headset, and accompanies the existing controller/floor status display. It explicitly says HEALTH, gives current/maximum values, fills the bar to the actual value and labels damage/healing changes. Low health changes the bar and message without flashing the whole view. The gauge stows when the pause menu opens. The text-control alternative retains its semantic health meter.

Neither boss exists in the playable scene at the start. At beat 152, about 69 seconds into the existing 89-second soundtrack, Admiral Quack or the mothership enters from farther away. The duck flagship now has a crown, paddle wheels and twin fruit mortars. After the four-beat entrance its target becomes vulnerable. Defeating the real boss and finishing the song is still required for a clear; merely surviving or enabling Cruise does not award victory.

Previous River records under prism-current.river.records.v1 are preserved byte-for-byte and left separate. New clears use prism-current.river.pacing.records.v1 with chapter, input, difficulty and Arcade/Cruise in each key. Existing Classic scores, lessons and expedition data are unchanged. The old records are not silently converted into Easy or Ultra Hard scores. Paused in-memory encounters still require the same explicit session/resume behavior; unfinished runs do not persist across page closure.

Recovery 0.12.1 removes unchanged status-canvas repaints, disables unnecessary mip generation at the same visible resolution and uploads the status textures before audio. Recovery 0.12.2 fixes a direct B/Y Resume being discarded immediately after a previous pointer action. Held-button edges, native/polled selection latches, controller/session checks and the existing frame-stall safeguard remain.

The full local model/lifecycle suite passes 359 checks, including the actual status painter and XR dispatcher fixtures. That count is not a native or physical-device result. Read PLAYABILITY-CHECKPOINT.md and qa/friendly-current-public.json for the actual source/public outcomes and their limits. Separate desktop software-rendering stalls remain recorded rather than hidden behind successful companion checks. The owner's next physical playtest determines whether the new density, slower movement and health display feel right.
