# Prism Current: owner-directed River toybox

Source baseline: 9e275225d0b7d082d44e1c50cb44309d289c21f4. The owner explicitly changed Prism's requested direction from a stationary abstract-note slicer to a rhythmic toybox with water-height phases, fruit-launching duck fleets, bombs, aircraft, saber lasers, grip shields and mothership combat. This newer game-specific requirement takes precedence over treating the older genre brief as a prohibition against ranged combat.

Hypothesis: three direct verbs (slice, shoot, shield), readable enemy silhouettes and changing wave families provide greater player expression than down/dot-only notes. The bounded change is two beat-authored main-entry battles; the former rhythm game is preserved at rhythm.html rather than deleted. No spatial map-connectivity quota is imported from the adventure games.

Canonical tasks are RV-01 through RV-06 in prism-current/AAA_CHECKLIST.md. Phrase approach, release and recovery remain relevant to slicing, while ranged attackers must telegraph actual incoming projectiles. Shield pose, collision, visible blocking and reflected counterattacks share the same game state. Rising decorative water must not move the player's XR camera.

Primary observation: owner playtest of the ordinary main entry, assessing whether cutting, shooting and shielding are readable, varied and enjoyable. Automated full-battle completion, saves, actual collision paths and transparent AR are distinct technical evidence, not a creative verdict. Physical Quest/Xbox/touch, unfamiliar-player feedback and sustained performance remain open.

See prism-current/RIVER_PRISM.md and PR #193 for exact mechanics, source/live test receipts, failed traces and scoped rollback. Preserve all earlier notes, saves and sibling-game work. The next iteration should respond to real play/listening feedback before adding another side mode.
