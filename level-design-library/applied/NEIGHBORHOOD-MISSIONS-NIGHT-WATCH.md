# Neighborhood Missions: Night Watch local application

The owner explicitly requested an Arkham Shadow-inspired direction while preserving the neighborhood game. This local candidate maps the work to LEVEL-01, XR-02, ACCESS-01/02 and a new optional ACTION-01, not a replacement roadmap. Existing residents, courier routes and safe public spaces remain.

The case gives existing verticality a second use: rooftop service access can shut down hijacked sentries without fighting, while a ground approach teaches guard timing, shield counters and pulse/smoke recovery. Both share investigation and relay outcomes. Gameplay menus/rays are hidden until requested, with direct actions, native confirmations and an optional hand board. This is an original two-sentry case, not an Arkham map, cast or campaign.

Read ../../svgn-planet/lantern/NIGHT-WATCH.md and its local evidence. 270 local tests and 102 preserved legacy hashes are narrow model/input evidence only. Twelve adapter checks use a fake renderer, not WebGL. Prepared browser suites have not passed, no physical device is certified, and no new remote commit or public release was made. A-frame was considered but not installed; sharing conventions does not require replacing the existing renderer.

Next opportunity is to test whether players understand the two solutions and use the kit without menus, followed by real headset gesture/comfort approval. Do not add a larger world or describe this candidate as a completed release.
