Vesperfall / Pilgrimage 0.17.0 / September 17, 2026.

The shared studio manual, quality framework, industry notes and Vesperfall recommendations were reconciled with master b74cc4d1849aded501b9f28576596efac5f5a407. Returning Bell, Open Line and Goldwind already exist; this release must not credit them as new work. The user's new direction is Goldwind by default and a procedural chapter pipeline, grounded in the supplied archery and place-mastery references.

The player-experience hypothesis is that learning a repeated mechanism relationship enables a different plan in a new arrangement. Two chapter families, The Lantern Causeway and The Ashen Archive, use compatible authored modules with explicit ordinary routes, conditional crossings, shooting lines, valid upper landings, defender roles and service-return connections. The archive's roof, columns and split stacks change visibility and aiming compared with the outdoor causeway. A gallery offers observation and reversible shutter control; a direct crossing opens a shared firing lane; a sheltered flank remains independent. The familiar return marker is visible through a gate that opens from its service side.

This is a bounded catalogue assembler, not a claim of arbitrary procedural architecture or recovered In Death source. Geometry, enemies, supplies and decoration use independent deterministic streams. Each expedition retains its seed, immutable layout ID, snapshotted tier and mechanism state. The new IDs are lantern-causeway-1 and ashen-archive-1. All three older readers remain. New Pilgrimage runs advance through both chapters and then into retained Endless; old saved expeditions do not silently change their geography or mode.

Goldwind is the default only where there is no valid explicit preference. Classic remains selectable and an existing saved Classic choice is honored. Xbox down/left arrow shortcuts, crossbow ownership, tracked archery, hand menus and AR suspension are preserved. The Architect's Table reconstructs the actual new chapter and shows discovered regions only. It remains inspection, not a full AR combat game or physical surface anchor.

The model suite uses real movement, production clearance queries and swept projectiles; end-to-end browser tests must use actual game controls and live resources. Initial geometry faults included stair lips, an upper-slab edge and an overlapping service descent. Those were corrected in geometry rather than hidden by player relocation. A test that tried to cross a separating wall now follows the real opened gate and arrival apron. The first remote candidate passed 230 models and workbook/source checks, but its push refused a stale branch; that run is not credited as published or browser-tested.

Human first/replay understanding, physical Quest/Xbox, tracking, comfort, sustained performance and final biome art remain open. The next useful observation is whether a player predicts exposure, recognizes the service connection and chooses a different approach on another seed; improve a weak module before multiplying it.
