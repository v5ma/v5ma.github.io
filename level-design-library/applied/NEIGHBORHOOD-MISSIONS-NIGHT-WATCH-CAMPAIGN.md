# Neighborhood Missions: Night Watch campaign application

This application extends the Living Portal and Signal Hijack foundation instead of replacing the courier/social game. It applies the shared place-mastery method to an action campaign: the player learns when rooftops, interior service functions, patrol timing, tools, stealth and direct combat are useful, then exploits those relationships in later cases.

Five sequential cases reuse the same authored ward. Rooftop Run turns familiar terraces into cape and grapple traversal. Quiet Circuit makes elevated observation, patrol timing, a service vent, smoke and behind/drop takedowns an alternative to a brawl. Rooms of the Ward connects the press, kitchen, storehouse, greenhouse, workshop and canal-service bed as one functional systems investigation. Courtyard Surge uses the receiving court and loading loft as distinct freeflow arenas linked by real vertical traversal. Last Light accepts either roof stealth or courtyard combat before returning through mastered connections.

The campaign does not enlarge the map merely to increase content count. It increases the number of useful relationships within existing geography and interiors. Campaign enemies are malfunctioning service sentries; residents are never targets. Sentry behavior is deliberately bounded and readable rather than an imitation of a licensed game's characters or AI.

XR embodiment stays native WebXR. Menus and long rays are hidden during ordinary play. Direct body zones select grapple, pulse and smoke; the cape uses a two-low-grip spread after unlock. Controller actions remain direct. First-person VR/AR and the centered full-depth third-person portal diorama share the same simulation, saves and collision.

Technical model acceptance is not human level-design approval. Browser acceptance must traverse the campaign through actual input, retain every earlier suite, and verify saves/rewards. Physical Quest/Xbox, comfort, tool-reach accessibility, frame timing, production art and unfamiliar-player understanding remain separate gates.
