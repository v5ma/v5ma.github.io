# Expanded opening adventures and original soundtrack

This revision implements Micah's request for a larger, more populated opening level and a complete music replacement. It retains a continuous lower route but adds enemies, bonus blocks, collectible parcels, shields, invincibility, nitro, raised platforms, open ramps, full optional loops and friendly postal characters. Safe ground does not require an empty level.

Sunrise Borough is 224 tiles wide, versus the earlier 76-tile route. Its six areas are Post Office Green, Market Lanes, Loopside Park, Copper Orchard, Canal Steps and Festival Finish. Five original postal neighbors provide brief proximity hints. The second and third chapters extend to 256 and 288 tiles and add further optional routes. Existing expert course IDs remain unchanged. The lower road remains continuous, with a gentle opening before the first enemy and a shield available in advance. Exact counts are defined and tested in ground-courses.js, not inferred from a screenshot.

The end column, including an airborne approach, is the finish. Introductory delivery quota is zero: paper deliveries and exploring raised routes earn score but cannot prevent completion. Levels one and two show a brief result and automatically start the next chapter with movement inputs cleared. Stay on results cancels that transition, as does a manual route/retry/editor choice. Custom editor levels never auto-load the campaign. The third chapter ends on results rather than forcing an expert sky course.

The old sixteen-step oscillator sequencer is replaced by three original 48-bar instrumental pieces: Postcards at Sunrise, Waterwheel Boulevard and Copperleaf Express. They use melodic phrases, chord voicings, bass, damped mallet/piano-like harmonics, plucked-string synthesis, pads and synthesized percussion. Sections include an introduction, theme, contrast, breakdown, reprise and turnaround. Stereo PCM is generated in a self-hosted Worker and played as a loop through Web Audio, independently of graphics-frame pacing. No external recording, Suno call or franchise music is included. These are synthesized original arrangements, not sampled acoustic instrument performances.

The music/effects buses have independently saved volume controls and shared master mute. The short effects use smoother envelopes and remove raw square/saw attacks. Opening Audio mix pauses gameplay. Music honors audio-context suspension, master mute, leaving the creator, page hiding and restoration. The waveform tests check finite samples, headroom, non-silence and stereo content, not whether a human listener prefers the music. Future licensed recorded tracks can replace the score source separately; no such service integration is made here.

Verification separates pure tests from actual-browser results. Native HTTP suites drive ordinary movement/jump buttons, traverse the first level on the road and through its optional full loop, finish with no paper deliveries, and observe the automatic second-level start. The audio suite exercises the actual Worker, AudioContext, mixer, mute, local preferences and stop-on-editor behavior. No rider position, velocity, score or completion is assigned by those browser tests. Software WebGL verification does not establish physical-device frame rates or subjective soundtrack quality.

The initial loop test read a successful upper-half condition, then took a slow screenshot, then incorrectly asserted that the rider was still at that height. The corrected test returns the observed position with the satisfied condition before requesting the screenshot. The game continues under its original physics. The first road replay reached the final district at x=6956 with six areas visited, five power-up pickups and four enemies defeated, but hit its wall-clock allowance while still moving at full speed. The allowance was increased without accelerating the game or injecting progress. Captured HUD review also removed a misleading 0/0 display now that mail is optional.

Runtime code and existing saved blueprints remain in the same application. Draft Route Workshop/Quarry/Vault PR19 is not merged by this release. Dino Atlas, Theology, the separate Studio and account settings are untouched. The temporary compressed patch transport was checksum-verified and deleted in the source preparation commit; the shipped runtime is readable JavaScript/CSS/HTML.

Technical reference: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Web_Audio_API/Advanced_techniques and https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/AudioContext/resume . Artwork and compositions are original project work; no new blanket license is selected on the owner's behalf.

Rollback is a scoped revert of the release commit without clearing player saves or resetting unrelated master history.
