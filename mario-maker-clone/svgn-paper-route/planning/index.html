THE SHARED KANBAN

What exists, what is next, what is still blocked

The acceptance rules

A path must carry its actual arrival speed into the next move. A metadata arrow or nearby endpoint is not evidence. Curves must retain clearance from other surfaces and from the trajectories they are meant to receive. Rewards should follow demonstrated routes rather than float in unreachable gaps.

The lower route remains playable and the finish still advances the chapter. The goal is a set of appealing choices, not a mandatory high-speed chain. Branches may ask for skill, but their risk and recovery should be understandable before the jump.

Longer-term work covers input-tolerance envelopes, alternative routes scored by unique rewards and travel time, peg-to-peg transfer witnesses, readable prefabs, human playtests, sound, accessibility and performance. Those are separate deliverables, not claims that this release has solved everything.
