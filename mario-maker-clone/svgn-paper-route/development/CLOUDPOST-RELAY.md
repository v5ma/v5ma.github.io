# Cloudpost: one optional upper-to-upper relay

This is an additive route above Sunrise Borough, not a replacement of its fifteen authored surfaces. The same two-sided grip, whip, nitro, road and finish rules remain. After High Garden, holding the normal whip key can reach a mint peg in clear air. A wound-up up-right release crosses to a broad receiving balcony and continues toward the existing Festival glide. Ignoring the peg retains the Bellflower hook and reversing cradle. The canal and later road entrances remain separate choices. The earlier dense layout, later chapters, editor documents, accounts and other applications are not migrated.

The declared model matrix varies seven cast positions and seven release phases, continuously carrying the same rider from a road jump through the first six surfaces, the cast, full wind-up, release, balcony and final glide. It uses the actual isolated two-sided runtime dynamics. A twenty-pixel swept rider envelope and a two-pixel rope segment are checked against thirty-four-pixel road bodies. All forty-nine selected cases pass; three entry speeds also pass Precision grip. These finite samples do not prove all release directions, boosted approaches, body impacts, hardware frame rates or human enjoyment. Badly timed releases can still miss. Rehearsals seed only the initial model jump and never reset intermediate contacts. Native acceptance remains a separate mandatory gate.

The 400-point relay bonus needs a real release from the matching peg followed by a receiving-track contact before other ground/rail contact, death, retry or expiry. It is awarded once in a run, retained as already earned across checkpoint retries and reset for a genuinely new run. Staying on the balcony or crossing it repeatedly cannot farm the award. Editor preview uses the existing campaign-save isolation. Imported or rearranged documents remain editable; their new geometry is not claimed to inherit the tested arrival window.

The first native attempt exposed a loader mismatch: the Workshop read the late-registered builder, while the campaign menu held the original frozen provider. The menu now resolves its provider on each access, including retries and next-route navigation. It does not rebuild a player's already-running level or silently migrate saved drafts. The shared build and publication manifest include this controller and the new route module. Acceptance workflows remain read-only and preserve failures.

The new sky postal building is background art adjacent to the physical balcony, not an extra collision platform. Its position follows that rail's current geometry. Removing the original peg also removes its special decorative marker, rather than leaving a fake target. Normal rail and tile rendering remain responsible for actual collision objects.

Release requires model/document checks, native full relay and unchanged high/road/canal/re-entry runs, editor/ride-lab/grip/whip regressions and post-merge public-file verification. Physical phones, controllers and native-GPU performance remain open checks. This addresses the high-to-high grapple link and reward-flow roadmap tasks; broader multi-peg backward exploration and later-chapter authored redesign remain unfinished. Rollback is a scoped revert without clearing player saves or resetting unrelated repository history.
