# Sky Cycle: the road and sky design method

Approved direction, September 15, 2026. This is the canonical chapter-design method, read together with the production roadmap, current release receipt and Waterwheel specification. It consolidates the two retained user-supplied essays in `sources/`; it does not replace the existing game with another platformer.

## Design promise and hierarchy

Ride a complete adventure on the ground, discover an interconnected adventure above it, and gradually learn to connect the two into your own route. The momentum-driven bicycle/unicycle, original controls, two-sided riding, optional whip, deliveries, editor and soundtrack remain the foundation.

Sonic and Motocross Maniacs inform movement. Mario informs how existing actions are introduced and recombined. Metroid informs changing understanding and useful returns, before ability locks. Castlevania informs visible terrain/enemy decisions, not compulsory stop-and-fight corridors. These are the roles assigned by the approved essays, not equal quotas for every room. Do not require maximum speed, unfamiliar movement, precise combat and searching at the same instant.

## The ground is a complete journey

Author its beginning, change of pace, delivery purpose, readable encounters and arrival. Hide aerial art/geometry in a separate declared design review and ask whether the remaining road is worth riding. A continuous strip beneath the real content is insufficient. Ordinary completion may use rolling or stepped approaches, modest obstacles, bridges and braking. It must not require an advanced loop transfer, high-to-high whip, optional seal, every mailbox or an expert route.

The sky is an extensive connected adventure, not scattered stunt props. Expand by meaningful connections among distinct places, not by raw curve count. A short teaching balcony must return clearly to the road before a larger network can capture the rider. Longer aerial journeys should repeatedly see the neighborhood below, offer deliberate exits and reconnect near known places. Lower routes may offer delivery, shelter, discovery or a better approach; they are not automatically punishment.

## Author the whole transfer

For each connection, record approach position and speed range, ordinary held actions, launch lip, intended receiver, useful post-contact state, likely early/late/short outcomes, clearances and onward options. A catch that immediately ejects the rider into an unseen obstacle is not success. Use actual carried position and velocity, not a reset at each rail. The Ride Lab and isolated physics give bounded recipes; native gameplay and human comprehension remain separate evidence.

Static geometry may be designed using simulation measurements. The shipped game must not secretly select geometry, change speed, steer or snap to guarantee the recipe. State the tested initial conditions. Boosted approaches, reverse traversal, moving encounters and edited documents are not automatically covered. Near-miss catchers and road returns belong in the geometry pass, not a late difficulty patch.

## Pace, delivery and discovery

Give a chapter one movement question. Introduce a safe complete example, change the situation, offer a choice, allow observation and relief, then recombine familiar ideas at the conclusion. The ground and sky can answer the same question at different depths. A faster route must not repeatedly stop for identical wall searches; a combat choice needs a visible slowing approach.

Delivery gives geography a purpose. Doorsteps, canal maintenance stops, quays and upper postal balconies should explain why a route exists. A demanding simultaneous grapple/jump/throw is optional mastery, not an opening requirement. Existing accepted-authored-finish award rules remain authoritative; entering a scene or testing an edited copy does not bank progress.

Prefer discovering what the existing actions permit to inventing an upgrade tree. Distinguish knowledge, skill and genuinely unavailable abilities. Environmental changes need visible consequences and an escape route. The illustrative moving-machinery grapple from the first essay is not an approved dependency. Swimming and moving grapple targets remain separate decisions. Continuous circulation within a chapter and separate portal journeys must not be conflated; current portal travel begins a new run.

## Camera, controls, art and sound

Frame the decision: approach, receiver and a meaningful recovery region where possible. Test along actual trajectories and at the speeds the level invites, not only in a static overview. Do not solve visibility by indefinitely shrinking the rider. Normal 3D, supported 2D and the seated stereo XR scene need their own readability reviews. Background architecture must not imply false collision surfaces or conceal receivers.

Jump, boost, paper and whip remain direct controls with familiar remaps. Test simultaneous demands on the real controller mapping. Hand UI availability does not prove that rapid sequences of small pinch targets are comfortable. The ordinary journey must not depend on that untested dexterity. Physical Quest 3 controller and hand use, comfort, lighting/tracking loss and actual Xbox ergonomics remain named qualification gates.

Animation reflects physics; pedal/grip contacts do not move the collision body. Sound reinforces an actual mechanism or arrival. Quiet Water's optional notices and bounded ambience support attention, not essential navigation. A muted-effects, optional-notices-off review must still explain the route, hazards, mechanisms and finish. Preserve the single soundtrack owner.

## Layout revision and authoring ownership

Preserve the game and player history, not every prototype arrangement. Retain stable route IDs/indices and earned earlier-layout records. Partition geometry-dependent times and new achievements by explicit layout revision; never silently compare unlike courses. Keep older medals visible and old Workshop copies unchanged. Provide an exportable classic layout where a campaign builder changes. An export is non-destructive and does not overwrite or load the current draft.

Audit persistent checkpoints before moving geometry. Current Waterwheel checkpoint positions are run-local, not a persistent campaign-position format; the redesign starts a new authored run. Do not generalize that fact to future save formats. Explicitly test old medals, career records, exploration, remaps, preferences, draft documents, reload, rejected travel and denied storage writes.

Preserve independent Bezier controls. Optional transfer annotations should associate launch, receiver, recovery and assumptions without restricting free editing. Edits invalidate the relevant rehearsal evidence. A copied authored recipe does not certify arbitrary edited geometry.

## Chapter quality gate and production order

Finish bounded release obligations already in flight, then prioritize a Waterwheel reference chapter over another cosmetic layer or unrelated destination. One slice can be released with explicit scope; the entire chapter-quality milestone stays open until all its own conditions pass.

The gate requires a complete ordinary road journey, a reversible introductory detour, a sustained express line, a meaningful lower/discovery alternative, useful recovery, visible landmarks, correct source/record ownership and ordinary-input acceptance. Review actual captures for contact readability, foreground clutter and camera surprises. Preserve failed attempts. Test beginners for hesitation, mistaken expectations and route recognition; death counts alone do not measure enjoyment.

Reference quality additionally requires unfamiliar-player playtests, measured device performance, physical Xbox/Quest tests and the chapter's remaining advanced sequence. Do not call code existence, a model matrix or emulated XR proof of those human/hardware qualities. Expand Copperleaf or the wider network only with explicit evidence from the reference chapter.
