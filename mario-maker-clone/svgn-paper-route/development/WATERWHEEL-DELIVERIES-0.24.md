# Waterwheel delivery quality, v0.24

This slice continues the Waterwheel Lab v0.23 preview. It does not promote the preview into the eight-route campaign or make mail mandatory. It gives the lower road a deliberate delivery rhythm and verifies all twelve authored targets with the original throw physics and familiar direct controls. Exact-source acceptance, merge and publication status are recorded separately in `verification/waterwheel-deliveries-0.24.json`.

## Design contract

The twelve road targets have explicit identities, regions, roles and intentions in `waterwheel-layout-core.mjs`. The sequence includes teaching, ordinary doorsteps, a proposed return reward, control, road choice, discoveries and a finale. Adjacent targets are at least 540 world pixels apart, more than twice the 250-pixel nearest-mailbox eligibility radius. Authored encounter centers remain more than 250 pixels from the mailboxes. That spacing is not a promise that a moving enemy can never enter a throw window. Ground-only and full-sky builds share the exact delivery journey.

`express-road-choice` gives road riders an objective beneath the aerial express line. `canal-observation-stop` offers a quieter canal-side target. `waterwheel-overlook` relates a delivery to the landmark. The final Wheelhouse target is after the last authored encounter and before the depot. These are design intentions; a road-only delivery run does not by itself qualify every combined porch/express/delivery route or prove that an unfamiliar player reads the intended rhythm.

The preview retains quota zero and the original Workshop isolation. Serving every target cannot create a `canal-choices-r2` career record, campaign medal, badge, stamp, ghost or permanent credit change. Prior `canal-choices` records and every encoded campaign builder stay intact. Delivery intent metadata survives the existing document codec.
