# Authored first-chapter routes, under the current rail engine

This is the scoped integration of PR28's design intent into v0.12's two-sided, momentum-preserving grip. It does not merge PR28's obsolete movement adapters, deflection code, alternative editor rehearsal or transport workflows. Ride Lab, Bezier authoring, the optional flight guide, default unicycle and bike choice remain unchanged.

The first chapter is authored around 15 actual surfaces instead of 46 mostly disconnected pieces. The high route carries a rider through the rising runway, crescent, open clocktower curl, receiving cradle, canal fork, high garden, left-launching Bellflower hook, reversing cradle, resting shelf and downhill finish approach. A brake-selected lower route takes the broad canal collector, curved descent and low swoop. Two later road entrances reconnect with those lines. The orchard entry uses the underside of the lower swoop before returning to the top of the final glide. No route choice changes controls, contact tolerance, gravity or velocity behind the player's back.

The hook's arrival tangent, return cradle and orchard shelf were retuned together using the actual carried landing state. Proving isolated edges was insufficient. Entry lips were lowered gently into their long runways without shifting the later launch curves; this removes the leading-edge clearance warning on the declared late/fast entry case. An underside parcel trail rewards the recovery route without a mandatory pickup. Existing lower terrain, encounters, postal characters, checkpoints, music and finish-only progression remain. The former elevated puzzle is replaced only in newly built chapter one; old saved drafts are not migrated or overwritten. Chapters two/three and expert course identities retain their previous builders.

The four declared model recipes cover every retained surface. The model matrix uses entry speeds 6, 7.5 and 9 and offsets 90, 120, 150 and 180, carrying real grip and flight states throughout all 48 combinations. A separate precise-grip pass and six braking positions are checked. A swept 20-pixel envelope contains the 26-by-30 rider and is checked against the 34-pixel deck bodies, including source and receiving surfaces. Distinct curve centerlines maintain at least 70 pixels of separation. These are bounded model claims, not proof of every control sequence or every missed jump. The model's first jump is an explicit seed; native tests start on the actual street and drive keys.

The gameplay camera frames the current curl and its lower catcher. Mint edging distinguishes the lower branch from the gold high line. Decorative diagonal supports that visually cut across this layout are omitted; actual road geometry still drives rendering. Passive hints are limited to the untouched featured first chapter and are not applied to arbitrary rearranged playtests. The old dense layout remains an explicitly labeled editable copy inside Worlds and drafts.

Acceptance must test the complete upper, brake-selected lower, underside recovery and ground-only routes, then verify editor export/undo and preservation of the old copy. Existing Bezier, Rail Grip Yard, First Flight, Hookline and Ride Lab/guide regressions remain. All tests run directly against committed source with read-only workflow permissions. Actual frames and source hashes, not route-count claims, determine release readiness. The public manifest now includes the three new or changed route modules.

The optional peg remains available, but a comfortable high-to-high whip route and comprehensive reverse-route coverage still need tuning. The later chapters have not received this authored pass. This is a coherent foundation, not a claim that fifteen shapes complete Micah's much denser long-term world. Native GPU/phone/controller performance and human enjoyment remain separate checks. No Supabase, payments, coupons, entitlement, multiplayer or unrelated project settings are changed. Rollback is a scoped revert without clearing player drafts or resetting master history.
