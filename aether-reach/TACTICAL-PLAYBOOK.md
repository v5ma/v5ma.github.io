# Field Engineering / Tactical Relay

An original optional combat layer on the existing Aether Reach map. This release does not include the unfinished living-city PR62. It uses no private story, external security system or network service.

## First experiment

Walk to the Field Engineering bench just east of Quay Outfitters and use E. Borrow the field rig, then equip Current. The range target stands in water: survey the live target with J, select a passive in N / Field rig, then use Q and the gun together. T cycles learned powers. Power energy/cooldown is separate from firearm ammunition/reloading; the weapon stays equipped.

Current costs 28 base energy. A wet target takes a stronger immediate discharge, the water conducts to visible nearby machines, and the charge expires. Cinder costs 32 and leaves a timed burn; shooting oil creates a seven-second burning area. Neither effect goes through a solid building. Charged water and fire can also hurt you. This is authored conductive/burnable surface interaction, not fluid simulation.

## Prepare the Atrium recovery

Walk north from Arrival Quay to Bellweather Atrium. The security junction sits to the right of the fountain. Rotate the nine visible conductors so the west source connects to the east turret through matching ports, then energize the circuit. It is a paused planning puzzle, not a timed network intrusion.

The friendly turret has fourteen capacitor shots per attempt. It assists but cannot win an unattended run, even with the Engineer passive. The nearby collector offers a separate Start action: defend its 180 hull through three two-machine waves and clear all enemies after the 48-second recovery. Use gunfire, status effects, surface traps and the turret together. The first success grants 180 credits. Further successful attempts are practice, not repeat payouts. Retreat, rescue or destruction ends the attempt; reloading resets an unfinished defense and never invents victory.

## One passive, different approaches

- Insulator: 65% less self-damage from charged water and burning oil.
- Capacitor: survey one class, then spend 25% less energy on Current/Cinder.
- Catalyst: survey two classes, then deal 20% more gun damage to shocked/burning enemies.
- Engineer: reroute security, then improve each friendly turret shot by 40%.

A live, visible survey records each of the target/scout/heavy/sentry classes once. It gives +10% gun damage against that class. There is no repeat-scan currency farming or image/photo storage. Passive changes are permitted on foot outside an active recovery, not as free mid-fight swaps.

## Inputs and limits

Keyboard: N field kit, T cycle, J survey, Q selected power; existing firing, scope, rail and Foldwing controls remain. Touch uses the Field/Power/Survey HUD buttons and existing PULSE action. Xbox-standard D-pad left opens the kit; LB casts. The experimental XR adapter uses the left controller ray for Current/Cinder, right for the gun, and provides the conductor diagram on its spatial menu. Physical Xbox/Quest testing and comfort remain unverified.

Gun, movement, status and UI remain separate modules. Effects use fixed scene pools. Existing version-1 equipment saves remain compatible; learned powers, module, survey classes, friendly security and first victory persist. Projectiles, hazard timers and unfinished waves do not.

## Follow-on work, not delivered here

T05: throwable debris and projectile redirection with two-hand/controller collision tests. T06: player-placed traps with constrained placement and visible ownership. T07: a heavier boss-style encounter that requires learning a weakness, rather than simply adding health. T08: deeper research objectives and multiple meaningful build slots. Keep every addition testable through a complete encounter and preserve the original expedition.

References informing the mechanical direction: the developer BioShock Remastered manual (https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manuals/bioshockremastered/latest/steam/) and 2K's BioShock 2 product description (https://store.2k.com/pt-BR/game/buy-bioshock-2). No franchise assets, dialogue or characters are imported. The current implementation is not a complete remake of either game.
