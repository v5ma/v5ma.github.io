# Aether Reach 0.7.0 - Crosswind Arsenal

Crosswind Arsenal turns the floating city into a faster first-person combat sandbox while retaining the existing expedition, save key, rooftops, Quay art and WebXR adapter. Rails now cruise at 48 m/s and can boost to 96 m/s, with strong braking and automatic station approach control.

Three enlarged combat annexes add low freight cover, taller walls and objective consoles. Combat keeps the player mobile: B crouches, R3 performs the wrist-tool strike, A jumps/attaches/dismounts, Y swaps the two carried firearms, X interacts/reloads/picks up dropped guns, LT aims, RT fires, and L3 changes the Longglass between 4x and 8x while scoped. LB supports tap casting or a held trap gesture; RB swaps recent powers or opens the power wheel.

The original companion is Tavi, an invulnerable field intelligence that follows the player, can offer scarce health/ammunition/energy aid, and manifests temporary cover, medical or turret rifts when conditions allow. New arena enemies drop weapons so the two-gun limit creates meaningful scavenging decisions without deleting weapons already purchased and stored at outfitters.

The soundscape is generated locally with Web Audio: distinct weapon transients, reload mechanisms, impacts, footsteps, rail machinery, wind, supernatural effects, shield break, melee, UI cues and an original adaptive procedural score. Master, effects, music and ambience levels are separate, with an optional night dynamic-range setting.

This release does not claim physical Xbox or Quest hardware certification, competitive multiplayer, networked matchmaking, or Counter-Strike parity. It is an original single-player browser FPS expansion inspired by fast tactical shooters and vertical immersive-sim traversal.
