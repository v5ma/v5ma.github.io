# Bellwether Rewired: first authored replacement slice

The user's September 15, 2026 attached Aether Reach level-design brief is the basis. Its priority is an integrated district experience, not more islands, independent features or decorative buildings. It proposes a recognizable blackout, safe electrical experimentation, different street approaches, readable circuit machinery, an ascent that looks back at earlier spaces, a rooftop decision encounter and a changed return journey. This file distinguishes the first implementation slice from the rest of that design ambition.

## What this slice changes

The market gets physical screens and low cover separating a direct lane from a protected service approach. An induction bench near the dispatch is repeatable without buying equipment. Its crank lights a lamp for a short demonstration; the marked water also uses the existing Current conductivity rule. A second supported conductive patch occupies the street encounter. The crank grants neither mission advancement nor money.

The Arcade retains its three dials, maintenance record and accessible solution. Three visible gauges move with the real dial values. Testing reports the first interrupted supply, return or balance stage. Stable power opens a west service shutter in both rendering and collision. The state is derived from the existing mission stage, so old completed-circuit saves reopen the shortcut without a new save schema.

The Arcade roof and rear gallery lead into a continuous multi-level maintenance route around the theatre. Four stair flights and five deck surfaces share their authored data with simulation and presentation. The gallery looks back into the market and reconnects to the receiver terrace through a real railing opening. The old south ladder remains a valid alternative and return path. No purchased power, mandatory rail trick or forced camera sequence is required. Both ascent and descent are tested with ordinary movement integration, not waypoint teleportation.

Receiver synchronization still lasts the existing six seconds. Its network feedback culminates in the market lamps and a public warning wheel running. There is no new hold timer added merely to stretch the chapter. Existing street and rooftop enemies, mission identifiers, checkpoint, record and one-time 300-credit reward remain. New connections may be explored before their objective is active, but cannot skip the circuit prerequisites or invent completion.

## Authoring and acceptance contract

bellwether-layout.mjs is the readable physical route contract. Each route has a purpose rather than being an interchangeable corridor: direct cover and supplies, protected service access with a restored shortcut, or upper reconnaissance and a continuous ascent. Gameplay floors/solids are shared with visible geometry. Use model tests to check reachability, collision, stage gates and save invariants. Use the real controller mission to check integration. Neither establishes that a reveal is memorable or combat is fair to an unfamiliar player.

Review screenshots and play the whole arrival-to-return arc before decorative expansion. Observe route recognition, wrong turns, repeated deaths, ammunition shortages, overlooked circuit feedback, clue use and whether the return looks meaningfully changed. Preserve a basic-equipment path. Do not classify a human-quality or hardware gate as complete from automated test counts.

## Work still open

This is the first replacement slice, not a claim that every old level or the whole Bellwether experience is finished. Stronger arrival composition, protected first-contact teaching, rail approach sightlines, encounter repositioning, environmental narrative rooms and observation-led tuning remain. The new route should be revised or replaced where playtesting exposes weak space; adding more stairs is not itself a quality measure. Campaign-scale continuity and the other districts remain later work.

The source brief treats the city as a connected, repairable place. New authored story cues here are public design proposals, not private established narrative. Do not import the unmerged living-city experiment or private manuscript.
