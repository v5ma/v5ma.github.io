# Living-city expansion acceptance

This expansion grows the existing Aether Reach map. This checklist is a release gate, not a declaration that a native or physical-device test has passed. Check the current PR and Actions receipts for actual status.

## Same-world continuity

Retain the original five districts, seven rail routes, relay expedition, weapons, purchases, loot, Foldwing and existing saved equipment. The southern additions must be reachable through physical connections, not an unrelated replacement map. Shared homepage and sibling applications must remain unchanged by this project except separately reviewed routing compatibility.

## New connected content to verify

- Walk into the cafe and other added interiors using their visible doors. Reach the below-deck pump vault by its actual stairs, interact at the correct floor, and return to the street without falling through ceilings or being rescued.
- Complete the missing-cat, fuse/pump and cargo-evidence sequence through proximity and dialogue. Verify optional restitution and the mayor's permit. Closed mission gates must actually block movement; earning the relevant permit must open them and persist after reload.
- Complete the ordered glyph lesson, learn Mend, spend its real energy and respect cooldown. Spend earned attribute points once; health and other values must not increase twice after a reload.
- Board the Kestrel only after the relevant unlock. Test continuously controlled ascent, movement, descent, collision and parking. Complete the airmail journey by reaching the actual Garden delivery location; a save/reload must neither invent a delivery nor strand the aircraft permanently.
- Check named conversational residents, walking citizens/patrols and cats. Optional companionship dialogue is adult and non-explicit; it is not a full relationship simulation. The Watch and criminal investigation are authored roles, not a completed systemic police/crime simulation.
- Verify ordinary keyboard/mouse, touch and standard-controller access to new actions and menus. Keep experimental XR orientation independent of aircraft and rail steering. Physical Xbox and Quest 3 checks remain separate from device emulation.

## Visual review

Inspect actual native first-person captures of a street, cafe interior, basement, conservatory/glyph room and skiff in flight. Check corrected sky output, stable crystal scale, model proportions, labels, door openings, interior lighting and mobile readability. Record rendered triangle/draw-call counts under comparable viewpoints; do not substitute a polygon increase for measured improvement. Keep the lower-detail mobile/headset profile.

## Test and publication evidence

Run the complete existing Node suite and restore-safety checks, both new civic/magic browser scenarios, and the original client, arsenal, rail, foldwing, controller and XR regressions. Browser tests must use real input and may observe read-only snapshots, not assign actor position, mission flags, money or time. Fix actual defects; do not widen interaction thresholds just to satisfy a fixture.

Remove one-time integration scripts/write workflows from the final tree. Review the latest master diff before merging to preserve concurrent projects. After successful acceptance, verify the actual hosted-file hashes and the versioned GitHub Release source-restore and download receipts. Do not describe this expansion as publicly deployed until those receipts exist.

Unpublished narrative and private repositories are outside this build, source archive, test logs and release assets.
