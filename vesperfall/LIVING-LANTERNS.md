Living Lanterns / Vesperfall / 2026-09-22.

This pass integrates the owner's Currentworks Water, Fire and Trees library into the existing Pilgrimage game. It uses the existing A-Frame/Three r184 renderer, not another engine or a separate graphics demo. Exact original module blobs are pinned under vendor/currentworks; PROVENANCE.json records the originating repository revision and both Git and SHA-256 hashes. Prism Current itself is unchanged.

The Lantern Causeway and Ashen Archive each receive two shallow animated court surfaces. Ordinary grounded movement and real low-height impact, Blink, shard and flask events can disturb the water. Upper-gallery movement does not create splashes on the floor below. Water stays above existing supported court floors; it does not create a hazard, a swimming system, buoyancy, new collision or a teleport destination. Sky reflection and authored-bed shading are analytic effects, not reflected-scene rendering or sampled camera refraction.

The Causeway receives four original seeded alder/willow trees rooted on its existing enclosing wall crowns. Their complete geometry, including a conservative wind envelope, stays clear of player bodies on the galleries. The Archive does not gain indoor trees. Both chapters gain a small refuge brazier/lens on each existing refuge marker. It is dark until that module's actual relay is restored, then displays a gold lens and bounded volumetric flame. A real release/signal shot or normal gallery interaction causes this change. Returning or reloading preserves it through the existing saved target set; no extra reward, inventory, objective or save namespace is created. Real cinder explosion events may produce cosmetic fire bursts, never extra damage.

This is the first visible refuge consequence, not the complete courier-lantern campaign. A carryable reusable lantern, NPC/service changes, new recovery mechanisms, expanded versioned chapters, melee and four difficulty tiers remain next work. Do not claim them as part of this graphical pass or recreate this integration instead of advancing them.

The existing Water/Tidelight settings retain authority. Off hides the new water. Ripples and caustic-detail controls remain available; reduced motion fixes wind/waves and suppresses transient effects while retaining static readability. Desktop Cinematic is capped to Currentworks Balanced, and immersive VR uses Light. There are at most two water meshes, four trees, two sustained braziers and no added dynamic lights. Preparation is asynchronous on the existing renderer while the new group stays hidden. Resources are separately owned, released on loss/removal, and rebuilt only after context restoration. A preparation failure leaves the old presentation and the working game intact. The previous Tidelight root is suppressed only while this integration owns a supported chapter, and restored for older worlds.
