Vesperfall 0.8.0: Resonant Hunt

This release extends the existing Hollow Dominions game. Its 25 districts, 15 enemy archetypes, elevated routes, procedural seeds, prior equipment, progression and AR Sanctuary remain available.

The score is composed locally in resonance-score.js. Five distinct eight-bar arrangements use original modal melodies, three-part synthetic choir chords, plucked strings, bells and a threat-dependent percussion layer. District changes request the next arrangement at a phrase boundary. The AR Sanctuary has its own calmer arrangement. These are procedural instruments, not recordings of a human choir and not third-party game music.

The sound graph uses a tracked listener, HRTF point sources, distance attenuation, filtered obstruction by the actual level walls, a deterministic reverberation impulse and a compressor. Bow nock and tension, release, crossbow shot, winding and latch, impacts, precision hits, magical explosions, frost, shield blocks and breaks, enemy warnings, footsteps, near-miss projectiles, pickups, shard steps and teleports have separate recipes. Attack warnings lower the music. Sound captions remain available when muted. Maximum polyphony is 34 voices on desktop and 28 in XR. Inactive and distant sounds are culled. Pausing clears active voices; returning from hidden tabs does not replay a backlog.

Master, music, effects and ambience levels are independent. The mix offers headphones, quiet and wide settings. The Sound check button plays the original instruments and left/right positional effects. The same settings and sound check are reachable through Xbox focus navigation and Quest spatial menus. Browser autoplay policy is respected: a user interaction may be required to start audio. Existing explicit mute preferences are preserved.

The default XR display is a magical free-hand familiar. Turn the free palm up while holding it comfortably in front of you to reveal numeric vitality, score, renown, guard, shard charges and ammunition. Lowering it hides the display. There is no passive vitality regeneration. An always-visible wrist display and the previous compact HUD remain available as accessibility options. Wrist canvas updates are throttled independently of tracked poses.

For Quest bow play, the weapon hand holds the bow and the other trigger grabs the string near the nock. Physical draw length and aim still determine the actual projectile. For the crossbow, the weapon-hand trigger fires one loaded bolt. Grab the rear winding handle with the free-hand trigger, pull it backward about 22 cm, then release. The Short physical-draw setting reduces that travel to 13 cm. A completed physical gesture takes 0.48 seconds to latch, or 0.35 seconds after Quickwind. The free-stick click remains the accessible timed-reload alternative. Invalid tracking, overextension, pausing and guard cancel a partial gesture without firing.

Quest grip roles have changed deliberately: the free-hand grip raises the physical directional shield; the weapon-hand grip performs a collision-checked short shard step. Guard prevents string draw and crossbow firing. Weapon-stick click switches bow/crossbow. The weapon-hand lower face button interacts, and its upper face button opens the menu. The free-hand upper face button toggles Blink. Weapon stick provides optional smooth movement and free stick snap-turns. Handedness reverses every physical role. The system-owned headset menu and browser permission dialogs remain outside game control.

Point the free hand at a visible crystal or reliquary within reach and hold its trigger to pull it. The model checks head-to-hand distance, hand-to-pickup distance and line of sight; the visual pull cannot collect through cover. Releasing early or losing tracking cancels it. A collected item grants its actual existing resource once. Practice includes a nearby frost crystal for learning this gesture without permanent rewards. Proximity collection remains an accessibility option and the browser/Xbox default.

Hold the free-hand lower face button to open the six-arrow magical quiver, use the free stick to select and release to equip. On Xbox, hold Y, select using either stick or the D-pad, and release Y; tap Y cycles to the next arrow. A confirms and B cancels. On keyboard, hold Tab, use arrow keys and release Tab; Escape cancels. Touch has a quiver button and selectable choices. Empty or locked selections leave the previous arrow equipped.

Tactical focus has a three-second reserve. It slows only enemies, projectiles and other simulation updates to 20 percent while the quiver is open. Headset and hand tracking continue at their normal update rate. The reserve drains using the unscaled active-frame clock and replenishes while the quiver is closed; after depletion selection continues in real time. A real-time-only preference is available. Pausing and lost-session transitions close the quiver and do not generate a stray shot.

Ricochet arrows reflect from collision-tested stone walls at most twice, losing speed and damage at each bounce. They consume finite ammunition. Mirror Thread unlocks them after banking 20 real arrow hits; future scored runs receive four and add them to supply drops. Practice offers six without granting permanent achievements. The previous profile key and all prior unlocks are retained, and old profiles simply begin their newly tracked hit count at zero.

Xbox retains RT draw/release or crossbow fire, LT guard, A interact, B Blink, X reload, RB shard step, LB cancel, left stick movement/click sprint, right stick aim/click weapon, View atlas, Menu pause and D-pad utility navigation. Menus, error dialogs, the journal, settings and seed keyboard remain controller-operable.

VR expedition and AR Sanctuary are distinct modes. AR uses an immersive-ar session with transparent background. It remains stationary and unscored; Blink, artificial movement and shard travel are disabled. It does not scan furniture, provide real-world occlusion or replace the headset boundary. Leaving AR restores the suspended expedition and its saved progress. Physical Quest comfort, real-controller ergonomics, passthrough quality, audio localization and hardware frame rate require hardware testing; software input emulation is not that certification.

Testing lives in tests/resonant.test.cjs, tests/resonant-browser.py and the existing regression suites. The additional browser test renders actual stereo Web Audio PCM, exercises physical input poses and reads live game results rather than assigning player health, score or enemy state. Publication compares served runtime files and assets byte-for-byte with the merged commit and exercises the public homepage card.
