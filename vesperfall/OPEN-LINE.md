Vesperfall 0.15.0 / Open Line.

This is a bounded refinement of The Returning Bell, not a second chapter or another map expansion. The player should learn that opening the cathedral's processional line creates both an opportunity and a threat. A fast approach, a useful firing angle and an enemy sightline share one physical opening. The gallery remains the place to observe and reverse that decision. The ordinary ambulatory remains a dependable alternative. Knowledge can later replace a tower visit with a legitimate signal shot.

The existing screen blocked a court firing line, but the tower's solid front facade also obstructed that direction and the ordinary routes primarily went around it. The improvement gives that screen a usable opening to govern. This is a source-derived design diagnosis, not a statement from an unfamiliar-player study. The library's Dark Souls, Dishonored, Castlevania and Half-Life references are translated into useful reconnection, consequential approaches, enemy attack geometry and visible cause/effect. No commercial maps or assets are copied.

Hypothesis and primary observation.

Opening a shorter shared movement/firing lane should make the screen a consequential speed-versus-exposure decision rather than an ornamental switch. The primary automated observation is actual controller-driven distance to the same tower destination, with simulation time, health, damage, shots and living threats recorded alongside it. The short approach must be substantially shorter than either flank, remain traversable and save-safe, and expose the existing watcher through the same collision aperture. Relative damage is reported, not predetermined as a universal balance score. Human comprehension and whether the choice feels worthwhile remain separate open observations.

Physical and conditional descriptions.

The existing footprint now contains a 2.8-metre-wide processional stair joining court and tower. Its landing and open lintel share the real collision/support system, projectile sweeps and Blink checks. Lowered screens close this direct connection. Raising them opens the connection and the firing lane; the graph reflects the same conditional edge. The existing gallery and east stairs remain available in both screen states. No room, pickup or enemy count is increased.

A visible brass release beside the screens can be hit by an ordinary damage arrow. Stone still occludes the projectile; Blink arrows do not activate the release. A strike raises the same screen state controlled by the gallery winch. Repeated strikes while it is already raised do not grant points, completion or duplicate events. The gallery can lower and raise the screens again. An occupied closing/lifting volume is refused rather than embedding an actor. The mechanism adds no new controller mapping or menu.

Behavioral and information descriptions.

The existing rear archer is placed behind the newly opened tower line. It uses its existing range, windup, shot and recovery rules; it does not gain invented stair navigation or new statistics. The player can expose that watcher by changing the screen state. A signal shot from the court may encounter the watcher first: actors, targets and walls remain genuine swept-projectile obstacles. After dealing with the obstruction, the signal can be rung without entering the tower or defeating every defender. Returning to the refuge is still required for the ordinary chapter outcome.

The brass catch, connecting cable, exposed-stair cue and retained landmarks communicate what changes. The upper route supplies observation and reversible control; the ambulatory bypasses dependence on the release; the short route trades that detour for an exposed crossing and an archery action. The learned long shot is an optional knowledge/skill approach, not a mandatory tutorial sequence or a new ability lock. Quiet refuge/service circulation and the previously authored return revelation are retained.

Embodiment and recovery.

Use the actual bow, arrow arc, player body and camera. Down D-pad still selects Blink; left D-pad still cycles usable damage arrows; menu/quiver navigation takes priority. The existing interact action operates the winch and gate. A missed release arrow leaves the screens unchanged and can be corrected with another Standard arrow; finite special ammunition is not required. A player who walks into the lowered screen can physically retreat and take the gallery or ambulatory. Model tests exercise blocked movement and that recovery, not just graph reachability. Existing valid gallery Blink landings still use the real predictor and swept arrival.

First-person VR shares the chapter and controller roles. The AR Architect's Table reconstructs the suspended layout version and shows discovered places only. It remains paused, unscored inspection with controller and hand-pinch UI, not a third-person campaign, room scan, physical anchor or hand-only combat mode. Switching modes must not move the saved player, change resources or rewrite mechanism state. Real Quest/Xbox behavior, seated/standing reach, tracking accuracy, comfort and sustained performance are not certified by these fixtures.

Save and rollback contract.

Keep hollow-dominions-1 and returning-bell-1 immutable. Open Line uses returning-bell-2 for new expeditions, not a silent rewrite under an old identifier. The original Surestep fixture and a scored Returning Bell v1 checkpoint generated from the actual 0.14.0 model remain regression inputs. New captures retain their actual generator. Restore recreates that geometry, applies screens and gate state, then validates the saved supported position. The crossing test saves a player inside the raised-screen opening, reloads through the real UI and checks position, health and mechanism state. A fabricated closed-screen checkpoint in the same position must be rejected, not relocated.

Profile keys, save envelope, reward receipts, existing modes and the one-time outcome remain. Completing this chapter still offers a blessing that explicitly continues into retained Endless content. No second authored chapter is included. A rollback must be a new scoped commit that preserves sibling work and all three saved layout identities. Reverting artwork is not permission to remove v2 restore support or clear storage.
