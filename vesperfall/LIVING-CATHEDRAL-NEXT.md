# Next art and traversal gates

## Current implementation
Six room families now have actual different dimensions or outlines, two optional height-gated reliquaries, a reversible skywalk between rooms and a two-stage belfry climb. A material-and-sculpture intake replaces the uniform surfaces with licensed local assets. The committed implementation is distinct from the final native and publication receipts.

## Next priorities

1. **Art review in matching playable views:** refine masonry scale, contrast and openings without altering proven collision. Keep imported assets cohesive. The large stylized bow/hand and limited character animation remain visible gaps, not completed art.
2. **More level families:** add authored courtyard/crypt/roof transitions and a larger graph vocabulary, not simply more square nodes or unconnected towers. The current topology is still nine logical slots with nonuniform physical extents.
3. **More meaningful upper encounters:** ranged crossfire, readable enemy entrances and counterplay from galleries. Current patrols still use the ground graph; multistorey pursuit is not implemented.
4. **Profile real Quest 3:** verify native controllers, floor height, blink comfort, stereo GPU/CPU time, memory and long-session rebuilding. Shared 1K materials, spatial batches and cached desktop-only shadows are implementation choices, not measured hardware guarantees.
5. **Human route review:** watch whether players notice the gold route/relic cues, can recover from taking a wrong turn and consider the climb worth its score/heal reward. Adjust reward and route pacing based on play, not solver counts.

No private story or service credential belongs in the public asset pipeline. Keep author/source/license/modification/checksum entries for every imported asset, including permission to redistribute raw project files.
