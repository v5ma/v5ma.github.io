Vesperfall 0.17.0 / Pilgrimage and Fieldwork.

The player feedback on September 18 established three failures: grip did not take a supply, teleport/contact collection was difficult to predict, and the new procedural chapters and old missions were not readily available in immersive play. Goldwind's A-button golden arrow was explicitly approved and is not reassigned.

This iteration recovers the unmerged Pilgrimage implementation rather than generating another replacement prototype. The default campaign is The Lantern Causeway followed by The Ashen Archive, using the existing deterministic authored-module pipeline. Read PILGRIMAGE.md for independent random streams, signatures, supported walking/Blink/shot links, fixed tier, variant catalogue and objective/reward contracts. Modules connect different observation, direct and sheltered approaches, paired with consequential shutters and persistent service returns. Both chapters end in the existing blessing flow, then retained Endless. This is an initial two-chapter catalogue, not a finished AAA campaign or an assertion about its human play length.

Grip takes priority when either tracked hand is within 0.5 metres of a visible supply. The draw hand can also aim at a supply within 7 metres and hold grip to pull it. A fresh press is required. Close reach does not demand pointing alignment. A pull remains subject to line of sight, tracked-hand reach and one-time simulation collection. Releasing grip, tracking loss, hand takeover or a pause cancels it without moving, deleting or awarding the item. The consumed grip cannot become a disk throw when released. Empty-space grip retains Goldwind's physically thrown disk. An active bow draw and the physical quiver retain their own control priority. Ordinary movement and bow-hand guard remain available during a pull.

The default is Grip pickup plus contact collection. A visible item within the player's body-height band and 0.85-metre ground radius can be collected after ordinary movement or a resolved teleport, with the same collision rule in either case. There is no collection along an arrow's route or across another floor. A saved Manual preference still disables contact collection; grip acquisition is available there too, alongside its existing trigger pull. Existing settings are not erased. Full-vitality supplies remain in place, rather than disappearing without benefit. A world-space label identifies the item and interaction, then confirms actual ammo/vitality gains. One bounded tether follows an active pull. This does not certify final interaction ergonomics on a physical headset.

VR and AR expedition are explicit first-screen entries. The headset's Missions browser directly offers Pilgrimage, Archive direct entry, The Returning Bell, original Hollow Dominions/Endless, Bellkeeper Oath and First Bell lessons/practice. Starting another scored mission asks before replacing an active expedition. Cancelling retains it. Normal saved-expedition protection also applies when starting from the title. Permission prompts are still browser-owned.

AR expedition is distinct from the old Sanctuary and Architect's Table. It retains the actual selected/current mission, collision, enemies, controls, exploration and normal save progress. Virtual movement uses the existing golden arrow or optional smooth input. Entry pauses and explains the real-play-space boundary. The background is transparent; artificial sky, flooded-floor presentation and environmental overlays are disabled where inappropriate. The player must not treat a virtual floor, stair or platform as a physical support or walk into furniture. There is no room scan, physical anchoring, furniture occlusion or promise of safe real-room reconstruction. Exiting keeps progress and pauses normally. Sanctuary remains stationary and unscored; the table remains discovery-limited paused inspection with its existing restoration contract. Bare hands operate menus, not archery.

The five supported layout identities remain hollow-dominions-1, returning-bell-1, returning-bell-2, lantern-causeway-1 and ashen-archive-1. Old saves are reconstructed under their recorded identity. The pickup change never changes item IDs, counts or layout signatures. Checkpoint and reward namespaces stay unchanged. First-person headset poses are not scripted for cinematic transitions.

The recovered pipeline browser run was not a complete pass. It reached both new chapters but reloaded a stale checkpoint near the Archive exit. The strengthened test compares the current complete captured state with the saved state before reloading, and records any refusal reason. It does not credit simply reopening an older valid save as successful preservation. The strict native diagnostic identified an audio-only soundPassed flag on hostile bolts as the rejection. Capture now strips exactly that transient cue while preserving projectile physics and strict unknown-field validation. A focused regression reproduces the condition.
