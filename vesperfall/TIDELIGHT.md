# Tidelight / flooded cloisters

Tidelight adds bounded, presentation-only water to selected existing Rain Court, Sunken Archive and Choir spaces. The pools reuse existing collision floors, so they do not change navigation, teleport validity, saves, combat or procedural generation. Water is translucent so the photographed stone remains visible beneath the surface.

Balanced mode is the default and is designed for WebXR. Cinematic mode adds finer vertex motion on desktop. Quest limits visible pools and ripple count and does not use a reflection camera or screen-space pass. AR disables the flooded-world layer entirely to preserve passthrough. Reduced-effects policy freezes ambient water motion. Arrow impacts, Blink/Shard arrivals and player movement can create pooled ripple rings.
