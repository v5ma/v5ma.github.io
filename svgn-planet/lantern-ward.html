Lantern Ward

Spatial XR

The same 3D game follows your character inside a room-fixed perspective portal. Head movement looks into it; it does not steer the courier. Foreground shell faces are transparent automatically. Both AR views request passthrough explicitly. First-person AR has no room scanning or physical obstacle detection: stay in a clear stationary play area and use the controller to travel. XR menus support tracked rays and hand pinches. Action controls: left stick moves and its click holds sprint; release brakes. Main grip interacts; main trigger strikes, or fires the selected field tool while the other trigger aims. Other grip guards/brakes. A hops, B mounts, X scans, Y opens Menu, and the turn-stick click cycles tools. Menus and long pointer rays stay hidden during normal gameplay. Raise a pinched hand near your head for Menu; low pinch holds movement. Controls, dominant hand, stick swap, snap angle and seated trigger alternatives are available inside XR. The courier preset remains available.
