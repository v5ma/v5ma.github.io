PLAY WITHOUT REACHING FOR A MOUSE

Your controller, your whole city.

Ride and explore

The left stick moves, with gentle movement for fine control. The right stick looks around and tilts the camera. Click the right stick to recenter. RT boosts; LT or B brakes. Click the left stick for an alternative boost control.

A hops. X interacts with a nearby mailbox or throws a paper when no interaction is nearby. LB throws toward the nearest undelivered mailbox within range. Y switches between riding and walking.

Find your next mission

RB cycles street, adventure and globe cameras. View or D-pad up opens the map. D-pad left/right changes the compass destination. D-pad down opens these controls. Menu pauses or resumes.

Every menu

D-pad or the left stick moves focus. Up/down selects a control; left/right changes a selected choice. A activates buttons, changes choices or toggles checkboxes. B returns or cancels a reset. The right stick scrolls long panels. The graphics recovery screen also accepts controller input. Disconnecting an active controller pauses play.

Keyboard and touch

WASD or arrows move. Shift boosts. Space hops. E interacts, Q throws, F rides or walks, V changes camera, C recenters, M opens the map, H opens controls, and P or Escape pauses. Drag the scene to turn the camera. On touch screens, use the joystick, action buttons and header controls.

There is no deadline. Complete the original eight deliveries and return to the depot, then keep exploring. Your city deliveries, postmarks, sprint gates, selected vehicle and location save on this device.
