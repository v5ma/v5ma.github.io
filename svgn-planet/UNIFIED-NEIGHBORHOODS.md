# Neighborhood Missions v0.16.0 / Unified Neighborhoods

Open index.html for the original city. The welcome screen, city pause menu and city map provide Lantern Ward / resident missions and Night Watch destinations. Travel keeps the document, renderer and active XR session, and returns to the original stopped city position. The districts retain distinct coordinate systems and save ledgers; this is not a claim of a physically seamless single terrain.

The main AR / VR control exposes First-person VR, Third-person VR, First-person AR, Third-person AR, Diorama First-person VR, Diorama Third-person VR, Diorama First-person AR and Diorama Third-person AR. The same views apply in both districts. Diorama Third-person keeps the miniature player centered in a room-fixed perspective aperture while the full world moves around the player. Diorama First-person is a character-eye-scale perspective through that aperture, not a flat image on a screen. Open front, open top or both; never both closed. Placement is manual, not room-scanned anchoring or physical-world occlusion.

Routine gameplay hides menu boards and long selection rays. Native maps, missions, settings and save recovery are accessed on demand. The original water map and wallet overlays are not composited over the unified XR view. Important puzzle text and backup feedback are copied into native menu text, not left exclusively on the desktop page.

Xbox retains left-stick movement, right-stick camera, A hop, X interact, Y mount/dock, RT speed, LT/B brake, Menu pause and D-pad down missions. In the optional on-foot encounter, RT strikes, LT guards or aims, LT+RT uses the tool, and the movement-stick click sprints. The earned cape uses both bumpers without also firing a tool or switching the camera. Release speed to stop; hitting a building or dismounting is not required.

The default right-handed XR Action profile uses left-stick movement and held stick click for speed, right grip for interaction, right trigger for strike/use, left trigger to aim and left grip to guard/brake. A hops, B mounts/docks, X scans and Y opens Menu. Turning-stick click cycles tools. Handedness, stick swapping and motion-strike alternatives are adjustable. Low pinch holds walking; release stops. An ordinary pinch interacts. A deliberate raised pinch opens Menu. Motion strikes, body holsters and cape gestures have synthetic tests, not physical comfort approval.

Lantern Ward includes eight resident stories plus Signal Hijack and its five sequential cases. Finish Signal Hijack and report to Mara to unlock Rooftop Run. Subsequent cases unlock sequentially: Quiet Circuit, Rooms of the Ward, Courtyard Surge and Last Light. Existing city contracts, Homecoming and Tidewater remain in the original city. The chapter, residents, Watch and campaign each keep their own exactly-once reward ledger; selecting a mission grants no completion or credits.

Rooftop Run now teaches a real hop/glide from the print terrace to a marked lower arcade landing. The return reuses the print-shop stairs. Shared floor geometry closes the arcade landing and stair-support seams. Enemies on the loft require supported destinations rather than pursuing through empty air. These are graybox fixes to useful routes, not a map-size expansion.

Failures retained during recovery include the duplicate portal GLSL wrapper, an unreachable enclosed glide destination, floor support cracks, off-axis grapple input, missing-backup assumptions, held-input Menu lockout and obsolete desktop HUD rectangles. A model pass alone never certifies their rendered fix. Source and public evidence are separate and recorded beside the release receipt.

The legacy recovery entry remains at legacy.html. It is not the unified main entry. Its 102 pinned runtime/layout files and original save key remain unchanged. The standalone lantern-ward.html is a district recovery entry with component version 0.15.0; the unified main shell is 0.16.0. Do not downgrade a save parser that understands city/Watch/campaign fields without preserving those fields.

Physical Quest, Xbox, hand tracking, room safety, motion comfort, sustained performance, finished art and human level-comprehension approval remain open. This release does not claim a commercial Arkham-scale campaign, cape fabric simulation, a fully authored interior for every city building, persistent spatial anchors or automatic room occlusion.
