SAN’s earlier self-modelling formulations

In 2022, Blumberg explicitly described a neural cell learning and predicting its own responses. The 2024 preprint and 2026 published version by Premakumar, Graziano and coauthors implement prediction of internal network activity. These cards show the shared roles, the dated wording, and the limits of each partial comparison.
