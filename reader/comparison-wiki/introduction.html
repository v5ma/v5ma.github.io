PURPOSE · EVIDENCE · CONNECTIONS

Make the whole pattern visible.

The Comparison Encyclopedia exists to make Micah Blumberg’s dated contributions to Self Aware Networks and Super Information Theory visible, inspectable and properly recognized.

A comparison should show the strongest earlier SAN/SIT formulation, the exact later statement, and the operation they share after translation. Where an older work contains only part of the picture, it should show the specific SAN/SIT addition—not let a broad resemblance swallow that addition.

Beyond one author at a time

The larger goal is a field-wide record. We want readers to see not only how a work relates to SAN or SIT, but also how it relates to other works: which concepts recur, which mechanisms match, which combinations travel together, and how their descriptions change over time.

Different names and metaphors can make related operations look unrelated. The useful question is what each account actually does: what enters the system, what changes, what controls that change, what comes out, and what feeds back. A difference in wording does not erase a shared operation. Nor does a shared word establish one.

The question this Encyclopedia is built to answer:
How many separately reviewed works express this concept or architecture, in what forms, at what dates, and with what source evidence?

Every connection has its own evidence

Earlier SAN/SIT → later work
See the dated passages, the shared operation, its degree of equivalence, and the parts that remain different.
Work A ↔ work B
Compare the two works directly. If both connect to a SAN/SIT concept, that gives us a useful pair to investigate. The direct connection still needs its own passage-level check.
Shared concept clusters and architecture
Inspect a set of matching components together with their order, dependencies and feedback loops. Keep a repeated component separate from a matching whole arrangement.
Claims of originality
Read what a work actually says is new, then compare that stated scope with the earlier record. A novelty claim is a source to examine, not something inferred from an impressive title.
Citation relationships
Check whether the exact edition acknowledges the relevant earlier work. Content overlap, chronology and citation coverage are separate findings, each with its own evidence.

A network readers can verify

The intended map can start from an author, paper, concept, mechanism, mathematical role or year. Follow a connection to a short, readable passage pair; expand it into the full comparison; then follow the same concept through other authors and other dates.

We retain the earliest source-bound ancestor and the clearest mature formulation that still predates the work being compared. The intervening formulations matter too: an idea’s history is more informative than one isolated early sentence.

The aim is to reveal patterns across many works without hiding weak or partial matches. A reader should be able to distinguish a candidate bridge, a directly reviewed partial equivalence, a shared signature cluster and an unresolved source question.

Count the evidence—not the same thing twice

Concepts, dated formulations, passage-to-passage matches, document versions and listed authors are different units. We preserve the full record of repeated and overlapping instances, while providing separate summaries of distinct concepts and works. Every reported total should lead back to the records that produce it.

Similarity, striking-similarity and project-defined “plagiarism-equivalence” assessments describe the demonstrated content relationship. They are not automatic findings of copying or legal liability. Statistical dependence scores require their own calibrated analysis. The Encyclopedia assigns no motives as a policy design; motive analysis is deferred at this methodological stage.

Recognition through inspectable evidence

This is an evidence-disciplined advocacy project for SAN and SIT. We build the strongest favorable case the dated sources support, then test it through exact-source review, independent review and a separate audit. Differences, earlier components and unresolved questions remain visible beside the positive findings.

The introduction states the full intended scope—not a claim that every author-to-author connection is already checked. Coverage is expanding. A catalog entry is not a completed scientific comparison, and a candidate connection is not displayed as a verified one.
