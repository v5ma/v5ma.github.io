# Rainward v0.8.1 - Controller-first expedition

The Xbox-standard layout keeps X exclusively assigned to reload during gameplay. LT aims, RT fires, Y interacts, A dodges or vaults, B crouches, right-stick click goes prone, LB listens and RB throws a bottle. D-pad up uses a medkit, down opens the satchel, left uses smoke and right switches shoulders. View opens the map and task journal. Menu pauses. Left-stick click toggles sprint by default; settings can restore hold-to-sprint. Reload feedback distinguishes an active reload, a full magazine and empty reserves without changing finite-ammunition rules.

Every in-game menu supports D-pad or left-stick navigation, A activation and B back/cancel. Left/right adjusts sliders and expedition selectors without opening operating-system menus. Right stick scrolls long panels. LB/RB cycle expeditions at the title or switch between journal, satchel and controls during an expedition. Checkboxes, crafting, quick-use items, task tracking and settings are reachable without the mouse. Rebuilt task rows retain focus. Confirmations for replacing a checkpoint, returning to a checkpoint and leaving an expedition are inside the game, default to Cancel and require released inputs before another action. Browser security prompts and external websites remain outside the game's control.

Controller sensitivity, radial stick deadzone, inverted vertical look, toggle sprint and optional vibration are saved locally. Unsupported vibration is ignored safely. Device replacement, disconnection and focus loss suppress held inputs and pause gameplay. Keyboard, mouse and touch remain available. The six chapters, puzzles, field tasks, enemy roles, character models and existing saves are retained.

The controller-menus native browser suite uses a validated shelter-save fixture to test the full UI journey through emulated standard gamepad input; the earlier controller suite retains ordinary enemy gameplay. Neither is a physical Xbox, Bluetooth or headset certification. Release publication is established by the merged commit and the hash-comparison workflow, not by this changelog alone.
