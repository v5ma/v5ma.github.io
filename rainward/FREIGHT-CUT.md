# Rainward v0.15.0 / Freight Cut

## Intended experience and source reconciliation

Arrival with an incomplete map; learn the clinic and its raised market outlook; carry the signal battery through an exposed crossing; recognize the receiver's service cable; choose whether to open the west loading passage; recover at the receiver; reuse crouching behind freight pallets; recover the spindle; break sight, spend finite supplies and retreat through the newly understood connection; return to the familiar clinic; choose a quieter approach to extraction.

Rainward's latest runtime was Wayfinder 0.14.1, merged in PR 162; its permanent receipt was 78dad9840163bbacb7308118cde44a1bb4f525f6. The clinic terrace, inside-released clinic gate, route chevrons and three XR views already existed. They are reused, not reimplemented. ## One hypothesis and bounded change

Hypothesis: making the existing optional freight receiver repair change a real route lets knowledge of the clinic/market/freight relationship support deliberate recovery, rather than merely collecting an ammunition reward.

The original depot-west-a wall keeps its ID but is split around a 3-metre loading bay. The existing ward-radio task, still local and dependent on the clinic battery, releases its shutter. A low pallet screen along the receiver-to-spindle aisle turns crouching into a slower sightline-management option, while leaving the original watcher patrol and both flanking ends open. A separate raised sorting screen shields the outside landing from the quay. The original south entrance and north exit remain open in both states. No chapter, required objective, shelter, task, reward, enemy or new control is added. The original north exit is the quicker route toward extraction; the lateral loading passage reconnects to market concealment and the clinic terrace for retreat or reapproach.

Opening is not pure advantage. It also opens a firing/sightline lane and allows the same NPC navigation to enter or leave through the bay. The sorting landing is not a safe zone. The aisle pallet screen does not make standing safe and does not remove the lookout's authored patrol turn. Existing crate cover, grass, smoke, bottles, medkits, stamina and search behavior remain the tools for making a retreat work. Skipping the receiver remains valid, including freight-first completion and extraction.

## Dependable physical state and readable causality

Body collision, pathfinding, line of sight and shot collision share the shutter's openOnTask state. Its graphic changes to a rolled shutter atomically with collision, avoiding an open collider under a closed-looking animation. The folded graphic fits inside the blocking lintel volume. A service cable links the existing receiver to the loading door. The physical signs and completion hint identify the opening and warn that lookouts can follow. The existing freight-manifest note retains its stable ID and explains the circuit. The map's existing obstacle rendering removes the disabled barrier normally.

The sorting screen has 0.6 metres of ground clearance. It blocks standing sightlines while allowing the existing prone body underneath. This also prevents a new obstacle from burying ground-level supplies saved in an older layout. A constructed legacy-drop unit fixture verifies collection with unchanged quantities and no duplicated reward after a second restore.

The aisle screen is ordinary shared collision, not stealth-only metadata. At its reviewed placement, standing remains visible from the freight lookout while a crouched body behind it is occluded. Its x=17.8 placement leaves watch-3's x=19,-19 authored patrol turn clear under the real navigation radius, and both ends of the screen remain flankable. The existing clinic/garden crouch relationship is therefore varied inside Freight Hall rather than introducing a new movement verb.

The graybox changes no arbitrary overlapping floors, body dimensions, IK targets or traversal verbs. Desktop/Xbox interaction remains Y or E. Quest interaction remains right grip or the established hand use action. XR render cutaways never change the gate's collision. First-person, VR Diorama and AR Diorama remain the same simulation with the existing no-double-closed shell invariant.

## Measurements and revealing tests

The primary controlled observation uses real acceleration and swept body motion between (17,-24) and (11,-24), standing without sprint in an explicitly enemy-free model fixture. The sealed detour measured approximately 19.9 metres / 6.7 seconds; the powered direct crossing measured 5.90 metres / 1.97 seconds. These are model measurements, not human times or live encounter win rates.

An early exposed-return model experiment failed. The reproducible paired preflight in tests/freight-route-preflight.mjs compares two continuations after the same receiver, spindle and clinic recovery sequence. The direct central return extracted in about 89.72 simulation seconds with 15 health; the longer western return extracted in about 102.18 seconds with 83 health. Both kept all five enemies alive, fired no shots and used only earned finite supplies. The longer route therefore traded speed for lower damage in these particular model runs. It is not a measured universal strategy or a human preference result.

Native testing then exposed three useful problems, all retained under evidence/freight-cut-v0.15.0/. First, the normal-start route reached the powered receiver but died before the spindle, showing that technical connectivity alone did not provide a recovery relationship. Second, the first low-cover placement overlapped watch-3's existing patrol turn; the patrol-clearance regression rejected it, so the screen moved instead of the enemy. Third, a later run earned a second medkit but the test had hidden its Classic heal request inside movement polling, so no heal event occurred before re-entry. The final acceptance removes automatic healing from route guidance. Recovery must be an explicit mapped D-pad-up action that consumes one earned medkit and records the real heal event before the crouch-cover aisle.

The native journey must independently earn its supplies, traverse both buildings, repair the receiver, use the new passage, recover at the clinic, extract and reload an earned shelter checkpoint without assigning player state. It uses ordinary virtual Xbox Classic buttons/sticks. Continuous route guidance writes only movement/sprint input; healing, stance, smoke, crafting and interactions are explicit mapped player actions. Any native failures remain evidence rather than grounds for health grants or weakened enemies.

Dedicated unit fixtures test the gate's three body stances, shots, sightline, actual investigating NPC traversal, original entrance reachability, one-time rewards, legacy drop access, patrol clearance and optional unpowered extraction. Unit fixtures and the reproducible model preflight are not end-to-end browser playthroughs. Additional district AR/VR diorama checks exercise the real scene with explicitly simulated poses, buttons and joints. They do not certify physical passthrough, hand reliability or a complete new-route headset playthrough.

## Saves, rollback and next question

The passage derives only from the existing ward-radio completed task. Checkpoint version 4 and older accepted versions, keys, item IDs, task IDs, rewards and shelter anchors are unchanged. An older save with that repair already recorded opens the passage on restoration, without replaying rewards. A save without it leaves the door sealed. Previewing a save does not change the active map. There is no localStorage clearing or migration.

Rollback is a normal revert of this release, not resetting master: old checkpoints contain no new task or field, so the preceding runtime can still load them. Keep other games and the shared library's unrelated sections intact. The library's Rainward note and existing canonical RW-009, RW-011, RW-013 and RW-028 rows record the iteration without marking the whole chapter or human gates approved. Detailed preflight observations and evidence boundaries are in evidence/freight-cut-v0.15.0/design-observations.json.

Before calling this a locked flagship slice, observe unfamiliar players choosing between the original north exit and the recovery passage, and whether they can explain why opening the shutter changes threat access. Remaining opportunities include the quay's final observation/commitment beat, better hand-menu access under pressure, and body/contact animation. Physical Xbox/Quest, comfort, performance, final art/audio, fresh-player comprehension and replay mastery remain unverified. Publication is established by the release PR and separate served-byte receipt, not this candidate document.
