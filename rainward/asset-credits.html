What was changed

Stone, brick, mossy paving and forest floor have 2048-pixel color textures, 1024-pixel normal textures and 512-pixel packed occlusion/roughness/metalness textures. Model UV coordinates, material bindings and fern transparency were preserved. The six moss rocks were simplified from 63,127 to 22,081 triangles; the fern set keeps 6,232 triangles. The larger edge boulder retains 56,534 triangles and uses lower-resolution 512-pixel textures because it remains outside the playable boundary.

The shipped binary pack is 13,629,084 bytes (about 13.0 MiB). WebP reduces download size, not the decoded GPU memory required to display a texture. Full quality uses the real normal/material maps and HDR environment. Reduced Graphics retains photographed color and source UVs but skips expensive normal and environment shading. The original procedural materials remain available as a reversible comparison and as a fallback for a missing asset.

Tools and reproducibility

glTF Transform prepared the self-contained GLB files, with meshoptimizer for bounded-error simplification. Pillow and Sharp resampled/compressed the textures. Three.js r177 GLTFLoader and RGBELoader match the pinned renderer; their MIT license is retained in vendor/LICENSE. Source file URLs and hashes are preserved in source-lock.json and shipped hashes in manifest.json. The import scripts live in tools/ and are not executed by the game.

License and remaining limits

Poly Haven publishes these assets under CC0, including reuse and redistribution in games. Credit is provided to support the artists and make provenance clear. The source website's text, logos and showcase images are not covered by its asset license and are not included here.

This is an environment-asset upgrade, not a claim of finished AAA visuals. The player character, combat animations and some architectural assemblies are still procedural. The three chapters, puzzles, enemy drops and monster encounters remain playable; swimming, ledge climbing, multiplayer and immersive VR are not added by this release.
