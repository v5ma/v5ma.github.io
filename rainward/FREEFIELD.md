# Rainward v0.16.0 / Freefield

This is a candidate until a merge and separate public-byte receipt identify the delivered build. The preceding verified release is Freight Cut v0.15.0. A version number, shader fixture or simulated headset test is not public deployment or physical Quest approval.

## Experience and level geography

The owner's September 17 brief replaces fatigue-limited default running, repetitive player Foley, permanent XR menus and a cropped miniature with continuous travel, quiet sound, on-demand controls and a centered window into the ordinary game. Meridian is the first redesigned chapter in this pass; the other six chapter redesigns are unfinished.

Meridian now connects a sunken drainage spine, a 2.4-metre western clinic terrace and a 6-metre eastern reading-hall ridge through continuous terrain. Its courtyard clinic, narrow market arcade, sawtooth works, domed reading hall, garden-roof kitchen and watch tower have different footprints and silhouettes. The pressure beacon orients the player toward the northern exit. Original filters, key, water diagram, three valves, required tasks and gate still determine completion.

The drain trades long views for low transfer space; the high neighborhoods offer observation and commitment points. Returning to the clinic or library should be recognizable, not another identical block. A raised privacy partition shields the original clinic valve from the straight east-door firing lane exposed by native testing, while both ends admit pursuit. It creates a preparation opportunity, not invulnerability. Old supply drops retain IDs and quantities and move only to adjacent clear ground when replaced walls would bury them.

The shared Level Design Library remains authoritative for experience-first, meaningful-route, graybox-first work. This increment does not claim unfamiliar-player comprehension, complete campaign redesign, or final visual approval.

## Movement and audio

Free Stride defaults to 9 game metres per second, with a 6-14 m/s control. Running does not drain stamina or slow from fatigue in this mode. Disabling it restores the legacy stamina-limited policy. Acceleration, turns, collision, aiming, crouching and injuries remain distinct from straight uninterrupted running. A model test exercises more than 3600 simulation seconds away from course reversals; it is not a physical hour-long session.

Optional blink previews a clear ground route while its assigned Quest control is held and commits on release. Keyboard T also requests blink. Walls, locked gates, deep water and unsafe height changes stop it. No inventory or objective is granted. It is fast collision-checked ground movement, not arbitrary teleportation through mechanisms.

Player footsteps default off, water movement to 15 percent, and the score to the sparse original After the Rain motif. Legacy music and music-off remain selectable. Separate controls restore player footstep and water sound levels. Enemy hearing still receives gameplay noise; quiet playback is not a stealth cheat. Real speaker/headphone listening approval remains separate.

## Four views and on-demand controls

Choose FIRST PERSON / VR, FIRST PERSON / AR CUTAWAY, THIRD PERSON / VR DIORAMA, or THIRD PERSON / AR DIORAMA. AR requires a transparent immersive-ar compositor. Controller and hand entry requests are deliberate and permissions remain controlled by the browser/headset.

First-person AR removes environment drawing above waist height while leaving characters, pickups, weapons and hands whole. Collision and enemy sight remain authoritative. No furniture recognition, camera recording, persistent room anchor or real-room depth occlusion is claimed.

The diorama is the active game rendered through a stereo aperture, not a small six-plane-cropped map or a flat theatre. The survivor stays centered in XYZ, distant scenery remains beyond rear and side faces, and physical viewing movement never steers the character. Scale affects the game rather than headset eye separation. Existing physical box size and recentering remain. Top/front options are both open, top open, or front open, never both closed. Eye-facing enclosure glass becomes transparent. An environment-only sightline cutaway reveals the survivor through intervening foreground architecture without erasing farther scenery or gameplay walls. Pointer picking follows the visible environment cut, while authoritative shots still start at the character and collide with real walls.

The full panel is hidden during ordinary play unless the legacy pinned option is selected. A small wrist display appears when viewed. Right-stick click or an open left palm summons menus. Hands use left pinch plus wrist displacement for movement and right pinch for selected USE, FIRE or BLINK mode. On-demand actions cover equipment, reload, turning, traversal, posture, listening, map, crafting and running. Source or mode changes require released inputs before rearming.

Quest direct defaults are A/right grip interact, B reload, RT fire/use, LT aim, X crouch/hold-prone, Y jump/traverse. In water X dives/surfaces and held Y boosts swimming. Left stick moves and its click follows the saved sprint toggle preference. Left grip previews/releases blink. Right stick snaps, changes gun/tool vertically, and clicks for pause. Legacy Quest Fieldwork remains selectable.

Quest and standard Xbox gameplay remaps persist separately from checkpoints. Menu A/B, system buttons, Quest right-stick pause and open-palm recovery remain reserved. A reset action restores defaults. This is not approval of every arbitrary remap combination or completion of all accessibility hold alternatives.

## Bodies, weapons and contacts

Existing licensed Quaternius authored body/outfit surfaces replace the bulky procedural garment overlay, retaining Rainward face/skin treatment and backpack. This reuses bundled source; it does not import Aether Reach's entire animation system. Bounded two-bone visual foot placement and brief planting follow terrain without moving collision bodies. Water, prone, vault and dodge bypass it. Full authored clips, weapon hand contacts and artist review remain unfinished.

Two stylized Kenney CC0 blasters are locally embedded as pistol and rifle GLBs with their original palette. Source hashes, license and transformations are in assets/freefield-weapons/manifest.json and LICENSE.txt. Fallback visible geometry remains if loading fails. These are stylized weapons, not photorealistic replicas. They do not grant ammunition or replace firearm trace rules.

The optional magnified sight renders a bounded 256-pixel image while aiming without changing headset field of view. Device cost, comfort and two-handed handling require physical review.
