# Rainward v0.9.0 - The sound of surviving

Six original, generated chapter arrangements replace the former short event tones. Rain on Wire, A Memory Under Glass, Last Train No Passenger, Windows Still Remember, Light Beyond the Seawall, and Footprints After Midnight have distinct motifs, harmonic sequences, tempi and environmental beds. Felt-key, plucked, glass-like, bowed and bass layers use deterministic synthesized samples. Nearby danger fades in rhythmic tension; listening reduces music and environment masking while retaining nearby threat detail. No commercial soundtrack or third-party recordings are included.

The soundscape includes layered shots, reload stages, casings, impact tails, bottle shattering, clothing and movement, six surface-footstep families, creature calls, machinery, smoke, bandaging, crafting, supplies and completion cues. Spatial effects follow camera yaw and source distance. Actual collision geometry muffles obstructed sources. Independent master, music, effects and ambience sliders, mono output, a compressed night mix, captions and a left/right sound check are controller navigable. Browser autoplay permissions can require a trusted first keypress or pointer gesture. The game does not bypass those permissions.

The new Survival controller preset uses tap B to crouch and hold B to go prone, A to jump or vault supported low cover, X to strike, LB to dodge, RB to listen, Y to interact, LT to aim and RT to fire or throw. LT plus X reloads. Right-stick click changes shoulders; left-stick click sprints. D-pad left selects the rifle, right selects the sidearm, and up cycles medical kits, bottles and smoke. Down opens the satchel, and holding a D-pad direction opens quick crafting. Release the direction after opening the panel, choose a recipe, and hold A to assemble it. The Classic v0.8.1 preset remains selectable.

Bandaging requires selecting the medkit and holding RT for the full action. Releasing the trigger or taking damage interrupts it. Crafting likewise requires a sustained A press in Survival mode. Patrols and simulation time continue during both actions. Cancellation refunds reserved resources once and never grants partial healing or completed items. Character poses and progress displays communicate these vulnerable actions. The circular health display and other HUD elements fade during quiet moments; the option is reversible. Movement and repeated firing affect the aiming reticle and actual Survival-preset shot spread.

One-time survivor caches near each chapter start contain rifle cartridges and a wear-limited melee tool. Pistol and rifle magazines remain separate and finite. The rifle has a slower action and a stronger impact. Melee is directional, range- and sightline-tested, stamina-limited and can wear out a scavenged club or blade. A timed dodge opens a short counterattack opportunity. Nearby noisy enemies have a listening silhouette and ripple layer; prone movement improves concealment but is not unconditional invisibility.

All six existing expeditions, required tasks, optional work, puzzles, source asset credits and save keys are preserved. Checkpoint version 4 records equipment and separate magazines and migrates earlier versions without granting extra ammunition. Human-shield grabs, a separate deployable trap system and arbitrary window/gap traversal are not implemented by this release; existing silent takedowns and collision-checked low-cover traversal remain.

Acceptance includes the existing twelve native-browser suites, separate Survival-controller and real Web Audio output tests, and the model regressions. The compatibility harness explicitly runs the previous Classic controller preset while the new controller journey exercises Survival mode. Simulated input and rendered sample measurements do not certify a physical Xbox controller, Bluetooth connection, headset, speaker system or subjective musical quality. The published-byte comparison is the release verification, rather than this document itself.
