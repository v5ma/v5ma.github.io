A living district.
Not a finished world.

This public prototype focuses on the game engine and original survival-stealth mechanics. The source of truth is the scoped repository and its tests. Model checks, native browser checks, physical-device testing and player approval are separate gates.

IMPLEMENTED / VERIFY IN RELEASE

Two expeditions + v0.3

  • A larger conservatory chapter, three-wheel sluice puzzle, distinct monsters and checkpointed supply drops.
  • Shoulder switching, collision-safe camera smoothing, noise/cover feedback and finite evidence-based enemy searches.
  • Third-person movement, aiming, crouch, prone, dodge and low-cover vault.
  • Patrol vision, sound investigation, search and concealment.
  • Scavenging, shared-material crafting, smoke and finite ammunition.
  • Two real interiors, a shelter checkpoint and complete extraction objective.
NEXT / PLANNED

Better moment-to-moment play

  • Physical Xbox testing and configurable bindings.
  • Animation transitions, camera collision tuning and richer environmental detail.
  • Further tune patrol search routes, difficulty and encounter variety using player feedback.
  • Multiple approach routes and encounter difficulty options.
LATER / NOT IMPLEMENTED

Expand carefully

  • Climbable ledges, swimming, rope traversal and more authored chapters remain unimplemented.
  • Further refine the original monster models, fair attack cues and stealth routes.
  • Environmental puzzles, additional tools and meaningful resource choices.
  • Original narrative after a separate approval process.
  • Level authoring and accessibility expansion; XR and multiplayer need independent design.

Release rules

Complete the chapter in the actual browser without assigning gameplay state. Check sightlines and resource use separately from graphics. Preserve save isolation and existing applications. No physical device or player-enjoyment certification is implied by an automated test. No private narrative is included.
