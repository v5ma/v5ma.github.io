IMPLEMENTED / v0.4 RELEASE CANDIDATE

Three expeditions

  • The Floodgate: urban stealth and scavenging.
  • The Drowned Conservatory: clue inscription, symbol wheels, creatures, terraces and persistent loot.
  • Bellweather Terminus: covered platforms, two service wings and an interlinked electrical puzzle.
  • Numbered controls, clue markers and optional three-stage hints. Hints never operate the puzzle.
  • Metre-scaled surface color, height and roughness; revised lighting, rocks and figure proportions.
NEXT / PLANNED

Asset and animation quality

  • Replace primitive character assemblies with an original rigged mesh and authored animation clips.
  • Build a consistent architecture/vegetation kit with UVs, trim details, believable wear and efficient lower-detail variants.
  • Introduce a validated glTF asset path, documented licenses, compressed textures and explicit memory/frame budgets.
  • Use repeated camera-matched comparisons and real-device playtests, not a render-feature checklist, to judge improvement.
LATER / NOT IMPLEMENTED

Deeper play

  • Ledge traversal, climbing and swimming need collision, camera and encounter design together.
  • More multi-step environmental puzzles, tools and alternative routes; distinct level layouts rather than repeated rooms.
  • Independent chapter save slots, control remapping and physical Xbox/phone acceptance.
  • Expanded original campaign, authoring, XR and multiplayer remain separate projects of work.

What the graphics pass does not mean

The textures are locally authored procedural materials, not scanned surfaces. The figures are still procedural, not motion-captured production characters. Lighting uses the current browser renderer, not an implementation of another engine's global illumination system. A Blender file is neither required to render richer assets nor sufficient to make a game look finished.

Release and save rules

Complete every chapter through actual browser controls. Validate puzzles, loot and saves separately from visual quality. Preserve sibling applications and unpublished material. Keep failed test evidence. Confirm public file hashes after merge before claiming deployment. The current checkpoint is one slot: starting a new expedition replaces it after confirmation; existing checkpoints remain readable. Physical-device performance and enjoyment are not certified by automated tests.
